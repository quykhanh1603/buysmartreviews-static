A Look at New Balance

New Balance is a prominent American brand recognized for its athletic shoes, selling products through both its own website and various retail outlets. However, while the brand is well-known, a closer inspection of customer reviews reveals notable issues worth discussing.

Understanding Customer Feedback

  • Trustpilot: Over 1,450 reviews, with a noticeable tilt towards the negative side.

Common Issues Faced by Customers

Negative Feedback: Many customers have reported quality concerns, including cases where premium running shoes have torn after just a few uses. Additionally, there are frequent mentions of delivery delays and customer service interactions that customers found challenging. In some instances, buyers were offered discounts instead of refunds on defective items, which left them frustrated.

Tips for Potential Buyers

  • Inspect your shoes promptly upon arrival and take clear photos of any defects for documentation.
  • If a discount is offered instead of a refund for a defective item, be firm about wanting the refund and request the policy in writing if necessary.

Final Thoughts

While New Balance is a legitimate and recognized brand, the feedback regarding its direct-to-consumer service suggests that potential customers should be cautious. Document any problems you encounter and clearly state what resolution you prefer for a smoother experience.
