New Balance is a well-known American athletic footwear brand, selling direct via its own site alongside retail partners. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

New Balance is a legitimate, established brand, but its direct-to-consumer review record shows significant customer service and quality complaints worth being aware of.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 1,450 reviews, skewing negative.

Recurring themes

Negative: quality complaints (including a report of premium running shoes tearing after minimal use), missed delivery estimates, and customer service described by multiple reviewers as difficult to work with. Some reviewers report being offered a discount rather than a refund for defective items.

Before you order

  • Inspect shoes carefully soon after receiving them and document any defect immediately with photos.
  • If offered a discount instead of a refund for a defective item and that’s not acceptable to you, ask explicitly for the refund policy in writing.

Bottom line

New Balance is a legitimate, well-known brand, but its direct-order customer service has a consistently weak review record. Document any issues immediately and be clear about what resolution you want.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
