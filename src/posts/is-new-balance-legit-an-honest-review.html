1,450 Customer Reviews with a Negative Spin

New Balance has made a name for itself as a prominent American athletic shoe brand, but a closer look at customer feedback reveals significant issues that potential buyers should be aware of.

Quality Control and Customer Service Concerns

  • Trustpilot: Over 1,450 reviews, with a noticeable tilt towards the negative side.

Negative Feedback: Customers have reported various quality issues, including premium running shoes tearing after just a few uses. Additionally, many users have experienced delays in delivery and unhelpful customer service. Some even faced the frustrating situation of being offered discounts instead of refunds for defective items.

  • Inspect your shoes promptly upon arrival and take clear photos of any defects for documentation.
  • If a discount is offered instead of a refund for a defective item, be firm about wanting the refund and request the policy in writing if necessary.

While New Balance is a legitimate and recognized brand, the overall feedback suggests exercising caution when dealing with their direct-to-consumer service.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
