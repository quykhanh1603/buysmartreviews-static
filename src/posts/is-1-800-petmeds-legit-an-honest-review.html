Overview of 1-800-PetMeds

1-800-PetMeds has been a staple in the online pet medication industry for many years, providing a range of pharmaceuticals and supplies for your furry friends. However, it's important to examine customer feedback to gauge its reliability.

Customer Feedback Insights

  • BBB: Holds an A+ rating and is accredited.
  • Trustpilot: Customers rate it around 2.6 out of 5, based on a moderate number of reviews.
  • SmartCustomer: Scores about 1.9 out of 5 from 179 reviews.

What Customers Are Saying

Positive Feedback: Many users appreciate the order tracking and communication regarding shipping.

Negative Feedback: The most frequent issues involve shipping delays and challenges in reaching customer support, where responses can vary significantly.

Important Considerations

  • If your pet relies on ongoing medications, consider placing orders well in advance due to the inconsistent shipping reliability noted by users.
  • It’s wise to keep all prescription documents and order confirmations handy in case you encounter any problems.

Final Thoughts

While 1-800-PetMeds is a legitimate and accredited entity, the varying scores from independent reviews signal that service can be hit or miss. It's best to prepare for potential delays, especially with essential medications.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
