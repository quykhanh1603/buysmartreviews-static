1-800-PetMeds’ Surprising Ratings

1-800-PetMeds has an A+ rating from the Better Business Bureau but scores dismally low on customer review platforms, including a 2.6 out of 5 on Trustpilot and a mere 1.9 from SmartCustomer.

Customer Experiences

  • Positive Feedback: Users often commend the order tracking system and effective shipping communication.
  • Negative Feedback: Common complaints revolve around shipping delays and difficulty reaching customer support, with response times varying significantly.

For those relying on regular pet medications, it’s advisable to order well in advance due to the noted inconsistency in delivery. Keeping all prescription documents and order confirmations accessible is also a smart precaution.

Service can be hit or miss, so prepare for potential delays.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
