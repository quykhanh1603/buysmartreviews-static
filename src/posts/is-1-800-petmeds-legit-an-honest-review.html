1-800-PetMeds is a long-running online pharmacy for pet medications and supplies. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

1-800-PetMeds is a legitimate, BBB-accredited business, but review scores across independent platforms are notably mediocre — worth planning around, especially for time-sensitive medication needs.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: A+ rating, accredited.
  • Trustpilot: around 2.6 out of 5 from a modest sample.
  • SmartCustomer: around 1.9 out of 5 from 179 reviews.

Recurring themes

Positive: some customers describe good tracking and order communication throughout the shipping process.

Negative: shipment issues and difficulty reaching support with conflicting explanations are the most cited complaints.

Before you order

  • For ongoing medications, order well before your pet’s current supply runs out, given the mixed shipping-reliability feedback.
  • Keep prescription documentation and order confirmations organized in case of a dispute.

Bottom line

1-800-PetMeds is a legitimate, accredited business, but independent review scores suggest inconsistent service. Build in a buffer for medication timing.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
