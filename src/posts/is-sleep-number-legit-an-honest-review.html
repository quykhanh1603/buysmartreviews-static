Overview of Sleep Number

Sleep Number is a mattress retailer specializing in adjustable air-chamber beds, offering products both in their physical locations and online. They have built a notable presence in the market thanks to their innovative approach to sleep solutions.

Customer Feedback Insights

  • Trustpilot: close to 2,400 reviews showcase a mix of experiences from customers.
  • BBB: not accredited, with several complaints logged about product quality and delivery issues.

What Buyers Are Saying

Positive Aspects: Many customers rave about the helpfulness and expertise of in-store staff, and a significant number have reported that the adjustable firmness has led to improved sleep quality.

Negative Aspects: A recurring issue relates to air loss in the adjustable chambers, alongside concerns regarding warranty support and pricing transparency.

Considerations Before Purchasing

  • Clarify the warranty specifics regarding air loss and how such issues will be addressed before committing to a purchase.
  • Whenever possible, test the mattress in-store to experience the adjustable firmness firsthand, as this can be a personal preference.

Final Thoughts

Sleep Number has established itself as a legitimate player in the mattress industry, attracting a loyal following with its adjustable bed offerings. However, potential buyers should be proactive in addressing the common durability complaints, particularly regarding warranty coverage.
