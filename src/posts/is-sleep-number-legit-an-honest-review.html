Overview of Sleep Number Products

  • Comfort Next™: Aimed at providing advanced pressure relief, starting at $2,549, with discounts reaching up to $900 off during sales.
  • Climate™: This innovative model adjusts temperature by up to 15 degrees cooler, perfect for hot sleepers, priced from $4,399.

Many users praise Sleep Number mattresses for their comfort and support, often highlighting the adjustable firmness as a standout feature.
