Sleep Number is a mattress retailer known for adjustable air-chamber beds, sold through both physical stores and online. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Sleep Number is a legitimate, established company with a large customer base and genuinely mixed reviews — strongly positive for some buyers, with recurring complaints around durability for others.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: nearly 2,400 reviews, mixed.
  • BBB: not accredited, with complaints on file about product quality and delivery.

Recurring themes

Positive: in-store staff are frequently described as knowledgeable and helpful, and many customers report genuinely improved sleep quality from the adjustable-firmness feature.

Negative: the most common complaint is air loss in the adjustable chambers over time, along with some warranty and pricing concerns.

Before you order

  • Ask specifically about the air-loss warranty terms and how air-chamber issues are handled before buying.
  • Test the bed in-store if possible before ordering, given how personal the adjustable-firmness feature is.

Bottom line

Sleep Number is a legitimate company with a loyal customer base for its adjustable-bed concept, but air-loss durability complaints are common enough to ask about warranty coverage upfront.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
