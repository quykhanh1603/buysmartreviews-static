Is Enterprise Car Sales a Reliable Choice?

If you're considering purchasing a used vehicle from Enterprise Car Sales, you should know that this branch of the well-known Enterprise rental car company focuses on selling previously rented and other used cars. While it benefits from the reputation of its parent company, customer feedback reveals a mixed bag of experiences.

What Are Customers Saying?

  • Trustpilot: Reviews here are limited, with less than 100 available, and the feedback is quite diverse.
  • PissedConsumer: On this platform, the average rating sits around 2 out of 5 from over 100 reviews. Be aware that this site usually highlights complaints rather than positive experiences.

What Issues Should You Be Aware Of?

Negative Feedback: Many buyers have reported discovering defects in their vehicles after purchasing them. Additionally, there are frequent complaints about slow responses regarding warranty claims, particularly for cars bought online.

Positive Aspects: On the upside, some customers appreciate the standard return policy, which allows for returns within a specific timeframe that can vary by location.

  • It's wise to get your local return/buyback policy in writing before you go ahead with the purchase.
  • If you can, try to inspect the vehicle in person to avoid complications that might arise when buying and shipping remotely.

While Enterprise Car Sales operates as a legitimate used-car retailer backed by a respected brand, it’s important to carefully consider both the pros and cons shared in customer reviews, especially if you’re looking at remote transactions.

The ratings mentioned reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
