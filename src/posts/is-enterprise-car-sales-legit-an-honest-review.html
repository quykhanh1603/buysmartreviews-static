Overview of Enterprise Car Sales

As the used-vehicle branch of the prominent Enterprise rental car company, Enterprise Car Sales specializes in selling previously rented and other used vehicles. While it operates under the umbrella of a trusted brand, the feedback from customers paints a varied picture.

Customer Feedback Insights

  • Trustpilot: Less than 100 reviews are available, and the sentiment is mixed.
  • PissedConsumer: The average rating is around 2 out of 5 from over 100 reviews. The majority are unfavorable, but keep in mind this site tends to attract complaints rather than praise.

Common Complaints and Praise

Negative Aspects: Buyers frequently report issues such as vehicle defects discovered post-purchase, along with delays in warranty claim responses, especially for cars bought remotely.

Positive Feedback: Some purchasers appreciate the standard return policy, which provides a short window for buybacks, varying by location.

Advice for Potential Buyers

  • Ensure to confirm your location's return/buyback policy in writing before finalizing a purchase.
  • If possible, inspect the vehicle in person to avoid issues that can arise from buying and shipping remotely.

Conclusion

While Enterprise Car Sales is an authentic used-car retailer backed by a reputable parent company, potential buyers should weigh the return policy benefits against the risks highlighted by customer reviews, particularly regarding remote transactions.

The ratings mentioned reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
