Enterprise Car Sales is the used-vehicle retail arm of the Enterprise rental car company, selling former rental and other used vehicles. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Enterprise Car Sales is a legitimate division of a major, established company, though the review record shows more customer dissatisfaction than the parent brand’s reputation might suggest.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: under 100 reviews on record, mixed.
  • PissedConsumer: around 2 out of 5 from over 100 reviews, with a majority unfavorable — though this platform specifically attracts dissatisfied customers by design, so treat it as a complaint log rather than a representative average.

Recurring themes

Negative: the most common complaints involve vehicle defects discovered after purchase and slow follow-up on warranty claims, particularly for vehicles that were shipped rather than bought in person.

Positive: Enterprise’s standard return policy (typically a short buyback window, terms vary by location) is cited by some buyers as a useful safety net.

Before you buy

  • Confirm your specific location’s return/buyback policy in writing before purchase.
  • Inspect the vehicle in person if at all possible rather than relying on remote purchase and shipping.

Bottom line

Enterprise Car Sales is a legitimate used-car retailer backed by a large, established parent company. The return policy offers some protection, but in-person inspection is worth prioritizing given the complaint pattern around shipped vehicles.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
