Sephora is one of the largest beauty retailers in the world, carrying both its own products and a wide range of third-party brands. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Sephora is a massive, legitimate retailer, but its independent review record is consistently weak, concentrated in shipping and customer service responsiveness.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 2.3 out of 5 from over 3,600 reviews.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Negative: orders arriving over three weeks past the estimated delivery date, lost packages, wrong items shipped, and customer service described as unresponsive (automated replies, broken links) are the most cited issues. Payment/refund processing problems also come up repeatedly.

Before you order

  • Order well ahead of any deadline (a gift, an event) given the reported shipping delays.
  • Keep your order confirmation and any payment records in case of a billing dispute.

Bottom line

Sephora is a legitimate, massive retailer, but shipping and customer service issues are common enough in reviews to plan around, especially for time-sensitive orders.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
