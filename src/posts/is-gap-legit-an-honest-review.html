Gap is a well-known American clothing retailer and the parent brand of Old Navy and Banana Republic. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Gap is a legitimate, long-established retailer. Review volume specific to Gap.com is more modest than some peers, with complaints concentrated in a few specific areas.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 600 reviews.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Negative: missing items from multi-item orders and refund-processing difficulty are the most specifically cited complaints.

Before you order

  • Check your order confirmation against what actually ships, and report missing items promptly rather than waiting.
  • Cross-reference reviews across more than one platform (Trustpilot, Google Reviews, BBB) before a large order, since single-platform scores can be misleading.

Bottom line

Gap is a legitimate, established retailer. The clearest complaint pattern is around partial/missing shipments, so verify your order contents on arrival.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
