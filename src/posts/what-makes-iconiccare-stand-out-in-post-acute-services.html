Dedicated Post-Acute Ancillary Services

IconicCare specializes in delivering post-acute ancillary services tailored for skilled nursing facilities, inpatient rehabilitation facilities, and assisted living centers. Their goal is to bridge the gaps in healthcare delivery by offering comprehensive post-acute care solutions that prioritize patient outcomes and operational efficiencies.

Comprehensive Service Offerings

The services provided by IconicCare include:

  • Physiatry (PM&R)
  • Telehealth Services
  • Post-Discharge Follow-Up
  • Principal Care Management
  • Chronic Care Management

These services are designed to enhance the recovery experience for patients, streamline communication among healthcare teams, and ensure effective follow-up care, addressing many of the challenges typical in post-acute care.

Impact on Patient Care

IconicCare's approach is grounded in the belief that connected care leads to better health outcomes. Their focus on personalized, attentive clinical teams aims to elevate the patient experience significantly. One satisfied administrator from Ensign Group reported a marked 25% decrease in opioid use among patients as a result of IconicCare's interventions, alongside increased patient satisfaction and therapy engagement.

Strong Leadership and Mission

Founded in 2022 by Dr. Christian Morris, a board-certified physiatrist, IconicCare was established to overcome the prevalent challenges in post-acute care, including fragmented care plans and lack of effective communication. The leadership team, including CEO Dallin Bills, brings a wealth of experience in healthcare and business, driving IconicCare's mission forward with a commitment to integrity and continuous improvement.

Why Choose IconicCare?

IconicCare stands out due to its relentless focus on enhancing patient care and supporting the staff at partner facilities. Their innovative solutions aim at transforming the landscape of post-acute recovery, ensuring that both patients and healthcare providers benefit from their services. Through their dedicated efforts, IconicCare is positioned as a leader in post-acute care.
