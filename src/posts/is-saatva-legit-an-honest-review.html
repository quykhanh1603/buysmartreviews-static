Saatva is a direct-to-consumer luxury mattress brand known for white-glove delivery and a long trial period. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Saatva stands out with one of the strongest review records of any mattress brand we’ve checked — a legitimate, well-regarded company by a wide margin.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4.8 out of 5 from over 42,000 reviews — an unusually high score for this large a sample size.
  • BBB: a notably low complaint rate for its sales volume.
  • Industry estimates put its return rate around 3%, low for a premium mattress brand.

Recurring themes

Positive: customer service and the 365-night trial policy are consistently praised, along with white-glove delivery service (setup and old-mattress removal included).

Negative: a smaller share of reviews mention product durability concerns over the long term or delivery scheduling delays.

Before you order

  • Take advantage of the full trial period rather than deciding in the first few nights.
  • Confirm delivery scheduling windows for your area, since white-glove delivery can take longer to coordinate than standard boxed shipping.

Bottom line

Saatva has one of the strongest, most consistent review records in the mattress category. A solid choice if the premium price point fits your budget.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
