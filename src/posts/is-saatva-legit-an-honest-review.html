Overview of Saatva

Saatva is a direct-to-consumer luxury mattress brand recognized for its exceptional white-glove delivery service and generous trial period. Let's delve into what public reviews reveal about this company.

Customer Satisfaction Ratings

  • Trustpilot: Approximately 4.8 out of 5 from over 42,000 reviews — an impressive rating for such a large number of customers.
  • BBB: Shows a significantly low complaint rate relative to its sales volume.
  • Industry estimates suggest a return rate of around 3%, which is quite low for a premium brand.

Common Feedback

Positive Aspects: Customers frequently commend Saatva's exemplary customer service and the benefits of their 365-night trial policy. The white-glove delivery service, which includes setup and removal of your old mattress, is also a highlighted feature.

Negative Points: A few reviews raise concerns about product durability over the long term and occasional delays in delivery scheduling.

Things to Consider

  • Utilize the entire trial period to fully experience the mattress before making a decision.
  • Be sure to check delivery scheduling options in your area, as coordinating white-glove delivery can take longer than standard shipping methods.

Final Thoughts

Saatva boasts one of the most commendable review histories in the mattress industry. If their premium pricing aligns with your budget, it could be a great fit for your needs.

The ratings mentioned above are based on publicly available data at the time of writing and could have changed since publication.
