What’s the Deal with Saatva?

Saatva is making waves in the luxury mattress space, particularly known for its white-glove delivery service and a generous 365-night trial. It’s worth checking out what other customers are saying about their experiences.

Are Customers Happy with Their Saatva Mattresses?

  • Trustpilot: Saatva boasts an impressive rating of about 4.8 out of 5 from over 42,000 reviews.
  • BBB: The brand has a low complaint rate when you compare it to its sales volume.
  • Industry estimates also suggest that only around 3% of purchases are returned, which is quite low for a premium mattress.

What Do People Say About Saatva?

Positive Feedback: Many customers rave about Saatva's customer service and the convenience of their 365-night trial. The white-glove delivery, which includes setup and even taking away your old mattress, is another big plus.

Negative Feedback: Some users point out potential concerns regarding the durability of the mattress over time, and there are occasional complaints about delivery delays.

Before making your final decision, consider utilizing the entire trial period to really get a feel for the mattress. Also, check the delivery options in your area since coordinating white-glove service might take longer than standard delivery.

Overall, Saatva has a solid reputation in the mattress industry. If their pricing fits your budget, it may be well worth considering.

The ratings mentioned above are based on publicly available data at the time of writing and could have changed since publication.
