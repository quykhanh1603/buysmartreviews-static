Nectar Sleep is a direct-to-consumer mattress brand sold online, known for its long trial period and memory-foam construction. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Nectar appears to be a legitimate, established mattress brand with a generous trial policy that meaningfully reduces the risk of buying a mattress sight-unseen. Comfort preference is subjective, so expect some review variance regardless of brand.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: rated around 4.1 out of 5.
  • 365-night trial period and a lifetime/forever warranty are standard with purchase.

Recurring themes

Positive: reviewers frequently mention a smooth delivery experience and general comfort satisfaction, particularly customers who were replacing an old, worn-out mattress.

Negative: the most common complaints relate to firmness not matching expectations for some sleep positions (side sleepers in particular) and some reports of durability/sagging concerns over time. Mattress comfort is inherently subjective, so treat individual complaints about firmness with that context in mind.

Before you order

  • Use the trial period fully before deciding — give a new mattress at least a few weeks to a month for your body to adjust before judging comfort.
  • If you’re a side sleeper or have specific firmness needs, check current firmness-specific reviews rather than the overall average, since mattress fit is highly personal.
  • Keep your order and delivery documentation in case you need to use the warranty or trial return later.

Bottom line

Nectar looks like a legitimate mattress brand with a strong risk-reducing trial policy. As with any mattress purchase, individual comfort will vary — the trial period exists specifically because of that.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
