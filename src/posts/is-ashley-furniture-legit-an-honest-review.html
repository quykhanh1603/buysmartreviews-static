Ashley Furniture is one of the largest furniture manufacturers and retailers in the US, with showrooms nationwide. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Ashley is a legitimate, massive furniture company, but its review record shows a consistent and notable pattern of product-quality and service complaints worth taking seriously.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 1,200 reviews, skewing negative.

Recurring themes

Negative: the clearest pattern is complaints about furniture quality and durability — several reviewers describe pieces showing wear or damage within just a few months of normal use — along with delivery scheduling issues and pressure to purchase extended warranties.

Positive: some reviewers cite helpful, low-pressure sales staff and satisfactory deliveries, particularly at specific locations.

Before you order

  • Read reviews for your specific local Ashley location, since it’s a franchise-heavy model and experience varies significantly by store.
  • Consider whether an extended warranty is worth it given the volume of quality complaints, rather than an automatic add-on.

Bottom line

Ashley Furniture is a legitimate, major retailer, but the consistency of quality-related complaints across review platforms means it’s worth inspecting pieces carefully and researching your specific local store.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
