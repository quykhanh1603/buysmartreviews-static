Understanding Ashley Furniture's Standing

Ashley Furniture is one of the largest furniture manufacturers and retailers in the US, with showrooms nationwide. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Customer Feedback Overview

  • Trustpilot: over 1,200 reviews, largely negative.

Common Complaints and Praise

Negative: A recurring issue among customers is the quality and durability of the furniture; many have reported that items show wear or damage after just a few months of regular use. Additionally, delivery scheduling has been problematic, and there are reports of pressure to buy extended warranties.

Positive: On the brighter side, some customers have shared experiences with helpful, low-pressure sales staff and satisfactory delivery services, especially at certain locations.

Important Considerations Before Purchasing

  • Take the time to read reviews for the specific Ashley location you're considering, as individual franchises can vary greatly in quality of service.
  • Evaluate the necessity of an extended warranty in light of the many quality complaints rather than viewing it as an automatic selection.

Final Thoughts

Ashley Furniture is undeniably a legitimate and large retailer in the furniture sector. However, the pattern of complaints about quality across review platforms suggests it’s wise to inspect items closely and do thorough research on your local store.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
