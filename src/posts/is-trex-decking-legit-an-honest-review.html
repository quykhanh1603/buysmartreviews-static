Upgrade Your Outdoor Living Experience

Trex is a leader in outdoor living solutions, specializing in high-performance composite decking, railing, lighting, and accessories. With over 30 years of innovation, Trex has established itself as the go-to brand for homeowners looking to enhance their outdoor spaces. Their products are engineered to withstand the elements while offering a beautiful, natural wood-like aesthetic.

Premium Composite Decking Options

Trex offers a wide range of decking collections that cater to various design preferences and budgets. Their decking is made from recycled materials, making it an eco-friendly choice that doesn't compromise on quality. Key features of Trex decking include:

  • Durability: Trex decking is designed to resist fading, staining, and scratching, ensuring it looks great for years to come.
  • SunComfortable™ Technology: This innovative feature keeps surfaces up to 35°F cooler than traditional decking, making your outdoor space more comfortable during hot summer months.
  • Variety of Colors and Textures: With multiple collections and a wide range of colors, homeowners can find the perfect match for their outdoor aesthetics.

Complete Your Outdoor Space

Beyond decking, Trex offers a comprehensive lineup of products to complete your outdoor living area. Their railing options include:

  • Composite Railing
  • Metal Railing
  • Glass Railing

Additionally, Trex provides a variety of lighting solutions, from post cap lights to stair lights, ensuring your outdoor space is both functional and inviting after the sun sets.

Planning Your Project with Trex

Trex offers useful tools to assist in planning your deck project. Their Deck Designer allows you to visualize your space with different decking and railing combinations. If you're unsure where to start, their Find Your Product Match Quiz can help guide you to the best options for your needs.
