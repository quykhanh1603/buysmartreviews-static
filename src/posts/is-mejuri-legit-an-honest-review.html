Understanding Mejuri

With an impressive 4 out of 5 cumulative rating on Trustpilot from over 5,000 reviews, Mejuri has established a solid reputation in the fine jewelry market. However, customer experiences vary significantly between their solid gold and gold vermeil offerings.

  • Rating: 4 out of 5 on Trustpilot
  • Reviews Count: Over 5,000
  • Product Types: 14k gold and gold vermeil jewelry

Overall Reputation

Mejuri generally receives high marks, but there is a notable disparity in satisfaction levels, particularly when comparing the solid gold pieces to the more affordable gold vermeil items.

What Customers Are Saying

Customer feedback showcases the strengths and weaknesses of Mejuri’s offerings. Many customers commend Mejuri’s solid 14k gold pieces for their durability, noting that they can withstand daily wear, including exposure to water. In contrast, the gold vermeil items face criticism for issues such as tarnishing, scratching, and breaking sooner than expected. There are also instances of missing stones and some reports of denied warranty claims.

What to Keep in Mind

Understanding the distinction between solid gold and gold vermeil is crucial, as expectations regarding durability can differ significantly. Additionally, be aware that Mejuri's return policy may offer store credit instead of refunds for certain circumstances, so checking this detail beforehand is advisable.
