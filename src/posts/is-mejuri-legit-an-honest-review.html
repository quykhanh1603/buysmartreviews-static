Mejuri is a direct-to-consumer fine jewelry brand known for everyday 14k gold pieces and gold vermeil items. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Mejuri is a legitimate, well-reviewed brand overall, but there’s a meaningful quality gap reported between its solid gold pieces and its lower-cost gold vermeil line worth knowing about.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4 out of 5 from over 5,000 reviews.

Recurring themes

Positive: the solid 14k gold pieces are frequently praised for durability, holding up well to daily wear including showering.

Negative: gold vermeil (gold-plated) pieces draw more consistent criticism for tarnishing, scratching, or breaking faster than expected for the price, along with some reports of missing stones and warranty claims being denied.

Before you order

  • Understand the difference between solid gold and gold vermeil listings before buying, since durability expectations differ significantly.
  • Note that Mejuri’s return/defect policy reportedly issues store credit rather than a refund in some cases — confirm this before purchasing if that matters to you.

Bottom line

Mejuri is a legitimate jewelry brand with strong reviews for its solid gold line. If considering the more affordable vermeil pieces, go in with realistic durability expectations.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
