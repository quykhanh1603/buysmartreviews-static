Concerns About Trustworthiness and Recent Changes

Trustworthiness is a key concern for Gardener’s Supply Company, particularly in light of its recent bankruptcy and ownership transition. After filing for Chapter 11 in June 2025, the company was acquired by Gardens Alive, Inc. just two months later. While new management is maintaining five retail locations, they are shifting distribution operations to Ohio, which has resulted in some service disruptions.

Customer Experiences and Ratings

Reviews from customers reflect significant dissatisfaction. On Trustpilot, the company has garnered over 800 reviews, revealing a notable trend of negative feedback, especially during the ownership transition. Similarly, on SmartCustomer, it holds a low average rating of about 1.3 out of 5 from 129 reviews, indicating serious concerns regarding customer satisfaction. Common complaints include unresponsive customer service, delayed shipments, and issues with the validity of gift certificates issued prior to the ownership change. Customers with outstanding gift certificates or orders made before August 2025 should reach out to the new management for verification before relying on them. Given these insights, it's advisable to start with smaller or lower-risk items if you decide to order from them.

The information above reflects publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
