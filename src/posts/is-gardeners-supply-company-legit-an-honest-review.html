Understanding Gardener’s Supply Company’s Recent Changes

Gardener’s Supply Company, known for its extensive range of gardening products, is currently in a period of transition. After filing for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in June 2025, the business was acquired by Gardens Alive, Inc. just two months later. The new management is maintaining five retail locations while shifting distribution operations to Ohio, which has led to some service disruptions.

Customer Feedback and Ratings

  • Trustpilot: The company has received over 800 reviews, with a notable trend of negative feedback, particularly during the ownership transition.
  • SmartCustomer: With a low average rating of approximately 1.3 out of 5 based on 129 reviews, customer satisfaction appears to be a concern.

Common Issues Reported

Negative Feedback: Many customers have reported unresponsive customer service, delayed shipments, and confusion regarding the validity of gift certificates issued prior to the ownership change.

Important Considerations for Shoppers

  • If you possess a gift certificate or have an order made before August 2025, it’s wise to reach out to the new ownership directly to verify its status.
  • To assess the current service quality, consider ordering smaller or lower-risk items before committing to larger purchases.

Final Thoughts

Despite the challenges, Gardener’s Supply Company continues to operate under new ownership following its bankruptcy. It’s crucial for customers to check the status of any previous orders or gift certificates directly before relying on them.
