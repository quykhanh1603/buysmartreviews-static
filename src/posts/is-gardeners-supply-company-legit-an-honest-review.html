Gardener’s Supply Company is a long-running Vermont-based garden products retailer. Here’s an important update before anything else: the company went through bankruptcy and a change of ownership in 2025.

Quick verdict

Gardener’s Supply Company filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in June 2025 and was acquired by Gardens Alive, Inc. in August 2025. The new owner is keeping five retail garden centers open but moving distribution operations to Ohio. This transition explains a lot of the service disruption described in recent reviews — including reports that the company lost access to previous customer records during the sale.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 800 reviews, skewing very negative, including complaints during and around the ownership transition.
  • SmartCustomer: around 1.3 out of 5 from 129 reviews.

Recurring themes

Negative: unresponsive customer service, items never shipping, and gift certificates from before the ownership change reportedly not being honored.

Before you order

  • If you have an old gift certificate or pending order from before August 2025, contact the new ownership directly to confirm it’s still valid rather than assuming.
  • Given the recent transition, order smaller/lower-risk items first to gauge current service quality before a larger purchase.

Bottom line

Gardener’s Supply Company is still operating, but under new ownership after a 2025 bankruptcy, with disrupted customer records. Confirm any older order or gift certificate status directly before relying on it.
