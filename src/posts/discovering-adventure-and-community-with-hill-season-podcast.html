Explore the Great Outdoors Through Hill Season Podcast

Since 2018, Hill Season Podcast has been a beacon for those who love running and the mountains. Hosted by Ivan Dimitrov, the podcast dives into inspiring conversations with athletes and community members who share their passion for outdoor adventures. Each episode provides insights into the lives of those who thrive in nature, making it a valuable resource for both seasoned runners and outdoor enthusiasts alike.

What to Expect from the Podcast

Listeners can look forward to engaging discussions that cover a range of topics related to trail running, endurance sports, and the camaraderie found in the running community. Recent episodes have featured notable guests, including:

  • The fastest Bulgarian at Ironman, Владислав Михайлов
  • Sports role models Виктор Асенов и Божидар Къртунов
  • Insights on the intersection of business, sports, and politics with Иван Христанов

Each episode can be enjoyed on popular platforms like Spotify and YouTube, allowing for flexibility in how you choose to listen or watch.

Community and Events

Hill Season Podcast is not just about listening; it's about connecting with a broader community. The podcast complements various community initiatives, including:

  • Hosting running competitions such as the Persenk Ultra and Thracian Ultra, which encourage participation and promote health.
  • A dedicated Facebook group with over 25,000 members, serving as a hub for discussions, advice, and shared experiences in running and mountain activities.
  • An active Discord community, where fans can engage with each other about their running journeys and adventures.

These initiatives showcase the podcast's commitment to fostering a supportive environment for those passionate about running and the great outdoors.

Why Hill Season Stands Out

What makes Hill Season Podcast particularly appealing is its focus on authentic storytelling. Ivan Dimitrov emphasizes the idea that "the mountains make people better," and this philosophy is evident in the episodes. The stories shared are not just about running but also about the connections formed through shared experiences in nature, creating a sense of belonging for listeners.

Moreover, the podcast serves as a platform for discussing the state of running in Bulgaria, providing insights into how the sport is evolving and the challenges within the community. Ivan actively engages with his audience, making the podcast a two-way conversation rather than just a one-sided broadcast.

Final Thoughts

Whether you are a seasoned runner, someone looking to start their journey, or simply an adventurist at heart, the Hill Season Podcast offers rich content that resonates well beyond the trails. With accessible episodes, community-driven initiatives, and heartfelt storytelling, it's an excellent choice for anyone looking to connect with the running culture and the beauty of the mountains.
