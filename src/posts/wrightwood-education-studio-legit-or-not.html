Welcome to Wrightwood Education Studio

Wrightwood Education Studio (WES) is a vibrant hub for artistic education located in Wrightwood, California. This studio was founded with the aim of making the arts accessible and enjoyable for individuals of all ages and skill levels. Whether you're interested in music, photography, or visual arts, WES offers a range of lessons, workshops, and courses tailored to foster creativity.

What They Offer

At WES, students can dive into various artistic disciplines. The studio provides music lessons for several instruments including guitar, piano, bass (both upright and electric), ukulele, and more. Additionally, WES boasts courses in recording, producing, and songwriting, ensuring that aspiring musicians have a comprehensive learning experience. There's also an opportunity for photography enthusiasts to enroll in an iPhone photography course, making it inclusive for those interested in visual arts.

Meet the Instructors

The heart of WES lies in its passionate instructors, Dylann and Kris Sveen. Together, they bring over fifteen years of experience in teaching music to students ranging from elementary to collegiate levels. Their enthusiasm is infectious, motivating students to explore their creativity and develop a love for music. They focus on individual student goals, whether it's playing for fun, performing live, or even releasing music on streaming platforms.

Community Feedback

Parents have expressed gratitude for the positive impact WES has had on their children. One parent, Brook, shared that her children have taken various classes, including piano and voice, and that the education received is comparable to what one might expect in a conservatory setting. Emily praised the instructors for their ability to connect with kids, while Anna highlighted the well-organized classes and the kindness of the staff. Such testimonials emphasize the supportive community WES has cultivated.

Affordability and Accessibility

WES not only focuses on quality education but also strives to keep it accessible. They offer online courses that consist of ten weeks of lessons, play-alongs, and more, priced at just $25 for lifetime access. This provides students with the flexibility to learn at their own pace, ensuring they have the right foundation and technique without the pressure of traditional class schedules.

Conclusion

With a rich history dating back to 1993 when it was originally founded as Keyboard Art, Wrightwood Education Studio has successfully nurtured a community dedicated to the arts. Under the guidance of Dylann and Kris, the studio has evolved to meet the needs of contemporary learners, making it a trustworthy choice for anyone looking to explore or enhance their artistic skills.

Final Thoughts

Wrightwood Education Studio stands out not just for its offerings but for its commitment to creating a nurturing environment for artists of all ages. With such a strong focus on community and creativity, WES is more than just a place to learn; it’s a space where individuals can truly flourish in their artistic pursuits.
