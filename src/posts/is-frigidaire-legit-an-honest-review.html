Frigidaire's Alarming Customer Ratings

With a shocking Trustpilot score of just 1.2 out of 5, Frigidaire faces significant criticism from consumers. Additionally, their rating with the Better Business Bureau is equally disheartening, sitting at around 1.03 out of 5, and they are not accredited.

Frequent Customer Concerns

Common Issues: Many customers have reported serious issues, including a notable case of a $2,000 refrigerator that failed after only four years due to an unaddressed defect. Other consumers have voiced frustrations regarding warranty claims and the difficulty of finding replacement parts, with some suggesting that minor problems might lead to costly full replacements instead of simple repairs.

Ratings mentioned are based on publicly available data at the time of writing and could change over time.

Bottom Line

Frigidaire's reputation is marred by serious reliability concerns and poor warranty support.
