Frigidaire is a well-known appliance manufacturer, selling both through retailers and directly online. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Frigidaire is a legitimate, established manufacturer, but its independent review record is among the weakest of any appliance brand we’ve checked — concentrated heavily in warranty support.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 1.2 out of 5.
  • BBB: a customer review score around 1.03 out of 5, not accredited.

Recurring themes

Negative: premature product failures (including a report of a $2,000 refrigerator failing within 4 years with a confirmed manufacturer defect left unresolved), warranty claims that are hard to get resolved, and difficulty getting replacement parts — with the company reportedly suggesting a full door replacement rather than a small, frequently-requested bracket part.

Before you order

  • Consider purchasing through a retailer with its own strong return/exchange policy rather than relying primarily on the manufacturer’s direct warranty process.
  • Register your appliance immediately and keep all documentation in case you need to escalate a warranty claim.

Bottom line

Frigidaire is a legitimate, established manufacturer, but its warranty support has a consistently weak review record. A strong retailer return policy is a reasonable backup plan.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
