A Look at Frigidaire's Reputation

Frigidaire is a notable player in the appliance industry, selling products both online and through various retailers. However, a dive into customer reviews reveals some significant red flags.

Consumer Feedback Highlights

  • Trustpilot: Approximately 1.2 out of 5.
  • BBB: A low score around 1.03 out of 5, and the company is not accredited.

Common Complaints

Navigating the Issues: Many customers report issues such as unexpected product failures, with one case highlighting a $2,000 refrigerator that malfunctioned within just four years due to a recognized manufacturer defect that was not addressed. Other frequent complaints point to difficulties in resolving warranty claims and challenges in acquiring replacement parts, sometimes leading to suggestions for costly full replacements rather than fixing minor issues.

What to Consider Before Purchasing

  • Opt for buying through retailers that offer robust return and exchange policies, rather than relying solely on Frigidaire’s warranty.
  • Immediately register your appliance and retain all relevant documents to facilitate any future warranty claims.

Final Thoughts

While Frigidaire remains a legitimate and established appliance manufacturer, the concerning trend in warranty support and product reliability is worth noting. It's wise to have a backup plan, such as a strong return policy from your retailer.
