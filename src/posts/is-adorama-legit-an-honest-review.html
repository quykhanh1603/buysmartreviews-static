Adorama is a New York-based photography, video, and electronics retailer, a longtime competitor to B&H in the pro camera space. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Adorama is a legitimate, BBB-accredited retailer with a genuinely mixed review record — solid on pricing and selection, weaker on post-purchase support for some customers.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 3,500 reviews, mixed.
  • BBB: accredited business.
  • SmartCustomer: around 3.3 out of 5 from nearly 480 reviews.

Recurring themes

Positive: wide product selection, competitive pricing, and fast shipping are frequently praised.

Negative: return process friction, customer service responsiveness, and condition disputes on used/open-box items are the most cited complaints.

Before you order

  • For open-box or used gear, read the condition description carefully and photograph the item immediately on arrival.
  • Understand the return window and process before ordering, particularly for higher-value camera gear.

Bottom line

Adorama is a legitimate, established retailer with solid pricing and selection. Extra care around used/open-box condition claims and return terms is worthwhile.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
