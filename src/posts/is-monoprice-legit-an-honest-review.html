Monoprice is a budget-focused electronics and cable retailer, in business since 2002. Here’s an honest look based on public review data — including a wide gap between sources.

Quick verdict

Monoprice is a legitimate, long-established retailer, but its rating varies dramatically depending on which platform you check — one of the widest gaps we’ve seen, so no single score tells the full story.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4.8 out of 5, with over 79% of customers rating the experience excellent or very good.
  • SmartCustomer: around 2.8 out of 5 from nearly 400 reviews.
  • BBB: not accredited, despite some third-party sources incorrectly citing a BBB grade.

Recurring themes

Positive: low prices relative to comparable cables and accessories from name-brand competitors are the most consistent praise point.

Negative: delayed shipping, unresponsive support, and inconsistent handling of warranty claims are the main complaint themes on lower-rated platforms.

Before you order

  • For budget cables and basic accessories (Monoprice’s core strength), risk is generally low. For anything requiring warranty support later, keep documentation from day one.

Bottom line

Monoprice is a legitimate, budget-friendly retailer, but given how much its rating varies by platform, don’t rely on any single score — the core cable/accessory business has less at stake than anything needing ongoing support.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
