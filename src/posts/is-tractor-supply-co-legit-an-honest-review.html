Tractor Supply Co. is a large rural lifestyle retailer selling farm, garden, and outdoor equipment through hundreds of stores plus online. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Tractor Supply is a legitimate, large, established retailer, but its independent review record is weak, concentrated in customer service and online order handling.

What customer ratings show

  • Sitejabber: around 1.4 out of 5 from 122 reviews.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Negative: customer service responsiveness, credit card/billing issues, and problems specific to certain local stores are the most cited complaints.

Positive: in-store shopping is generally regarded as the stronger experience compared to online orders, consistent with the brand’s community/rural-store-focused model.

Before you order

  • For larger equipment purchases, consider buying in-store where you can inspect the item, rather than online.
  • Check reviews for your specific local store if service quality matters for an ongoing relationship (e.g., livestock feed delivery).

Bottom line

Tractor Supply is a legitimate, major retailer, with in-store shopping generally the stronger experience over online ordering based on available reviews.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
