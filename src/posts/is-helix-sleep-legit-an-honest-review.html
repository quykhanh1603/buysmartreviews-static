Helix Sleep is a direct-to-consumer mattress brand known for a sleep quiz that recommends a model based on sleep position and preferences. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Helix Sleep is a legitimate, well-regarded mattress brand with a generally strong review record, especially through BBB.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: generally positive, though review volume is relatively modest for the brand’s size.
  • BBB: A+ accredited, with a strong complaint-resolution record.

Recurring themes

Positive: customers frequently mention the mattress being comfortable and helping with back pain, aligning with the brand’s personalized-recommendation approach.

Negative: a smaller set of reviewers report the recommended firmness or cooling not matching expectations, along with some sagging complaints after a period of use, and occasional customer service friction resolving those issues.

Before you order

  • Use the 100-night trial fully before deciding, since firmness/cooling mismatches are the main complaint category.
  • If the sleep quiz recommendation doesn’t feel right within the first couple of weeks, contact support early about a possible model swap rather than waiting out the trial.

Bottom line

Helix Sleep is a legitimate, generally well-reviewed mattress brand. Its trial period is the best tool for addressing the most common complaint (a mismatched firmness recommendation).

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
