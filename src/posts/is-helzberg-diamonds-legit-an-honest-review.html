Helzberg Diamonds is a jewelry retailer founded in 1915, operating over 200 stores across the US. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Helzberg is a legitimate, long-established retailer with a genuinely mixed review record — better than some mall-jewelry peers on Yelp, weaker on more complaint-focused platforms.

What customer ratings show

  • Yelp: around 3 out of 5 from nearly 1,750 reviews.
  • PissedConsumer: around 2.1 out of 5 (note: this platform specifically attracts dissatisfied customers, so treat it as a complaint log rather than a representative average).

Recurring themes

Negative: jewelry repair quality issues, reports of diamonds falling out of settings, and warranty disputes are the most consistently cited complaints.

Before you order

  • If sending jewelry in for repair, document its condition (photos) before and after.
  • Read the specific warranty terms for setting/stone security before relying on it.

Bottom line

Helzberg is a legitimate, long-established jeweler with a reasonably solid Yelp record but real complaint patterns around repairs and warranties. Document everything if you use their repair service.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
