Overview of Helzberg Diamonds

Founded in 1915, Helzberg Diamonds is a well-known jewelry retailer with over 200 locations throughout the U.S. Although the brand has a long-standing history, customer experiences can vary significantly.

Customer Feedback

  • Yelp: approximately 3 out of 5 stars from nearly 1,750 reviews.
  • PissedConsumer: around 2.1 out of 5 stars (this site tends to attract dissatisfied customers, so think of it more as a record of complaints).

Common Concerns

Negative Feedback: The recurring issues reported include poor jewelry repair quality, diamonds reportedly falling from their settings, and disputes related to warranties.

Advice for Shoppers

  • When sending jewelry for repair, ensure you document its condition with photos both before and after.
  • Thoroughly review the warranty terms regarding stone security and setting before making any assumptions.

Conclusion

In summary, Helzberg Diamonds is a legitimate and established jeweler with a decent overall Yelp rating. However, there are clear patterns of complaints, particularly concerning repairs and warranties, so it’s wise to keep thorough documentation if you're utilizing their repair services.

The ratings mentioned are based on publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
