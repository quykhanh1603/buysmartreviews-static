Is Helzberg Diamonds a Reliable Choice?

With a history dating back to 1915, Helzberg Diamonds boasts over 200 locations across the U.S. But does that mean you should trust them with your jewelry needs? Customer experiences can really vary, so it’s worth diving into the details.

What Are Customers Saying About Their Experience?

  • Yelp: They have around 3 out of 5 stars, based on nearly 1,750 reviews.
  • PissedConsumer: Their score is about 2.1 out of 5 stars, but keep in mind this site tends to attract complaints, so it might not be a complete picture.

Are There Any Issues You Should Be Aware Of?

Some common concerns pop up repeatedly. Many customers talk about poor quality in jewelry repairs, diamonds falling out of their settings, and issues related to warranties. If you go ahead with them, it’s definitely best to document the condition of your jewelry before sending it for repairs.

The ratings mentioned reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
