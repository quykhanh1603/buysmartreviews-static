Overview of Home Depot

As the leading home improvement retailer in the US, Home Depot offers a wide range of products, including major appliances and essential hardware. However, insights from customer reviews suggest that while the company is legitimate and publicly traded, some aspects of its service deserve scrutiny.

Customer Ratings Insight

  • BBB: Holds an A rating and is accredited.
  • Trustpilot: Scores an average of about 1.9 out of 5, based on nearly 580 reviews.

Common Customer Complaints

Negative Feedback: A significant number of customers have reported issues specifically related to appliance deliveries, citing failed or delayed deliveries, improper installation (including incidents involving leaks from broken hoses), and extended wait times for customer service assistance.

Considerations Before You Buy

  • When opting for appliance installation, inquire about the installer’s procedures, particularly for water and drain line connections, and ensure that leak testing is part of the process.
  • Get your delivery window in writing and be proactive in following up if the delivery is missed.

Final Thoughts

While Home Depot is a well-established retailer, caution is advisable, especially regarding appliance delivery and installation services, where many customer complaints have surfaced.
