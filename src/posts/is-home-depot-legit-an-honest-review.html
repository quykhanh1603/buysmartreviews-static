Home Depot is the largest home improvement retailer in the US, selling major appliances alongside its core hardware and building materials business. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Home Depot is a massive, legitimate, publicly traded company, but there’s a stark gap between its BBB accreditation and its Trustpilot record — particularly around appliance delivery and installation.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: A rating, accredited.
  • Trustpilot: around 1.9 out of 5 from nearly 580 reviews.

Recurring themes

Negative: for appliances specifically, repeated failed or delayed delivery attempts, improper installation (including at least one report of a leak from a broken drain hose), and long customer service wait times are the most cited issues.

Before you order

  • For appliance installation, ask about the installer’s specific process for connecting water/drain lines and confirm someone will test for leaks before leaving.
  • Confirm your delivery window in writing and follow up promptly if it’s missed.

Bottom line

Home Depot is a legitimate, major retailer. Appliance delivery and installation is the area most worth double-checking, given the volume of related complaints.
