Trustworthiness of Home Depot

Home Depot is considered a legitimate business, but there are notable concerns regarding its appliance delivery and installation services. Many customers have expressed dissatisfaction, especially with the reliability of these services. While it maintains a good standing in certain areas, others warrant caution.

Customer Ratings Insight

Home Depot holds an A rating with the Better Business Bureau (BBB) and is accredited, signaling a level of credibility. However, its average score on Trustpilot is about 1.9 out of 5, based on nearly 580 reviews, indicating significant customer dissatisfaction. Many complaints revolve around appliance deliveries, including missed or delayed arrivals, improper installations, and lengthy wait times for customer support. Given these issues, it's essential to proceed with caution, particularly if you're considering appliance purchases or installations.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
