Old Navy is a large, budget-friendly clothing retailer owned by Gap Inc. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Old Navy is a legitimate, massive retailer, with reviews reflecting the kind of mixed experience typical of high-volume budget clothing brands.

What customer ratings show

  • SmartCustomer: around 2.6 out of 5 from nearly 780 reviews.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Negative: delivery delays, customer service responsiveness, and order fulfillment issues (missing items, wrong sizes) are the most cited complaints.

Positive: in-store shoppers and customers with straightforward orders more often report satisfaction, consistent with a budget retailer where online order complexity drives more of the friction.

Before you order

  • Double-check your order confirmation for size/color/quantity before submitting, since fulfillment mix-ups are a common complaint.
  • Consider in-store pickup where available to sidestep shipping-related issues.

Bottom line

Old Navy is a legitimate, large retailer well-suited to budget shopping. Order accuracy and shipping are the areas most likely to cause friction.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
