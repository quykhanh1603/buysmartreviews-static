Understanding Old Navy's Reputation

Old Navy's average customer rating is notably low, sitting at 2.6 out of 5, reflecting a mix of satisfaction and frustration among its shoppers. As a budget fashion retailer, it offers a variety of affordable clothing options, but customer feedback reveals a significant range of experiences.

Customer Insights

  • SmartCustomer: averages around 2.6 out of 5 based on nearly 780 reviews.
  • BBB: is not accredited.

Challenges: A common theme in reviews highlights delivery delays, unresponsive customer service, and issues with order fulfillment, such as missing items and incorrect sizes.

Positive Feedback: Conversely, shoppers who visit physical stores or place straightforward orders often report higher satisfaction, suggesting that online order complexities contribute to dissatisfaction.

  • Be vigilant in reviewing your order details—size, color, and quantity—before confirming, as this can help avoid the frequent mix-ups noted by other customers.
  • If possible, opt for in-store pickup to reduce shipping-related complications.

Shoppers should weigh the affordable offerings against potential issues with order accuracy and delivery.

The ratings provided are based on publicly available information at the time of writing and may have changed since then.

Overall Assessment

Old Navy's offerings are tempting, but be prepared for some potential pitfalls.
