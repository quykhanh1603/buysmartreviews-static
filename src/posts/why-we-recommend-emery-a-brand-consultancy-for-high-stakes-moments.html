Understanding Emery's Approach to Brand Strategy

Emery is a specialized brand consultancy dedicated to guiding companies through significant transitions, known as inflection points. These are crucial moments when a brand's existing narrative no longer aligns with its future direction. Whether facing rapid growth, crisis, or a need for reinvention, Emery offers the expertise to navigate these challenges with clarity, courage, and conviction.

What Sets Emery Apart

Founded by John Emery, the consultancy thrives in situations where brands are at risk of losing their identity. Emery emphasizes that brands should not be viewed merely as decorative elements, but rather as fundamental operating systems that shape belief and behavior within organizations. They advocate for a strategic focus on aligning a company's identity, narrative, and experience with its future ambitions.

Services Offered

Emery’s offerings are comprehensive and tailored to meet the unique needs of their clients:

  • Inflection Point Diagnostic: Assessing the current state of a brand and identifying critical moments for evolution.
  • Strategic Brand Thesis: Crafting a foundational narrative that supports future growth.
  • Naming & Narrative Architecture: Developing a cohesive story that resonates with the target audience.
  • Brand System Design: Creating a visual and verbal identity that accurately represents the brand.
  • Brand Behavior & Decision Frameworks: Translating strategic insights into actionable practices.

Target Audience and Industries

Emery works with a diverse range of clients, including startups, small to medium-sized businesses (SMBs), and non-profits. Their focus is on organizations led by founders or leadership teams that recognize branding as a strategic asset. Their industry agnostic approach allows them to cater to clients across various sectors, including technology, healthcare, consumer brands, and social impact initiatives.
