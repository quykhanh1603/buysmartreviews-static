Vans is a well-known skate and casual footwear brand, part of VF Corporation. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Vans is a legitimate, established brand, but its direct online store draws consistent complaints around customer service and refund handling.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 1,000 reviews, mixed.
  • Sitejabber: around 2.3 out of 5 from 37 reviews.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Negative: quality complaints (stripes/decoration coming off, soles deteriorating within a couple months), refund issues where customers report not being credited for returns, and order cancellations.

Positive: in-store experience is generally rated better than online ordering.

Before you order

  • Keep your return tracking confirmation and follow up if a refund doesn’t post within the stated window.
  • If a local store is available, consider shopping in person given the gap between in-store and online sentiment.

Bottom line

Vans is a legitimate, established brand, but online order and refund handling draws consistent complaints. Keep documentation and consider in-store shopping where available.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
