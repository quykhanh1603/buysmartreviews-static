Understanding Ollie's Offerings

Ollie is a fresh dog food subscription service, dedicated to delivering human-grade meals right to your door. Many dog owners have turned to Ollie, hoping to enhance their pets' diets with high-quality nutrition.

Customer Feedback Overview

  • Trustpilot Ratings: Over 9,000 reviews with a mix of positive and negative experiences.
  • Food Quality Ratings: Independent expert reviews often rate the meals highly, with scores ranging from 4.5 to 5 out of 5.

What Customers Are Saying

Positive Insights: The majority of pet owners report that their dogs eagerly enjoy mealtime, even those who were previously picky eaters. Many also note improvements in their dog's digestion and overall energy levels.

Concerns Raised: Some complaints filed with the BBB highlight issues such as unexpected shipments, challenges with cancellation, and charges being sent to old addresses following a move. In addition, a handful of reviews mention that the food's appearance may not always meet expectations upon arrival.

Important Considerations

  • If you move, ensure you update your address and payment details to avoid issues.
  • Consider setting a calendar reminder before your next shipment if you plan to change or pause your deliveries.

Final Thoughts

Ollie has received favorable reviews for the quality of its dog food, but managing subscription details can be tricky. It's essential to stay proactive with your account settings to avoid any potential frustrations.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
