Ollie is a fresh dog food subscription service, delivering human-grade meals to your door. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Ollie is a legitimate company with generally positive product feedback, but subscription management (delivery timing, cancellation) is a recurring source of complaints worth watching.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 9,000 reviews, mixed to positive.
  • Independent expert review sites rate the food itself highly (4.5-5 out of 5 in some cases).

Recurring themes

Positive: the large majority of reviews describe enthusiastic mealtime response, including from previously picky eaters, plus improvements in digestion and energy.

Negative: BBB complaints center on unwanted shipments, difficulty canceling, and charges sent to outdated addresses after a move. A smaller number of reviews mention product appearance issues on arrival.

Before you order

  • Update your address and payment info promptly if you move, and confirm any cancellation request in writing.
  • Set a calendar reminder ahead of your next scheduled shipment if you want to pause or adjust delivery.

Bottom line

Ollie’s core product is well-regarded. Subscription and shipping logistics are the areas most likely to cause frustration, so stay on top of account settings.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
