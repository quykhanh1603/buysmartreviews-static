Is Brilliant Earth a Trustworthy Choice?

Brilliant Earth has carved out a niche as a reputable online jeweler, well-regarded for its commitment to ethically sourced materials. Overall, it boasts a respectable rating, though there are a few specific issues noted by some customers.

Customer Feedback Overview

  • Trustpilot: approximately 4 out of 5 stars based on nearly 1,500 customer reviews.

What Customers Are Saying

On the positive side: Many customers praise the quality of the products and the attentive customer service they experienced.

Conversely: Some reviews highlight concerns such as defective items, including broken settings soon after the purchase and issues with replacement settings not being aligned properly. Additionally, a few customers expressed frustration regarding delivery processes, particularly the inability to hold packages at certain carrier locations.

Important Considerations

  • Upon receiving your order, carefully examine both the setting and the stone, documenting any issues with photographs right away.
  • If you need your delivery by a specific date, check on the delivery and signature requirements beforehand.

Final Thoughts

Brilliant Earth stands out as a legitimate online jeweler with generally positive feedback from customers. While the reports of defects are notable, they seem to be infrequent, suggesting that most buyers have a satisfying experience.

The ratings mentioned above are based on publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
