Trustworthy Reputation

Brilliant Earth has received nearly 1,500 customer reviews, averaging around 4 out of 5 stars on Trustpilot, showcasing its popularity in the online jewelry market. While it prides itself on ethically sourced materials, some customers have reported issues that deserve attention.

Customer Insights

  • Positive Feedback: Many customers commend the high quality of the jewelry and the attentive service they received.
  • Negative Feedback: However, a few reviews mention defects like broken settings shortly after purchase and misaligned replacement settings, alongside complaints about delivery challenges that include restrictions on package holds.

Important Tips:

  • When you receive your order, inspect both the setting and stone, and document any issues with photographs immediately.
  • If you require delivery by a specific date, confirm the delivery and signature requirements in advance.

Brilliant Earth is a legitimate choice for online jewelry shopping, with mostly positive customer experiences despite some reported defects.

The ratings mentioned above are based on publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
