Brilliant Earth is an online jeweler focused on ethically sourced diamonds and lab-grown gemstones. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Brilliant Earth is a legitimate, well-known jeweler with a decent overall rating, though a handful of specific quality complaints are worth being aware of.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4 out of 5 from nearly 1,500 reviews.

Recurring themes

Positive: good customer service and product quality are cited by most reviewers.

Negative: a smaller number of reviewers describe specific defect issues — a broken setting shortly after purchase, or a crooked replacement setting — along with some delivery-related frustration (restrictions on holding packages at carrier locations).

Before you order

  • Inspect the setting and stone carefully upon arrival, and document any issue with photos immediately.
  • Confirm delivery/signature requirements ahead of time if you have a tight schedule.

Bottom line

Brilliant Earth is a legitimate online jeweler with a generally solid rating. The specific defect complaints reported are worth a careful inspection on arrival, but don’t appear to be the norm.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
