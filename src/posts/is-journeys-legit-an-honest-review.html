Journeys is a mall-based shoe retailer targeting a younger demographic, operated by Genesco. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Journeys is a legitimate, established retailer, but its independent review record shows consistent dissatisfaction, concentrated around online order fulfillment and shipping.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 226 reviews, mixed.
  • Sitejabber: around 1.8 out of 5 from 244 reviews.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Negative: shipping and delivery delays, order fulfillment errors (wrong size/color or incomplete shipments), and refund processing delays are the most cited complaints.

Positive: some reviewers specifically praise individual in-store employees for good service and affordable pricing.

Before you order

  • Double-check your order confirmation for size and color before submitting.
  • Consider in-store purchase if a location is nearby, given the gap between in-store and online order sentiment.

Bottom line

Journeys is a legitimate retailer with generally weak online order reviews. In-store shopping appears to be the more consistently positive experience.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
