Understanding Journeys' Reputation

Journeys operates as a mall-based shoe retailer under the umbrella of Genesco, and it primarily caters to a younger demographic. However, what does the feedback from customers reveal about their shopping experience?

Insights from Customer Reviews

  • Trustpilot: Approximately 226 reviews show a mixed bag of opinions.
  • Sitejabber: The retailer holds a low rating of around 1.8 out of 5 based on 244 reviews.
  • BBB: Journeys is not accredited, which could be a concern for potential shoppers.

The Good and The Bad

Negative Feedback: The most frequent complaints center on issues such as shipping delays, incorrect order fulfillment (including wrong sizes or colors), and lengthy refund processing times.

Positive Feedback: On the flip side, some customers have highlighted the excellent service provided by staff members in-store and noted the affordability of the products.

Shopping Tips

  • Make sure to verify your order details thoroughly before confirming any purchases.
  • If there’s a store nearby, you might want to consider shopping in person, as the in-store experience tends to receive better feedback.

Final Thoughts

While Journeys is undoubtedly a legitimate retailer, its online ordering experience often falls short. If you’re looking for a positive shopping experience, opting for in-store purchases may be the better choice.

The ratings mentioned reflect publicly available information at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
