Understanding Journeys' Reputation

Journeys operates as a mall-based shoe retailer under the umbrella of Genesco, primarily attracting a younger demographic. However, when it comes to customer feedback, what do shoppers really think about their experiences with the brand?

Insights from Customer Reviews

Examining various review platforms reveals a spectrum of opinions. On Trustpilot, approximately 226 reviews showcase a mixture of satisfaction and dissatisfaction. In contrast, Sitejabber presents a more concerning picture, with the retailer holding a low rating of about 1.8 out of 5 based on 244 reviews. Furthermore, it's worth noting that Journeys is not accredited by the Better Business Bureau (BBB), which could raise some flags for potential shoppers.

The Good and The Bad

The feedback gathered from customers reflects both positive and negative aspects of shopping at Journeys. Common complaints often focus on issues such as shipping delays, incorrect order fulfillment—like receiving the wrong sizes or colors—and extended refund processing times. On a brighter note, some customers have praised the in-store service, applauding the helpfulness of staff members and the affordability of products available at Journeys.

Final Thoughts

While Journeys is undoubtedly a legitimate retailer, the shopping experience can vary significantly, particularly when ordering online. For those seeking a more reliable and positive experience, considering in-store purchases may be the best option.

The ratings mentioned reflect publicly available information at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
