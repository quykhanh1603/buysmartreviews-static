Introduction to Orbit Lifestyle

Orbit Lifestyle specializes in personalized online coaching and wellness, focusing on bridging the gap between fitness and mental health. Their mission is to create sustainable habits around training, nutrition, and lifestyle, helping clients achieve long-lasting results.

What They Offer

At Orbit Lifestyle, clients receive personalized training programs tailored to their individual circumstances, including available equipment and fitness levels. The coaching encompasses not just physical training but also nutrition guidance and lifestyle coaching aimed at improving time management and organization.

The program is designed to help you:

  • Lose fat
  • Build muscle
  • Boost mood
  • Gain confidence
  • Move pain-free
  • Grow in mind and body

Upon signing up, clients can book a free discovery call to discuss their goals before committing to a plan.

Meet Your Coach

Your primary coach is Ma'Kenan Mitchell, the founder of Orbit Lifestyle. He emphasizes a supportive approach, guiding clients regardless of their experience level. His philosophy, "Always forward, forward always," reflects his commitment to helping clients achieve their fitness goals.

In-Depth Coaching Approach

Orbit Lifestyle’s coaching is characterized by:

  • Nutrition Coaching: Clients receive personalized nutrition plans and ongoing support to ensure they know how to eat for their desired results.
  • Movement Focus: The program prioritizes joint stability and increased mobility through dedicated strength work, reducing the risk of injury.
  • Mental Strength: The coaching approach encourages overcoming mental barriers, reviewing lifestyle habits, and providing the structure necessary for progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clients often wonder about the specifics of online personal training:

  • What is online personal training? It consists of tailored training and nutrition programming, frequent support via in-app messenger, and regular feedback, allowing clients to progress with guidance from their coach.
  • Do you need a gym membership? No, the training plans are customized based on the equipment clients have available, making it suitable for those who may not have access to a gym.
  • How is client progress measured? Progress is measured through various metrics, allowing for a comprehensive view of improvements in strength and overall fitness.

Conclusion

Orbit Lifestyle presents a valuable option for individuals seeking personalized coaching in fitness and mental health. With a strong client support system, tailored plans, and a focus on comprehensive well-being, it’s a reputable choice for those looking to embark on or continue their health journey.
