Welcome to the Redwood Empire Remodelers Association

The Redwood Empire Remodelers Association (RERA) is a reputable organization based in Santa Rosa, California, established in 1991. This association serves as a hub for over 70 professionals within the remodeling industry. RERA's primary mission is to promote the professionalism and integrity of its members, making it an excellent resource for homeowners seeking reliable remodeling services.

Key Features of RERA

RERA stands out for its commitment to high standards. Members are required to pledge adherence to core values of honesty, integrity, and responsibility in their business dealings. Additionally, all contractors and installers associated with RERA must submit a current certificate of general liability insurance annually. This requirement helps ensure that clients can trust the contractors they hire through the association.

Educational and Community Engagement Opportunities

The association is not only focused on connecting homeowners with remodelers but also prioritizes the education and skill development of its members. RERA actively participates in programs designed to enhance labor skills in the remodeling industry. Furthermore, the organization encourages and supports community service projects, reflecting a strong commitment to giving back to the community.

How to Find the Right Contractor

If you are in the market for remodeling services, it's recommended to hire a contractor affiliated with RERA. The association's vetting process and ongoing education requirements give clients peace of mind, knowing that they are working with qualified professionals. While RERA provides a directory of its members, specific details about individual contractors might not be extensively detailed on the website, which could require some additional research on potential hires.

Conclusion

In a market where trust and quality are paramount, the Redwood Empire Remodelers Association stands as a significant authority in the remodeling sector. By focusing on professionalism, integrity, and community engagement, RERA offers a reliable platform for homeowners to find experienced remodelers. Be sure to consider RERA as your go-to resource when planning your next home improvement project.
