Chewy is one of the largest online pet supply retailers in the US, selling food, medication, and accessories with subscription auto-ship options. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Chewy is a legitimate, large-scale retailer with an unusually strong reputation for customer service, backed by one of the largest review volumes of any company in this category.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 13,000 reviews, one of the largest sample sizes of any retailer covered on this site.

Recurring themes

Positive: Chewy has a well-documented reputation for going beyond standard service — reviewers repeatedly mention fast shipping, responsive support, and even unprompted gestures like sending flowers or a card to customers who lost a pet. Auto-ship subscribers frequently report years of reliable, uneventful reorders.

Negative: the most common complaints involve delays on medication orders, occasional product quality issues (such as damaged or expired items on arrival), and friction with promotional discounts or state sales tax on prescription items in some regions.

Before you order

  • For pet medication, order with buffer time rather than waiting until your pet’s current supply runs out.
  • Check expiration dates on food and treats on arrival, particularly for less-frequently-ordered items.
  • Keep order confirmations for auto-ship subscriptions so you can quickly resolve any billing questions.

Bottom line

Chewy is a legitimate, well-reviewed pet supply retailer with a strong customer-service reputation. Most reported issues cluster around medication timing and occasional product condition — both manageable with a bit of buffer time.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
