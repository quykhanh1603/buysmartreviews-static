What’s the Reputation of Lowe’s?

You might be wondering how Lowe’s stands in the vast world of home improvement retailers. They’re one of the biggest players in the U.S., going head-to-head with Home Depot, especially when it comes to appliances and building supplies. Public review data gives us a good insight into how customers feel about their services.

How Do Customers Feel About Lowe's?

  • BBB: Although they’re not accredited, Lowe’s has an A+ rating on their own site, which sounds positive.
  • PissedConsumer: On this site, they’ve got a rating around 2 out of 5 from over 2,600 reviews. But remember, this platform tends to attract more unhappy customers, so take that rating with a grain of salt.

What Are the Common Complaints?

Some recurring issues pop up among customer feedback. Many people report problems like installation damage and safety concerns with delivered appliances. Additionally, missed or incorrect deliveries and incomplete orders are pretty common complaints.

If you do decide to order from Lowe’s, it’s wise to inspect your appliances right when they arrive and to document any problems with photos before the delivery crew leaves. Also, if you’re having something installed, especially with gas or water lines, make sure the installer checks those connections thoroughly before they go.

The information here is based on publicly available data at the time of writing, and it might change down the line.
