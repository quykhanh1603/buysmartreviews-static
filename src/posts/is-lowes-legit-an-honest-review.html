Lowe’s is one of the largest home improvement retailers in the US, competing directly with Home Depot on appliances and building materials. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Lowe’s is a legitimate, massive retailer, with a review pattern very similar to its main competitor — strong on paper credentials, weaker on independent review sentiment, particularly for appliance delivery and installation.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: not accredited, though the company cites an A+ rating on its own site.
  • PissedConsumer: around 2 out of 5 from over 2,600 reviews (note: this platform specifically attracts dissatisfied customers, so treat it as a complaint log rather than a representative average).

Recurring themes

Negative: installation damage and safety concerns with delivered appliances, missed or wrong deliveries, and incomplete orders are the most cited issues.

Before you order

  • Inspect appliances for damage immediately at delivery, before the crew leaves, and document with photos.
  • For gas or water-line connections specifically, confirm the installer tests the connection before departing.

Bottom line

Lowe’s is a legitimate, major retailer with a review pattern similar to its main competitor. Careful inspection at delivery/installation time is worth the extra few minutes.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
