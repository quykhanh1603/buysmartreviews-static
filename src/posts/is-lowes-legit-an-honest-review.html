Understanding Lowe's Reputation

Lowe’s is one of the largest home improvement retailers in the US, competing directly with Home Depot on appliances and building materials. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Customer Feedback Insights

  • BBB: While not accredited, Lowe’s boasts an A+ rating on its own site.
  • PissedConsumer: The retailer holds a rating of around 2 out of 5 from over 2,600 reviews. Keep in mind that this platform often attracts dissatisfied customers, making it a bit of a complaint log rather than a balanced average.

Common Complaints

Negative: Many customers have reported issues such as installation damage, safety concerns regarding delivered appliances, and problems with missed or incorrect deliveries as well as incomplete orders.

Tips for a Smoother Experience

  • Always inspect appliances for damage immediately upon delivery, before the crew leaves, and make sure to document any issues with photos.
  • For connections involving gas or water lines, double-check that the installer tests these connections before they depart.

The Final Word

While Lowe’s is a legitimate major retailer, its review patterns mirror those of its competitors. Taking a few extra minutes to inspect your items during delivery or installation could save you from potential headaches.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
