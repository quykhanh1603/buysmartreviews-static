Pandora is a globally known jewelry brand famous for its charm bracelet system, sold through branded stores and department retailers. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Pandora is a legitimate, large, established brand with reasonably strong reviews on Trustpilot specifically, though other platforms show more customer dissatisfaction — a reminder to check more than one source.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4 out of 5 from around 1,500 reviews.
  • PissedConsumer: around 1.9 out of 5 from over 350 reviews (note: this platform specifically attracts dissatisfied customers, so treat it as a complaint log rather than a representative average).

Recurring themes

Positive: fast delivery and product quality/appearance are the most consistent praise points.

Negative: delivery delays beyond the promised date, and on BBB specifically, complaints about products being added to receipts without clear consent.

Before you order

  • Review your itemized receipt in-store before paying to catch any unwanted add-ons.
  • If ordering online for a specific date (a gift), build in buffer time given reported delivery delays.

Bottom line

Pandora is a legitimate, well-known brand. Trustpilot reviews are more favorable than some other platforms, so checking more than one source before a big purchase is worthwhile.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
