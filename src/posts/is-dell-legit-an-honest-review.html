Dell's Reputation Overview

Dell stands out as one of the largest computer manufacturers globally, offering everything from laptops to enterprise solutions. However, the experiences shared by customers reveal a disparity between its strong BBB accreditation and feedback on independent review sites.

Customer Ratings Insight

  • BBB: Holds an A+ rating and is accredited, though this grading doesn't account for actual customer experiences.
  • Trustpilot: Almost 3,500 reviews, with a notable skew towards negative feedback.

Common Customer Feedback

Negative Experiences: Many users report challenges with customer service, citing difficulties in resolving issues, extended support delays, and durability concerns with products that fail sooner than expected.

Things to Consider Before Purchasing

  • If you foresee needing significant support, think about investing in an extended warranty or support plan due to the volume of complaints related to customer service.
  • Make sure to register your product and maintain your purchase documentation for warranty claims.

Final Thoughts

Dell is a legitimate and vast manufacturer, but potential buyers should prepare for the likelihood of needing extra patience or support options when ordering directly from them.

Ratings and feedback mentioned above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
