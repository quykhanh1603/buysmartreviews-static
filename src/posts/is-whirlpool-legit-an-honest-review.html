Whirlpool is one of the largest appliance manufacturers in the world, selling through retailers and directly online. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Whirlpool is a legitimate, BBB-accredited manufacturer, but current customer review sentiment is weak, concentrated in customer service and repair/warranty responsiveness.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 476 reviews.
  • BBB: A+ accredited (the BBB notes complaint volume is small relative to Whirlpool’s total US unit sales).

Recurring themes

Negative: long customer service hold times, warranty and refund requests facing delays or denials, and repeated repair visits without resolution are the most cited complaints. Some reviewers also report missed or canceled repair appointments.

Before you order

  • Register your appliance right after purchase and keep the receipt accessible for any warranty claim.
  • If scheduling a repair, confirm the appointment in writing and follow up promptly if there’s a no-show.

Bottom line

Whirlpool is a legitimate, massive, accredited manufacturer, but its repair/warranty service process draws consistent complaints. Keep documentation organized from the start.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
