Overview of Whirlpool

Whirlpool is recognized as one of the leading appliance manufacturers globally, distributing products through both retail channels and their online platform. This review dives into customer experiences and sentiments based on available public reviews.

Current Customer Sentiments

While Whirlpool is a BBB-accredited company, the overall sentiment among customers appears to be trending negatively, especially concerning their customer service and warranty processes.

Ratings Breakdown

  • Trustpilot: approximately 476 reviews.
  • BBB: holds an A+ accreditation, with complaint volume noted as minor relative to total U.S. sales.

Common Issues Reported

Negative Feedback: Customers frequently mention long wait times on customer service calls, difficulties in obtaining timely warranty service or refunds, and repeated repair visits without satisfactory resolution. Additionally, some users reported issues with missed or canceled repair appointments, which compounds frustration.

Tips for Potential Buyers

  • It’s advisable to register your appliance immediately upon purchase and to keep your receipt handy for any future warranty claims.
  • When scheduling repairs, ensure to confirm your appointment in writing, and follow up if there’s a no-show to avoid surprises.

Conclusion

In summary, Whirlpool is undeniably a significant and accredited manufacturer in the appliance sector; however, the complaints surrounding their repair and warranty services are noteworthy. Keeping meticulous documentation from the onset may help streamline any future issues.

The ratings and information referenced in this article reflect publicly available data as of the time of writing and may be subject to change.
