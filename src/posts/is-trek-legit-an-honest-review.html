Trek is one of the largest bicycle manufacturers in the world, founded in 1976, selling through its own branded stores and dealer network. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Trek is a legitimate, long-established manufacturer, with reviews varying quite a bit by specific store location since much of the buying experience happens through independently-run Trek retail locations.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 336 reviews, mixed.
  • BBB: not accredited at the corporate level; individual store locations have separate BBB profiles with varying ratings.

Recurring themes

Positive: helpful, knowledgeable staff at specific locations are frequently praised by name.

Negative: paint quality complaints (flaking within months in some reports), pricing concerns relative to competitors, and e-bike motor reliability issues after relatively low mileage.

Before you order

  • Check reviews for your specific local Trek store, since the buying and service experience is largely location-dependent.
  • For e-bikes specifically, ask about the motor warranty terms in detail before buying.

Bottom line

Trek is a legitimate, major manufacturer. Because most of the experience runs through individual retail locations, checking your specific local store’s reputation matters more than the brand average.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
