Understanding Sally Beauty

With a Yelp rating of approximately 2.9 out of 5 from over 13,500 reviews, customers have mixed feelings about their experiences with Sally Beauty. This renowned beauty supply retailer spans numerous locations across the United States, offering a range of professional-grade hair and beauty products. Analyzing customer feedback provides insight into their overall shopping experience.

  • Trustpilot: Approximately 480 reviews, showing a mixed response.
  • Yelp: Scored around 2.9 out of 5 from over 13,500 reviews.

What Shoppers Are Experiencing

Common Complaints: Shoppers often voice concerns about the online ordering process and product quality, as well as issues with promotional offers that don’t meet expectations.

Positive Experiences: Those who visit physical stores generally report a more pleasant experience, describing better customer service and product selection.

Considerations for Shopping

  • If there’s a Sally Beauty location near you, consider shopping in person to take advantage of the more favorable reviews about in-store experiences.
  • Be sure to carefully check the terms of any promotions before making online purchases.

Sally Beauty stands as a legitimate retailer, but experiences can vary significantly between online and brick-and-mortar shopping.

The ratings mentioned are based on publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
