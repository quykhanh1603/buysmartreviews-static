Sally Beauty is a large beauty supply retailer with hundreds of stores across the US, selling professional-grade hair and beauty products. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Sally Beauty is a legitimate, large, established retailer, with a genuinely mixed review record concentrated around website/online ordering experience.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 480 reviews, mixed.
  • Yelp: around 2.9 out of 5 from over 13,500 reviews.

Recurring themes

Negative: website/ordering issues, product quality concerns on specific items, and promotional-offer complaints are the most cited issues.

Positive: in-store service is generally regarded more favorably by reviewers who shop in person.

Before you order

  • If a local store is available, consider shopping in person given the gap between in-store and online sentiment.
  • Double-check promotional terms before checkout if ordering to take advantage of a specific offer.

Bottom line

Sally Beauty is a legitimate, established retailer. In-store shopping appears to be the more consistently positive experience based on available reviews.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
