An Overview of Sally Beauty

Sally Beauty is a large beauty supply retailer with hundreds of locations across the United States, specializing in professional-grade hair and beauty products. This review evaluates the feedback from customers to give you a clearer picture of their shopping experience.

Analyzing Customer Feedback

  • Trustpilot: Approximately 480 reviews, showing a mixed response.
  • Yelp: Scored around 2.9 out of 5 from over 13,500 reviews.

What Customers Are Saying

Common Complaints: Shoppers frequently mention issues with the website and ordering process, along with specific product quality concerns and dissatisfaction with promotional offers.

Positive Notes: Customers who shop in-store tend to report a more favorable experience, highlighting better service and product availability.

Things to Consider

  • If you have a Sally Beauty nearby, it might be worth visiting in person due to the better reviews for in-store shopping.
  • Make sure to review the terms of any promotional offers before placing your order online.

The Final Takeaway

Sally Beauty holds its ground as a legitimate and established retailer, yet the shopping experience can differ greatly between online and in-person visits.

The ratings mentioned are based on publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
