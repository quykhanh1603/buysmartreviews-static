MAC Cosmetics is a well-known, professional-grade makeup brand sold through its own stores, site, and major retailers. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

MAC is a legitimate, established brand with well-regarded product quality, though online ordering and promotional handling draw specific, recurring complaints.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: mixed reviews across multiple regional pages.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Positive: product texture and quality, plus helpful in-store staff, are frequently praised.

Negative: promotional offers (free gift items) running out before customers can claim them, delivery delays, and refund processing time along with customer service hold times are the most cited complaints.

Before you order

  • If ordering to claim a specific promotion, do so as early as possible in the promo window given the reported stock-out issue.
  • Keep order confirmations handy in case a refund takes longer than expected.

Bottom line

MAC is a legitimate, well-regarded brand for product quality. Promotional stock and shipping speed are the areas most likely to cause frustration.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
