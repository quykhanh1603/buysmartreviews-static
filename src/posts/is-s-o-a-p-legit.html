Discover the Handcrafted Quality of S.O.A.P

S.O.A.P, or Soothing Oasis of Aromatic Pleasure, specializes in creating unique, handcrafted soap and body products that focus on quality and natural ingredients. Each product is crafted in small batches, which helps maintain the integrity and effectiveness of the ingredients. This brand emphasizes a commitment to wellness through its carefully designed skincare solutions that cater to various skin types, including sensitive skin.

Explore the Product Lineup

S.O.A.P offers a diverse selection of products that extend beyond traditional soap. Here’s a glimpse of what you can find:

  • Soaps: The star of the collection, featuring top-sellers like the Honeysuckle and Sexy Man soaps, both priced at $6.00. These soaps are known for their nourishing properties and delightful fragrances.
  • Salves: These topical treatments, priced at $10.00 each, include options like Calendula and Pine Tar, designed to soothe various skin irritations and provide deep hydration.
  • Body Lotion: At $10.99, this non-greasy lotion absorbs quickly and is available in several scent options, including Lavender and Raspberry Vanilla.
  • Laundry Powder: Priced at $18.00, this eco-friendly laundry solution uses all-natural ingredients and promises to be tough on stains without harsh chemicals.
  • Mystery Mix: For $35.00, customers can indulge in a surprise selection of five soaps, offering a fun way to explore S.O.A.P's offerings.

The S.O.A.P Philosophy

What sets S.O.A.P apart is not just their product range but the intention behind each item. Their journey started as a passion project and has continued to grow while staying true to their core values of quality and natural wellness. Customers can feel confident that they are investing in products made with care and purpose.
