Introduction to Arwem: A Family Legacy in Food Production

Arwem has a rich history dating back to 1964 when Mr. Constant Van Dyck founded the company focusing on biscuits, chocolates, and confections. Over the years, this family-run business has evolved into a prominent player in food production, renowned for its high-quality offerings and extensive product line.

What Arwem Offers: A Diverse Product Range

As specialists in the food industry, Arwem provides a full spectrum of products that cater to various consumer needs. Their portfolio includes:

  • Chocolates
  • Biscuits
  • Patisserie items
  • Snacks
  • Confectionery

This diversity not only showcases their commitment to quality but also their ability to adapt to market demands, ensuring that clients can find a wide array of products suitable for their retail locations.

Quality and Safety Standards

Quality is at the core of Arwem's business model. The company has received several certifications, including IFS Broker, which guarantees the safety and quality of their products. They also offer organic options, appealing to health-conscious consumers. These certifications highlight their stringent quality control measures, which are critical in the food industry.

Global Reach and Distribution Network

Arwem is not just limited to local markets; they have established a robust international distribution network. This allows them to export products beyond Europe, bringing their goods to a wider audience. Their partnerships with retailers and discounters across Europe further solidify their presence in the food market.

Commitment to Innovation and Customer Satisfaction

With over 50 years of experience, Arwem prides itself on its ability to innovate while staying true to its roots. Their approach includes market studies, product development, and a strong emphasis on customer relationships. This focus ensures that they meet the needs of their partners and the demands of consumers effectively.

Contact and Further Information

For businesses interested in sourcing high-quality food products, Arwem offers excellent customer service and support. Their headquarters in Ovifat, Belgium, is equipped to handle inquiries and facilitate partnerships worldwide.

In summary, Arwem represents a blend of tradition, quality, and innovation in the food industry, making them a reliable source for a wide range of food products.
