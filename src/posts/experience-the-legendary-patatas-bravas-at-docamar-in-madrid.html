Discover Docamar: A Madrid Tradition

Located in the heart of Madrid, at Calle de Alcalá 337, Docamar is a cherished local bar that has been serving its famous patatas bravas since 1963. Known for their unique flavor and secret sauce, these beloved tapas have earned a rightful place in the culinary landscape of the city.

A Menu That Celebrates Tradition

At Docamar, the menu is simple but full of heart. The star of the show is undoubtedly the patatas bravas, which are made with a secret family recipe that has been passed down through generations. Alongside the bravas, the bar offers a variety of dishes perfect for sharing, showcasing the rich flavors of traditional Spanish cuisine.

For those looking for something different, the bar also features a daily special menu available from Tuesday to Friday, which includes a drink and dessert for a set price, providing an excellent value for money.

Enjoy the Atmosphere

Docamar prides itself on being a neighborhood bar, embodying a warm and inviting atmosphere where customers can enjoy their meals in comfort. While the interior is bustling, there is also a smoke-free terrace area for those who prefer dining outside. Guests can expect a casual dining experience, making it a great spot for friends, families, or anyone looking to indulge in some authentic Spanish fare.

Convenience and Accessibility

Whether you want to dine in, take away, or enjoy delivery, Docamar has options to suit your needs. Customers can place orders for pickup directly at the bar or through delivery services like Uber Eats. However, keep in mind that the bar does not accept reservations; seating is provided on a first-come, first-served basis. If there is a wait, the staff will inform you when your table is ready.

Policies and Customer Care

Docamar has set clear policies to ensure every visit is enjoyable. Guests are welcomed until 30 minutes before closing, and the kitchen remains open for orders until 15 minutes before the bar closes. Payment can be made via credit card (excluding Diners Club and American Express) or cash. Additionally, pets are welcome in the terrace area, making it a friendly space for all patrons.

If you encounter any issues during your visit, the staff encourages you to speak up so they can address your concerns in real time. This commitment to customer service highlights their dedication to providing a pleasant dining experience.

Conclusion

With over six decades of history, Docamar stands out as a must-visit destination for anyone in Madrid. Whether you're a local or just passing through, indulging in their legendary patatas bravas and soaking in the neighborhood ambiance makes for a delightful experience.
