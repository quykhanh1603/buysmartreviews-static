A Commitment to Quality and Community

Based in Lancaster, Pennsylvania, Square One Coffee Roasters has been a pillar of the local coffee scene since 2007. This award-winning micro-roaster not only focuses on crafting exceptional coffee but also emphasizes the importance of ethical sourcing and community engagement. Each cup reflects the company's dedication to quality, sustainability, and respect for everyone involved in the coffee journey—from farmers to baristas.

Specialty Coffee Offerings

At Square One Coffee, each batch is meticulously roasted, ensuring that the unique flavors of the coffee beans shine through. The roastery prides itself on sourcing coffee that is responsibly farmed and sustainably traded. Customers can explore a diverse range of coffees through their online shop, which offers convenient delivery right to your doorstep.

For those who prefer to enjoy their coffee fresh, Square One's café welcomes visitors with a warm atmosphere. The café serves a variety of hand-brewed coffees, espresso drinks, and even seasonal espresso cocktails that are crafted with care and expertise. Whether you’re a coffee aficionado or just looking for a great cup to start your day, the café's offerings are designed to delight every palate.

Wholesale Services for Businesses

Understanding the needs of local businesses, Square One Coffee also provides wholesale services, ensuring that coffee served in cafes and restaurants is of the highest caliber. They offer comprehensive barista training and technical support to help their partners succeed. This commitment to quality service and education is a testament to Square One's dedication to fostering a supportive coffee community.

Convenient Pickup and Shipping Options

Square One Coffee offers a local pickup option for those who want to grab their orders directly from the café. Located at 145 North Duke Street, Lancaster, PA, customers can pick up their orders during café hours. Orders placed before 5 PM are processed the same day, while shipping typically occurs within two business days, making it easy to receive your coffee promptly.

It's important to note that shipping times may be affected by seasonal weather, promotional events, or public holidays, so allowing for some extra time during these periods is wise.

Educational Opportunities

The passion for coffee at Square One extends beyond just selling it. The company offers training classes aimed at enhancing coffee skills for both individuals and professionals alike. With several Specialty Coffee Association Certified Lead Instructors on staff, customers can expect to learn from some of the best in the industry. This educational focus not only enriches the coffee community but also elevates the overall coffee experience for everyone involved.

Conclusion

In summary, Square One Coffee Roasters is not just a place to buy coffee; it's a hub for coffee lovers and professionals alike. Their unwavering commitment to quality and ethics, alongside educational opportunities and community engagement, makes them a standout choice in the coffee industry. Whether you're looking for a daily brew or interested in developing your coffee skills, Square One has something to offer for everyone.
