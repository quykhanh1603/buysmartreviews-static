Pottery Barn is a well-known home furnishings retailer, part of Williams-Sonoma Inc. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Pottery Barn is a legitimate, long-established brand, but there’s a striking gap between its formal BBB rating and its day-to-day customer sentiment on Trustpilot — worth understanding before ordering.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 1.2 out of 5 from over 2,000 reviews.
  • BBB: A+ rating, though not BBB accredited.

Recurring themes

Negative: canceled orders, delayed shipments, unresponsive customer service, and slow refunds are the most consistently cited issues — along with billing problems like duplicate charges.

For bedding/textile items specifically, some reviewers also report shrinking, fading, or wear issues faster than expected for the price point.

Before you order

  • Track your order confirmation and any billing charges closely, given the frequency of duplicate-charge complaints.
  • Pay by credit card for dispute protection if a refund is delayed.

Bottom line

Pottery Barn is a legitimate, established retailer, but the very low recent Trustpilot score suggests real, ongoing service friction. Extra documentation and payment protection are reasonable precautions.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
