Uniqlo is a Japanese casual-wear retailer known for basics and technology-driven fabrics (like Heattech), with a growing US store and online presence. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Uniqlo is a legitimate, large international retailer. Ratings vary noticeably by platform, with in-store experience generally rated better than the online ordering process.

What customer ratings show

  • Yelp: around 3.4 out of 5 from over 4,100 reviews.
  • Reviews.io: around 1.7 out of 5 from a smaller sample — a notably wide gap from the Yelp score.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Positive: in-store shoppers frequently rate the experience well, consistent with the brand’s broader reputation for quality basics.

Negative: online order complaints center on shipping delays, refund processing time, and customer service responsiveness.

Before you order

  • If a local Uniqlo store is available, consider buying in person given the gap between in-store and online review sentiment.
  • For online orders, track shipping closely and follow up early if a delay seems to be happening.

Bottom line

Uniqlo is a legitimate, well-regarded brand, particularly in-store. Online order fulfillment appears to be the weaker link based on available reviews.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
