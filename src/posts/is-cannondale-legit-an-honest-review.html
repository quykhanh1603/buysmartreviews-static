History of Cannondale

Founded in 1971, Cannondale has established itself as a prominent American bicycle manufacturer and is now part of Cycling Sports Group. This long-standing brand has garnered a loyal following among cycling enthusiasts.

Customer Feedback Overview

While Cannondale has a solid reputation for quality, the feedback from customers is mixed. Notably, the Better Business Bureau (BBB) rates Cycling Sports Group with an F, citing numerous unanswered complaints. On Trustpilot, sentiments vary significantly, often depending on the region.

What People Are Saying

  • Positive Comments: Many users appreciate the quality of Cannondale bikes, often highlighting the exceptional ride feel and overall performance.
  • Common Complaints: A recurring issue is the lack of responsiveness from customer support, especially regarding warranty claims and product recalls.

Important Tips for Buyers

If you choose to purchase a Cannondale bike, consider these precautions:

  • Make sure to register your bike right after the purchase. This ensures you can be contacted in case of a safety recall.
  • Keep all receipts and documentation related to the purchase and any service visits.

Final Thoughts

While Cannondale is recognized as a credible and historic name in cycling with a focus on quality, potential buyers should be mindful of the brand's customer service shortcomings as reflected in its BBB records. Being vigilant about registration and maintaining documentation can make a difference in your ownership experience.
