Cannondale is a long-established American bicycle manufacturer, founded in 1971, now part of Cycling Sports Group. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Cannondale is a legitimate, historic manufacturer, but its BBB record is notably weak, and combined with mixed Trustpilot sentiment, warranty and recall handling is worth extra attention.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: an F rating for Cycling Sports Group (the operating company), with multiple unanswered complaints.
  • Trustpilot: mixed, ranging by regional site.

Recurring themes

Positive: bike quality and ride feel draw genuine praise from satisfied customers.

Negative: customer support responsiveness, particularly around product recalls and warranty claims, is the clearest complaint pattern.

Before you order

  • Register your bike promptly after purchase so you’re reachable in the event of a safety recall.
  • Keep all documentation from your purchase and any dealer service visits.

Bottom line

Cannondale is a legitimate, historic manufacturer with well-regarded bikes, but its weak BBB complaint-response record means being proactive about registration and documentation is a reasonable precaution.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
