Overview of Ellio's Pizza

Ellio's Pizza has been a staple in frozen pizza for over 60 years, known for its unique rectangular shape that fits perfectly on lunch trays. Founded in 1963 and now a certified women-owned business, Ellio's continues to delight fans with its classic flavors, making it a nostalgic favorite for many families. The brand is committed to maintaining the quality of its products, which include original crust pizzas and the recently launched Sicilian style pizzas.

Product Offerings

Ellio's offers a range of products that cater to various tastes:

  • Original Crust Pizza: This classic favorite is made with 100% real mozzarella cheese and features crispy edges.
  • Sicilian Style Pizza: With 33% more cheese and a thicker crust, this option is perfect for those craving an extra cheesy experience.
  • Breadsticks: These crispy-on-the-outside, soft-on-the-inside breadsticks pair well with pizza or can be enjoyed as a standalone snack.

Cooking Instructions for Optimal Enjoyment

To ensure you enjoy your Ellio's pizza at its best, follow these recommended cooking times:

  • For a crisper crust: Bake directly on the oven rack at 425°F for 11-12 minutes.
  • For a softer crust: Place on a nonstick baking sheet and bake for 13-14 minutes.

These simple instructions ensure that your pizza comes out perfectly every time, whether you use a conventional oven, toaster oven, or air fryer.

Where to Find Ellio's Pizza

Ellio's pizzas are widely available throughout the United States. To locate the nearest store carrying Ellio's products, you can use their Store Locator. If you can’t find it in your local grocery store, you can always request that they stock it.

Health and Dietary Information

Ellio's is committed to ensuring that its pizzas cater to various dietary preferences. The pizzas are produced in a facility that is peanut-free, tree nut-free, and sesame-free. However, it's important to note that the pizzas are not gluten-free, as they contain wheat flour in the crust. For vegetarians, the original crust cheese pizza contains no animal-derived ingredients apart from milk.

Conclusion

Ellio's Pizza combines nostalgia with quality, making it a beloved choice for many. With its classic flavors and easy availability, it's a great option for family meals or gatherings. Whether you're revisiting a childhood favorite or introducing it to your kids, Ellio's Pizza continues to be a delicious choice for pizza lovers everywhere.
