Charlotte Tilbury is a prestige British beauty brand known for its makeup and skincare lines, sold direct and through major retailers. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Charlotte Tilbury has one of the stronger review records of any beauty brand we’ve checked — a legitimate, well-regarded company by a wide margin.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4 out of 5 from over 20,000 reviews.

Recurring themes

Positive: customer service responsiveness and product quality are consistently praised across a very large review sample.

Negative: most negative reviews specifically call out third-party shipping carriers (rather than Charlotte Tilbury itself), with the company reportedly resolving these issues quickly when they come up.

Before you order

  • If a shipping issue occurs, reach out to Charlotte Tilbury’s own support rather than the carrier, since reviewers report faster resolution that way.

Bottom line

Charlotte Tilbury has an exceptionally strong, high-volume review record. One of the more trustworthy beauty brands available.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
