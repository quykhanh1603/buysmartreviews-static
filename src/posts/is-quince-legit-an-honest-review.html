Quince is a direct-to-consumer clothing brand known for selling higher-end materials (cashmere, mulberry silk, full-grain leather) at lower prices by skipping traditional retail markup. Here’s an honest look at whether it holds up.

Quick verdict

Quince appears to be a legitimate, well-regarded clothing brand with genuinely good material quality for the price. Sizing consistency and customer service response time are the main things to watch.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 3,700 reviews, generally positive.
  • BBB: one reviewer publicly alleged that negative reviews don’t always get published on some platforms — we can’t verify this claim independently, but we think it’s worth mentioning so you can weigh it yourself.

Recurring themes

Positive: the most consistent praise is around material quality relative to price — customers highlight the cashmere and silk items in particular as good value versus comparable pieces from traditional retailers.

Negative: the most common complaints are inconsistent sizing between different item types, some durability concerns after repeated washing, and mixed customer service response times — some customers report smooth returns while others describe delivery delays or slow replies.

Before you order

  • Check the specific sizing chart for each item type rather than assuming your usual size — reviewers note sizing varies by category.
  • Follow the exact care instructions for natural-fiber items (cashmere, silk) to reduce the durability issues some reviewers mention.
  • Order early if you need something by a specific date, given mixed reports on shipping speed.

Bottom line

Quince looks like a legitimate brand with real material-quality advantages over comparable traditional retail prices. Pay close attention to per-item sizing guidance and build in shipping buffer time.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
