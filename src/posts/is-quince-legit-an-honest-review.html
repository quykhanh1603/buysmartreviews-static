The Trustworthiness of Quince

Quince presents itself as a trustworthy option for premium clothing but has some customer feedback worth considering. They offer high-quality items crafted from luxury materials like cashmere and mulberry silk, often at more affordable prices than traditional retail. Many customers praise the brand for its value, especially concerning product quality, but some have raised concerns about sizing inconsistencies and customer service experiences.

Customer Sentiment and Concerns

According to over 3,700 reviews on Trustpilot, Quince generally enjoys positive feedback from its customers. However, reviews on the Better Business Bureau (BBB) indicate that some negative experiences may not be published, which raises some potential red flags. While many find great value in the material quality, including cashmere and silk, others report issues such as inconsistent sizing, durability problems after washing, and varied experiences with customer service. While some users appreciate the ease of returns, there are reports of delivery delays and slow response times. To ensure a good shopping experience, it's advisable to consult the sizing chart for each product and follow care instructions closely to avoid the durability issues noted by some reviews.

The ratings and insights shared here reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
