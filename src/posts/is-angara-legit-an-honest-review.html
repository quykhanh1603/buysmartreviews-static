Angara is an online jeweler specializing in colored gemstones and made-to-order pieces, family-run since 2005. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Angara stands out with one of the strongest review records of any jeweler we’ve checked — a legitimate, well-regarded company by a wide margin.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4.6–4.9 out of 5 from over 2,400 reviews.
  • BBB: A+ rating, accredited.

Recurring themes

Positive: customer service and design versatility (Angara’s made-to-order model covers a wide range of gemstone and setting combinations) are consistently praised.

Negative: some reviewers mention delayed delivery, which makes sense given the made-to-order production model — not the same as a ready-stock retailer.

Before you order

  • Ask for an estimated production/delivery timeline upfront, especially if you need the piece by a specific date, since these are typically made to order rather than shipped from stock.

Bottom line

Angara has one of the strongest, most consistent review records of any jeweler on this list. Just plan for made-to-order lead times rather than expecting off-the-shelf shipping speed.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
