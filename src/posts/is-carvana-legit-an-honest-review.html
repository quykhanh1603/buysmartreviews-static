Carvana is an online used-car retailer known for its car vending machines and fully digital buying process. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Carvana is a legitimate, established company, but customer experience is unusually polarized — buyers tend to report either a smooth, modern purchase or a genuinely frustrating post-sale process.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: reports vary by source, generally in the high-2s to mid-4s out of 5 across 11,000+ reviews — an unusually wide spread that itself signals polarized experiences.
  • BBB: not accredited; rating and complaint volume vary by source, but title/registration delays are a frequently cited, multi-state issue.

Recurring themes

Positive: reviewers who had a smooth experience consistently praise the convenience, transparent pricing, and delivery process.

Negative: the single most-cited complaint by far is delays getting a title or registration processed after purchase — significant enough that it has drawn state-level regulatory attention in multiple states.

Before you buy

  • Ask specifically about expected title/registration timeline for your state before completing a purchase.
  • Keep close track of your temporary tag expiration and follow up proactively rather than waiting to be contacted.

Bottom line

Carvana is a legitimate, large-scale used-car retailer. The purchase experience itself is generally praised, but title/registration delays are common enough that you should ask about timelines upfront.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
