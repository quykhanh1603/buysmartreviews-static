Assessing Carvana's Trustworthiness

Carvana presents a mixed bag when it comes to trustworthiness, with some customers praising the service while others highlight significant issues. Let's dig into the details of customer feedback to get a clearer picture.

Customer Experiences and Feedback

Positive Feedback: Many customers appreciate Carvana for its user-friendly purchasing process, transparent pricing, and reliable vehicle delivery. However, Common Complaints: the most frequent issues revolve around delays related to title and registration, which have drawn the attention of state regulators in several regions.

Online ratings tell a varied story as well. On Trustpilot, Carvana has received over 11,000 reviews, generally sitting between the high-2s to mid-4s out of 5, showcasing the differing experiences customers have had. Similarly, while the BBB has not accredited Carvana, it does report a fluctuating rating and numerous complaints, particularly regarding the slow process for obtaining titles and registrations.

While Carvana is a recognized player in the used-car market with a generally positive purchase experience, prospective buyers should be aware of potential delays. It's wise to inquire about timelines for title and registration before finalizing any order.

Ratings mentioned above are based on publicly available information as of the time of this writing and may change over time.
