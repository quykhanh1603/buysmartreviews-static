Exploring Carvana's Reputation

Carvana has made a name for itself as an innovative online used-car retailer, featuring unique car vending machines and a fully digital buying process. But how legit is it when it comes to actual customer experiences?

Customer Feedback Insights

  • Trustpilot: With over 11,000 reviews, ratings generally hover between the high-2s to mid-4s out of 5, highlighting the stark contrast in customer experiences.
  • BBB: While not accredited, the BBB reports a varying rating and volume of complaints, with many users noting significant delays in title and registration across multiple states.

What Customers Are Saying

Positive Feedback: Those who have had a seamless experience often commend Carvana for its user-friendly process, clear pricing, and reliable delivery.

Common Complaints: The most frequent issue raised involves delays in obtaining title and registration, a problem serious enough to attract attention from state regulators in various areas.

Tips for Potential Buyers

  • Prior to completing your purchase, inquire specifically about the expected timeline for title and registration in your state.
  • Monitor the expiration date of your temporary tag closely, and be proactive in following up to avoid issues.

Final Thoughts

While Carvana is a recognized player in the used-car market with a generally positive purchase experience, prospective buyers should be aware of potential delays in title and registration. It’s wise to ask about these timelines before finalizing your order.

Ratings mentioned above are based on publicly available information as of the time of this writing and may change over time.
