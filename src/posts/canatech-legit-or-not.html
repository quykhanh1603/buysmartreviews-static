Understanding CanaTech and Its Offerings

CanaTech, under the banner of MSFERNANDES Consultoria Agrícola, specializes in providing comprehensive agricultural consulting services tailored for producers and sugarcane mills, as well as national and multinational companies involved in grain production. With a commitment to improving the Brazilian agricultural landscape, CanaTech emphasizes ethical practices and respect for the environment.

Expertise and Services

Michel Fernandes, the founder of MSFERNANDES, brings a wealth of knowledge and experience to the table, having worked in the agricultural sector since 2004. His qualifications include a degree in Agronomic Engineering and advanced studies in Plant Protection and Agroindustry. The consulting firm provides a range of services:

  • Complete recommendations for soil preparation and crop management, including the selection of planting materials and growth monitoring.
  • Guidance on the application of various agricultural chemicals (herbicides, insecticides, fungicides, etc.) to support the maturation and harvesting of sugarcane.
  • Research development and assistance in creating operational protocols for sugarcane mills.
  • Evaluation of production improvement areas, including cultural rotation practices.

Additionally, CanaTech supports national and multinational companies in developing experimental protocols and sharing new technologies through lectures and presentations.

CanaTech Event: A Key Industry Initiative

The CanaTech event, founded by Michel Fernandes in 2017, serves as a significant technical forum for the sugar-energy sector in Minas Gerais. Over the years, it has become a platform where industry leaders gather to discuss advancements and share ideas. With increasing participation from important figures in the field, the event highlights the latest in agricultural technology and practices.

Engaging with the Community

CanaTech also aims to reach a broader audience through digital platforms. The CanaTech Agro YouTube channel, hosted by Michel Fernandes, provides valuable insights and practical solutions for farmers, ensuring that even producers not present at the events can access innovative agricultural knowledge.
