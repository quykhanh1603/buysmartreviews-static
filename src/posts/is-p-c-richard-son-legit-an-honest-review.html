P.C. Richard & Son is a Northeast US appliance and electronics retailer with a long operating history. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

P.C. Richard & Son is a legitimate, BBB-accredited, long-established retailer, but review platforms show a consistent pattern of complaints around installation and warranty service worth knowing.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: accredited since 2010.
  • Trustpilot: over 220 reviews, mixed.

Recurring themes

Positive: quick delivery is cited favorably by a number of reviewers.

Negative: installation damage (including scratched appliances), warranty disputes, and reported repair delays of a month or more are the most consistent complaints.

Before you order

  • Inspect your appliance’s finish carefully at delivery/installation and document any scratches or damage immediately.
  • Ask about typical repair turnaround time for your specific appliance type before relying on the extended warranty for a time-sensitive need.

Bottom line

P.C. Richard & Son is a legitimate, established retailer. Careful inspection at installation and realistic expectations about warranty repair timing are worth planning for.
