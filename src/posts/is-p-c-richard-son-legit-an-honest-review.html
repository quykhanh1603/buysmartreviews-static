Overview of P.C. Richard & Son

P.C. Richard & Son is a well-known retail chain specializing in electronics and appliances, operating primarily in the Northeastern United States. With a history dating back to 1970, the business has built a reputation for providing a variety of appliances, electronics, and other home goods. However, customer experiences have been mixed, as indicated by recent reviews.

Product Offerings

The store offers an extensive range of products, including:

  • Major Appliances: Refrigerators, washers, dryers, and cooking appliances.
  • Electronics: TVs, audio systems, and computers.
  • Home Goods: Furniture, kitchenware, and small appliances.

While P.C. Richard & Son has a wide selection, customers have voiced concerns about the reliability of deliveries and the quality of customer service.

Customer Feedback and Trust Signals

  • Unhelpful customer service representatives and rude communication.
  • Delays in appliance repairs and installation services.
  • Delivery problems, including damaged items and incomplete setups.

Despite these concerns, some customers have praised their in-store experiences, noting that certain sales associates were friendly, knowledgeable, and willing to provide detailed assistance.
