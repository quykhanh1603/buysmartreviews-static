Trustpilot Reviews

With over 220 reviews on Trustpilot, P.C. Richard & Son has garnered a mix of customer experiences overall. Delving deeper, you’ll find both commendations and criticisms that shape their reputation.

  • BBB Status: Accredited since 2010.
  • Trustpilot Ratings: Over 220 reviews, reflecting a mixed bag of customer experiences.

Customer Experiences

Many customers appreciate the swift delivery of their purchases, which is often highlighted positively. However, on the flip side, a significant number of complaints revolve around installation issues, such as damage to appliances during setup, warranty disputes, and repair delays that can stretch over a month.

Key Takeaways

  • Be diligent when inspecting your appliances upon delivery and installation. Document any scratches or damage right away.
  • Inquire about the average repair time specific to your appliance to ensure your warranty meets your needs.
