Foot Locker is one of the largest sneaker and athletic footwear retailers in the US. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Foot Locker is a legitimate, large, established retailer, but its independent review record is notably weak across every platform we checked — worth taking seriously before ordering online.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: a D- rating, not accredited, citing failure to respond to over 300 complaints.
  • Trustpilot: around 1.6 out of 5 from over 280 reviews.
  • Yelp: around 2.6 out of 5 from over 2,500 reviews.

Recurring themes

Negative: delayed orders are the most cited complaint, along with very long customer service hold times (some reviewers report over an hour).

Before you order

  • For time-sensitive purchases (like a sneaker release), consider in-store pickup rather than shipping given the reported delivery delay pattern.
  • Use email or chat rather than phone if hold times are a concern, and document all communication.

Bottom line

Foot Locker is a legitimate, major retailer, but its review record is consistently weak across BBB, Trustpilot, and Yelp. Plan for possible delays, especially with online orders.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
