Understanding Foot Locker's Reputation

As one of the largest sneaker and athletic footwear retailers in the US, Foot Locker holds a significant position in the market. However, a dive into public reviews reveals some concerning trends.

Customer Ratings Breakdown

  • BBB: Foot Locker received a D- rating and is not accredited, primarily due to its failure to address over 300 customer complaints.
  • Trustpilot: The company has an average rating of around 1.6 out of 5 based on over 280 reviews, indicating widespread dissatisfaction.
  • Yelp: With approximately 2.6 out of 5 from over 2,500 reviews, the feedback is similarly unfavorable.

Common Customer Complaints

One of the most frequent issues reported by customers is delayed orders. Many also express frustration over long wait times when trying to reach customer service, with some waiting over an hour.

What to Consider Before Buying

  • If you need products quickly, especially during sneaker releases, opting for in-store pickup might be a better choice to avoid shipping delays.
  • For customer service queries, consider using email or chat instead of phone calls to reduce wait times, and make sure to keep a record of all your communications.

Final Thoughts

While Foot Locker is a well-established retailer, the feedback from customers suggests significant areas for improvement. Be mindful of potential delays with online orders.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
