Understanding Hertz Car Sales

As the used-vehicle retail branch of the established Hertz rental car brand, Hertz Car Sales aims to provide customers with quality pre-owned vehicles. However, a closer examination reveals a range of consumer experiences that warrant consideration before purchasing.

Customer Feedback Overview

  • BBB: The company is not accredited, which is common for many businesses, as accreditation requires an opt-in fee.
  • Trustpilot: Hertz Car Sales has received a limited number of reviews specific to its vehicle sales, some of which highlight noteworthy complaints.

Negative aspects: Complaints often mention advertised features missing from vehicles upon delivery, discrepancies regarding the accuracy or timing of pre-sale vehicle inspections, and billing disputes according to BBB reports.

Important Tips for Buyers

  • Ensure that any advertised features (like extended warranties or accessories) are documented in your bill of sale, not just confirmed verbally.
  • Request the inspection report date and verify that it accurately reflects the vehicle's current condition.
  • Meticulously review your final bill against the original quote before signing any agreements.

Final Thoughts

While Hertz Car Sales operates legitimately as part of a reputable rental car company, the patterns of complaints regarding inclusions and billing accuracy suggest that customers should take extra care to verify details in writing prior to completing a purchase.
