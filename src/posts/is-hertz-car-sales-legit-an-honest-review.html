Hertz Car Sales is the used-vehicle retail arm of the Hertz rental car company. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Hertz Car Sales is a legitimate division of a major, established rental company, but the review record includes enough specific complaints about sales practices that extra diligence is warranted.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: not accredited (not unusual for many businesses, since accreditation is a paid, opt-in program).
  • Trustpilot: a small number of reviews specific to the car-sales division, with several detailing specific complaints.

Recurring themes

Negative: reported complaints include advertised inclusions not actually being present on delivered vehicles, disagreements over the accuracy or timing of the pre-sale vehicle inspection report, and, per BBB complaints, disputes over billing.

Before you buy

  • Get any advertised inclusions (extended warranty, accessories, etc.) confirmed in writing on your bill of sale, not just verbally.
  • Ask for the date of the vehicle’s inspection report and confirm it reflects the car’s current condition, not an outdated report.
  • Review your final bill line-by-line against what was quoted before signing.

Bottom line

Hertz Car Sales is a legitimate used-car retailer, but the complaint pattern around inclusions and billing accuracy means it’s worth double-checking every detail in writing before you finalize a purchase.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
