Understanding Hertz Car Sales

As a branch of the established Hertz rental car brand, Hertz Car Sales offers a selection of used vehicles intended to meet buyer needs. However, a closer look at consumer feedback reveals mixed experiences that potential buyers should be aware of.

Customer Feedback Overview

Reviews on key platforms highlight some concerns. Although Hertz Car Sales isn't accredited by the Better Business Bureau, which is typical for many businesses since accreditation involves a fee, customer experiences vary widely. On Trustpilot, the company has received limited reviews with some notable complaints regarding vehicle sales. Many customers have reported issues such as missing advertised features upon delivery, inaccuracies in pre-sale vehicle inspections, and billing disputes.

Given these reported problems, it becomes essential for buyers to verify every detail in writing before finalizing a purchase. While Hertz Car Sales is part of a reputable brand, the patterns of complaints suggest a cautious approach is prudent.
