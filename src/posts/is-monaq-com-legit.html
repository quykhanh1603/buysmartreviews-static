Overview of Mona Frick's Literary Work

Mona Frick is a vibrant author based in Stuttgart, known for her engaging novels that mix humor with intriguing crime stories. Her series, "Schäfers Ermittlungen," which follows the adventures of the ex-police officer Jürgen Schäfer, has captivated readers with its witty narrative and relatable characters. With a total of twelve installments, the series invites readers to experience crime in the charming backdrop of Swabia.

What You Can Expect from Mona Frick

Visitors to monaq.com can find a wealth of content related to Frick's work, including:

  • Signed paperback copies of her books, which are available upon request. Mona even offers to personalize these for readers.
  • Exclusive summer promotions, where readers can obtain themed packages that include her works along with other goodies, like bath ducks, perfect for your summer relaxation.
  • The complete "Schäfers Ermittlungen" series, available in various formats including E-Book, paperback, and even audiobooks for those who prefer to listen.

Her latest crime novel, "Ein letzter Limoncello," marks the conclusion of the Schäfer series, providing a fitting farewell to the beloved character as he embarks on his much-deserved retirement.

Engagement and Community

Mona actively interacts with her readers and fans, often hosting discussions and events that enhance the reading experience. Reviews from platforms like Lovelybooks have highlighted her engaging narrative style, bringing warmth and humor to her stories. This connection with her audience is a significant aspect of her work, as evidenced by her gratitude expressed towards readers who participate in her book discussions.

Future Projects and Events

Excitingly, Mona’s work is not limited to writing books! She is also involved in theatrical adaptations of her stories. Her fairy tale, "Prinzessin Samira und die verzauberte Dattel," will be staged in Stuttgart, showcasing her versatility as an author and storyteller. Additionally, this story is available as an illustrated book and audiobook, enriching the experience for younger audiences.
