Free People is a bohemian-style women’s clothing brand, part of the same parent company as Anthropologie and Urban Outfitters. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Free People is a legitimate, well-known brand, but recent review sentiment leans negative, concentrated in a few specific order-fulfillment areas.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: most recent reviewers report being let down by their experience.
  • Yelp: around 3.2 out of 5 from over 1,600 reviews.

Recurring themes

Negative: order cancellations, missing items from multi-item orders, and delivery/refund handling issues are the most cited complaints.

Before you order

  • Confirm your order contents against the confirmation email once it ships, and report discrepancies immediately.
  • Track refund status proactively rather than assuming it will process automatically within the stated window.

Bottom line

Free People is a legitimate, recognizable brand, but recent reviews suggest real order-fulfillment friction. Double-check your shipment and stay on top of refund status.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
