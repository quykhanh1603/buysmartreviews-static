Overview of La-Z-Boy

La-Z-Boy has forged a strong identity in the furniture manufacturing scene, particularly for its iconic recliners and upholstered pieces. While the brand holds a notoriously trusted position, delving deeper into customer reviews uncovers a mix of satisfaction and concern that could sway your buying decision.

Customer Feedback Insights

An analysis of customer feedback paints a varied picture of La-Z-Boy's service. On Trustpilot, about 795 reviews highlight a notable trend of dissatisfaction, particularly aimed at customer service experiences. Meanwhile, its profile with the Better Business Bureau shows mixed ratings, where several unresolved complaints linger, indicating potential gaps in customer support and service that could affect future buyers.

Common Customer Complaints

Customer grievances frequently spotlight the “Guardian” protection plan, which many find difficult to navigate, leading to frustration when it comes to filing claims. There are also numerous reports of warranty claims being declined for what are often considered normal wear and tear, alongside issues like bonded leather peeling prematurely and delivery scheduling complications. On the flip side, many customers express satisfaction with their recliners and upholstered furniture when they receive products without warranty issues, showing that the items themselves can often exceed expectations.

Purchasing Considerations

For anyone considering a purchase, it's crucial to thoroughly assess the details of any extended warranty or protection plan. Understanding what is classified as “normal wear and tear” could save you from disappointment down the line. Additionally, it’s wise to compare bonded leather products with genuine leather or fabric options, especially given the noted concerns surrounding peeling. As La-Z-Boy continues to be a prominent name in furniture, prospective customers should approach their decision with a measured perspective, balancing the brand's reputation against the complaints regarding warranties and service.

The ratings and reviews cited reflect publicly accessible information at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
