Overview of La-Z-Boy

La-Z-Boy has established itself as a trusted name in furniture manufacturing, particularly known for its recliners and upholstered pieces. However, a closer examination of customer reviews reveals some areas of concern that may influence your buying decision.

Customer Feedback Insights

  • Trustpilot: Approximately 795 reviews, with a notable trend of dissatisfaction related to customer service.
  • BBB: Ratings are mixed, highlighting several unresolved complaints on at least one profile.

Common Customer Complaints

Negative Aspects: Many customers voice frustrations with the “Guardian” protection plan, describing it as challenging to navigate. Other recurring issues include warranty claims being denied for what are deemed normal wear and tear, bonded leather furniture experiencing premature peeling, and problems with delivery scheduling.

Positive Notes: On a brighter note, when customers receive their recliners and upholstered furniture without warranty issues, the products themselves tend to receive favorable reviews.

Purchasing Considerations

  • Thoroughly review the details of any extended warranty or protection plan before committing, especially to understand what constitutes “normal wear and tear.”
  • When considering bonded leather products, weigh them against genuine leather or fabric options, particularly given the reported peeling issues.

Final Thoughts

While La-Z-Boy is a long-standing and reputable furniture brand, prospective customers should approach it with cautious optimism. The products typically enjoy a good reputation, but the notable complaints regarding warranties suggest that careful consideration of the terms is essential.

The ratings and reviews cited reflect publicly accessible information at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
