Petco is one of the largest pet supply and services retailers in the US, with physical stores offering grooming and veterinary services alongside products. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Petco is a legitimate, large retailer, but its review record shows consistent dissatisfaction concentrated in grooming services and online order fulfillment.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 1,670 reviews, skewing negative.
  • SmartCustomer: around 2.1 out of 5 from 252 reviews.

Recurring themes

Negative: grooming service complaints (including reports of health concerns for pets during service), long wait times, unhelpful staff in some locations, and online order/return issues are the most cited problems. Some reviewers also reported receiving unhealthy live animals.

Before you order or book a service

  • For grooming, ask about the groomer’s specific experience with your pet’s breed/coat type before booking.
  • For live animals specifically, inspect the animal closely in-store before purchase.

Bottom line

Petco is a legitimate, major retailer, but grooming and live-animal complaints are common enough that extra caution in those specific areas is worthwhile.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
