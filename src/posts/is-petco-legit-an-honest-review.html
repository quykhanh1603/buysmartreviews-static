What's the deal with Petco's reputation?

As a big name in the pet supply industry, Petco has a lot of physical stores in the U.S., and they offer services like grooming and veterinary care. But when you look closer, the feedback from customers paints a picture that's a bit concerning.

What are customers really saying?

  • Trustpilot: Over 1,670 reviews show that many experiences lean towards the negative side.
  • SmartCustomer: The average rating is around 2.1 out of 5 based on 252 reviews.

What should you watch out for?

Common issues: Customers often share frustrations about grooming services, voicing worries about their pets' well-being during appointments, facing long wait times, and encountering unhelpful staff members at some locations. There are also notable complaints about online orders and returns, plus reports of unhealthy live animals being sold.

Make sure to check the groomer's experience with your pet's breed before booking and inspect live animals in-store before making a purchase.

Keep in mind that the ratings and information provided here are based on publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
