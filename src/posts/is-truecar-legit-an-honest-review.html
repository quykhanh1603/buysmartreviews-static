TrueCar is an online platform that shows estimated fair pricing and connects buyers with a network of certified dealers — it’s important to understand it’s a referral service, not a direct car seller. Here’s an honest look based on public data.

Quick verdict

TrueCar is a legitimate, established company (operating since 2005) with a strong BBB rating. The main thing to understand is what it actually does: it’s a lead-generation and price-comparison layer on top of dealer networks, not a car dealer itself.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: A+ rating, working with over 16,000 certified dealers.
  • Trustpilot: mixed reviews.

Recurring themes

Negative: the most common complaints are that the actual price offered by a dealer often comes in lower than the number displayed on TrueCar’s site, and that submitting your information triggers a wave of calls, texts, and emails from multiple dealerships.

Positive: buyers who go in understanding it’s a comparison/referral tool (not a direct seller) generally find the price transparency useful as a starting point for negotiation.

Before you use it

  • Treat the displayed price as a starting estimate, not a guaranteed final price.
  • Consider using a secondary contact method (like a dedicated email) to limit the volume of dealer outreach after submitting your info.

Bottom line

TrueCar is a legitimate price-comparison and dealer-referral service, not a car seller itself. Useful as a starting reference point, less useful if you expect the listed price to be the final one.
