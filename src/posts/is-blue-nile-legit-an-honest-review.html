Blue Nile is one of the oldest and best-known online jewelers in the US, specializing in certified diamonds and custom engagement rings. Here’s what the publicly available data says about buying from them.

Customer Feedback Highlights

  • Trustpilot: rated around 4 out of 5 across more than 1,500 reviews.
  • Headquartered in Seattle, WA; all diamonds sold are independently certified.

Strengths and Challenges

Strengths: Many customers appreciate that items arrive true to their online descriptions, and they often praise the live-chat support, particularly for sizing inquiries. Additionally, the 30-day return policy is frequently noted as being hassle-free. Since sales staff are paid a salary instead of commissions, shoppers often feel less pressure to purchase more expensive options.

Challenges: On the downside, turnaround times for repairs have been problematic for some customers, with reports of jewelry repairs taking months. A few customers also experienced delays with orders during significant occasions like anniversaries or proposals.

Things to Consider Before Ordering

  • To avoid issues, it’s wise to place your order well in advance, especially if you have a specific date in mind (such as a proposal). Always confirm the estimated delivery date in writing.
  • Don’t forget to keep the independent grading certificate that comes with certified stones—this can be essential for insurance and resale.
  • For any repairs, ask for a time estimate upfront and make sure to check in regularly for updates.

Final Thoughts

Blue Nile stands out as a legitimate, long-standing retailer that caters well to those who plan ahead. If you’re looking for time-sensitive purchases, such as an engagement ring for a specific date, it's advisable to add extra buffer time considering the reported delays.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
