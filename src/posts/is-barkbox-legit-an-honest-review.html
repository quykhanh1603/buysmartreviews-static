BarkBox is a monthly subscription box for dog toys and treats, operated by the publicly traded company BARK, Inc. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

BarkBox is a legitimate, publicly traded company with a largely positive review record, though subscription cancellation friction is a real and recurring complaint.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4.4 out of 5 from over 8,000 reviews.
  • BBB: A+ rating.

Recurring themes

Positive: toy and treat quality plus the convenience of monthly delivery are consistently praised.

Negative: the most common complaint pattern involves auto-renewal and cancellation friction — some customers report difficulty canceling on time before the next billing cycle — along with occasional reports of damaged items.

Before you order

  • Note your renewal date and set a reminder if you’re considering canceling, since auto-renewal timing is the main complaint.
  • Contact BBB-listed support channels if a cancellation request isn’t honored, since the company is reported to respond well there.

Bottom line

BarkBox is a legitimate, well-reviewed subscription service. The main thing to manage is your renewal date if you plan to cancel.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
