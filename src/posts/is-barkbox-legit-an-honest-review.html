Is BarkBox a Good Choice for You?

If you're a dog owner thinking about trying out BarkBox, you're in for a treat—literally! This monthly subscription service delivers an array of toys and treats straight to your door. It’s managed by BARK, Inc., a publicly traded company that's built a solid reputation in the pet community.

How Do Customers Rate Their Experience?

  • Trustpilot: About 4.4 out of 5 stars with over 8,000 reviews.
  • BBB: An impressive A+ rating, which shows the company takes customer service seriously.

What Do Current Subscribers Think?

Positive feedback: Many dog owners love the quality of the toys and treats they receive each month, finding it a convenient way to keep their pups entertained and happy.

Negative feedback: On the flip side, issues with canceling subscriptions have been reported. Some customers have faced challenges in managing their subscriptions, leading to unexpected charges. There have also been a few complaints about damaged items.

Just a tip: if you’re considering the subscription, keep an eye on your renewal dates and set a reminder to avoid any surprises. And remember, if you do encounter problems, reaching out through the BBB-listed support can often lead to quick resolutions.

The ratings mentioned reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
