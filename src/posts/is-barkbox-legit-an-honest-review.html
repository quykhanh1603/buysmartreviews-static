BarkBox Overview

BarkBox is a monthly subscription service dedicated to dog owners, delivering a variety of toys and treats right to your door. Operated by BARK, Inc., a publicly traded company, it has garnered a substantial following based on public feedback.

Customer Ratings Insights

  • Trustpilot: Approximately 4.4 out of 5 based on over 8,000 reviews.
  • BBB: Holds an impressive A+ rating.

What Customers Are Saying

Positive feedback: Many users rave about the quality of the toys and treats, as well as the convenience of having them delivered monthly.

Negative feedback: A common concern is the complexity surrounding subscription cancellations. Numerous customers have reported challenges in canceling their subscriptions on time, which can lead to unintended charges, alongside occasional complaints regarding damaged products.

Things to Consider Before Subscribing

  • If you think you might want to cancel, keep a close eye on your renewal date and set a reminder.
  • For issues with cancellation, contacting the BBB-listed support channels is often met with prompt responses from the company.

Final Thoughts

Overall, BarkBox is a legitimate subscription service with generally favorable reviews. However, if you intend to cancel, make sure to manage your renewal date carefully.
