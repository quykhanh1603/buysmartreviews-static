Spring Hill Nurseries is a mail-order plant and gardening company. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Spring Hill Nurseries has one of the more consistently weak review records among the plant retailers we’ve checked, concentrated in shipping and refund handling.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: around 1.12 out of 5 from 44 reviews.
  • SmartCustomer: around 1.1 out of 5 from 66 reviews.

Recurring themes

Negative: plants arriving dead, orders stuck on backorder or never shipping, poor communication, and difficulty getting refunds are the most consistently cited issues across multiple independent platforms.

Before you order

  • Pay by credit card so you have a dispute option if an order never ships or a refund is refused.
  • Photograph any dead or damaged plants immediately on arrival to support a claim.

Bottom line

Spring Hill Nurseries has a consistently weak review record across independent platforms. If you order, use payment protection and document everything.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
