Understanding Spring Hill Nurseries

As a mail-order plant and gardening company, Spring Hill Nurseries has some notable concerns raised by its customers. Let’s delve into what the public reviews reveal about this retailer.

Customer Feedback Overview

  • BBB Rating: approximately 1.12 out of 5 based on 44 reviews.
  • SmartCustomer Rating: about 1.1 out of 5 from 66 reviews.

Common Complaints

Many customers report similar issues, including plants arriving dead, orders that either get backordered indefinitely or fail to ship at all, poor communication from customer service, and challenges in obtaining refunds. These themes appear consistently across various independent review platforms.

Tips for Potential Buyers

  • Consider paying with a credit card for added protection in case your order doesn’t arrive or if you face issues with refunds.
  • Immediately photograph any plants that are dead or damaged upon arrival to support your claims.

Final Thoughts

Overall, Spring Hill Nurseries has a concerning reputation based on customer reviews. If you choose to make a purchase, ensure you take precautions like using payment protection and documenting any problems.
