Discovering Findlay Hats

Based in Portland, Oregon, Findlay Hats is a brand that has made a name for itself since its inception in 2013. With a focus on creating hats that are not only stylish but also functional, they have managed to establish a loyal following of hat enthusiasts. The company prides itself on being community-driven, with no outside investors, and has grown into a tight-knit team of ten people.

Standout Features

What sets Findlay Hats apart from other brands in the market? Here are some notable features:

  • Patented Stampede Laces: These are designed to keep your hat securely in place, whether you're skiing, hiking, or just enjoying a windy day. The laces can be tied up or dropped down, providing versatility in both style and function.
  • Hidden Stash Pocket: Each hat includes a cleverly concealed pocket inside the crown, perfect for storing small essentials like cash or keys.
  • Lifetime Warranty: Findlay Hats offers a lifetime warranty on all their products, meaning if anything goes wrong, they will repair or replace your hat at no extra cost.

Weekly Drops and Custom Options

Every Friday at 7 PM Pacific, Findlay Hats releases new limited edition hats, often centered around a theme. Once they sell out, they're gone for good. This creates a sense of urgency and exclusivity among collectors and hat lovers alike.

In addition to their regular offerings, Findlay also provides custom hat options, catering to groups and brands looking to create personalized designs.

Shipping and Return Policies

Findlay Hats ships all orders from Oregon, with free standard shipping on US orders. For standard hats, you can expect your order to arrive within a week, while limited editions may take a little longer — typically around 2 to 3 weeks. They also offer a 30-day money-back guarantee if you're not completely satisfied with your purchase.

Community and Reputation

Findlay Hats has garnered over 14,800 verified reviews, most of which are positive, highlighting the quality and comfort of their hats. Their commitment to building a community around their brand is evident in the large following they have cultivated, with over 300,000 members in their Findlay Force community.

While the brand's limited edition drops can sell out quickly, this strategy has kept the excitement alive and has encouraged a culture of collectors within their fanbase.

Final Thoughts

If you're looking for a hat that combines style, functionality, and community spirit, Findlay Hats is definitely worth considering. With their innovative features and solid warranty, you can feel confident that you're making a smart purchase.
