Serta is one of the largest and longest-running mattress manufacturers in the US, sold through its own site and major retailers. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Serta is a legitimate, massive, long-established manufacturer, but recent reviews for its parent company show significant customer service and warranty-related dissatisfaction.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: ratings vary by source, generally in the 2.4 to 3.6 out of 5 range.
  • BBB: parent company Serta Simmons Bedding holds an F rating (unaccredited), with a customer review score around 1.44 out of 5.

Recurring themes

Negative: warranty coverage disputes are the most common complaint theme, with several reviewers describing claims rejected on technicalities. The brand has also had past product recalls related to federal flammability standards.

Positive: customers who buy through major retailers (rather than dealing with warranty claims directly) more often report satisfaction with comfort and cooling features.

Before you order

  • Read the specific warranty terms carefully, especially exclusions, before you buy.
  • Consider buying through a major retailer with its own return policy as a backup, rather than relying solely on the manufacturer’s warranty process.

Bottom line

Serta is a legitimate, long-established manufacturer, but warranty-related complaints are frequent enough that understanding the fine print — and having a retailer return policy as backup — is a reasonable precaution.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
