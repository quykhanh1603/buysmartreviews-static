Unveiling Extraordinary Live Experiences

House of Live is your gateway to engaging and immersive exhibitions that bring art, history, and culture to life. With a focus on extraordinary live experiences, the venue hosts a variety of exhibitions designed to captivate audiences of all ages.

Engaging Exhibitions

Each exhibition at House of Live is crafted for a memorable experience, ensuring that visitors can step inside art and culture through life-sized installations and interactive exhibits. This approach not only enhances appreciation for the art but also allows for a deeper understanding and connection to the historical and cultural narratives involved.

Current and Upcoming Exhibitions

Among the striking exhibitions is Michelangelo's Sistine Chapel: The Exhibition, where attendees can experience the master's work up close and personal. This breathtaking exhibit, which originates from Rome, is currently touring cities worldwide. Additionally, Princess Diana & The Royals: The Exhibition showcases over 700 rare artifacts, revealing royal intrigue and the untold story of Diana and the Royal Family, exclusively available in Las Vegas.

World-Class Curation

The exhibitions at House of Live are distinguished by their world-class curation, crafted by leading experts in the field. This ensures that every visit offers an unforgettable journey of discovery, appealing to both art enthusiasts and casual visitors seeking enriching experiences.

Global Reach

House of Live operates in major cities across the globe, making it accessible to a diverse audience. Whether you're in Las Vegas or traveling to see one of their touring exhibitions, you'll find a unique cultural experience awaiting you.
