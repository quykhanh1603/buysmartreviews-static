SHEIN is a global fast-fashion retailer known for extremely low prices and a massive, constantly refreshed catalog. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

SHEIN is a legitimate, large international retailer (not a scam), but “legitimate” and “consistent quality” are different questions here — go in with realistic expectations about sizing and material quality at this price point.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4.1 out of 5 from over 265,000 reviews — one of the largest review volumes of any company we’ve covered.
  • BBB: not accredited; SHEIN is based outside the US, which affects how BBB accreditation applies.

Recurring themes

Positive: the sheer scale of reviews suggests most orders go through without major issue, and price-to-selection ratio is the most consistent draw.

Negative: sizing inconsistency between items, material quality not always matching photos, and slower resolution on returns/refunds compared to full-price retailers are the most common specific complaints.

Before you order

  • Check the specific size chart for each item rather than assuming consistent sizing across the catalog.
  • Read recent reviews for the specific item, not just the brand overall, since quality can vary a lot by product.

Bottom line

SHEIN is a legitimate, high-volume retailer. At this price point, treat it as fast fashion: manage expectations on material longevity and check sizing carefully before ordering.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
