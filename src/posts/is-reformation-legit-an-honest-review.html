Overview of Reformation

Reformation is a sustainability-focused women’s clothing brand celebrated for its stylish dresses designed in Los Angeles. With a commitment to creating fashionable yet eco-friendly apparel, it has garnered attention from consumers looking to make more conscious choices.

Customer Insights

According to various customer ratings, including Trustpilot, feedback ranges widely, often falling into the low-to-mid range based on several hundred reviews from different regions. This indicates a mixed reception among shoppers.

What Buyers Are Saying

Positive Notes: Many customers express satisfaction with the aesthetic appeal and overall fit of Reformation's clothing when they find the right size. Additionally, the customer service has received praise for being attentive and effective when addressing issues.

Negative Feedback: The most frequent complaint centers around sizing inconsistency, with some garments running larger or smaller than expected. Customers also mention concerns about the quality of materials, noting that some items feel thin or unlined and may shrink after washing, despite following care instructions.

Tips for Shopping

  • Check reviews specific to the item you’re considering rather than relying solely on overall brand ratings.
  • Adhere closely to care instructions and consider air-drying delicate items to prevent damage.

Final Thoughts

Overall, Reformation is a legitimate brand that offers trendy designs with a commitment to sustainability. However, the variability in sizing can pose challenges, making it essential for shoppers to review item-specific feedback prior to making a purchase.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
