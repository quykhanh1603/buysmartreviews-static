Reformation is a sustainability-focused women’s clothing brand known for its LA-designed dresses. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Reformation is a legitimate, well-known brand with genuinely liked designs, but sizing consistency is a real and recurring issue worth planning around.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: reports vary by region/source, generally landing in the low-to-mid range from several hundred reviews.

Recurring themes

Positive: customers consistently praise the design and fit when the sizing lands right, along with responsive, helpful customer service when issues come up.

Negative: the most common complaint by far is sizing running inconsistently between styles — some items too big, others too small — along with reports of thin or unlined material and shrinking after washing per care instructions.

Before you order

  • Check size-specific reviews for the exact item, not just the brand overall.
  • Follow care instructions precisely, and consider air-drying delicate pieces even if machine-dry is listed as an option.

Bottom line

Reformation is a legitimate brand with well-liked designs; sizing inconsistency is the main risk factor, so check item-specific reviews before ordering.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
