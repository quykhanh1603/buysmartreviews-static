Stark Rating Discrepancy

CarMax holds an impressive A+ rating from the Better Business Bureau, showcasing its responsiveness to formal complaints. However, its Trustpilot score lingers around 1.8 out of 5, accompanied by over 2,000 complaints logged on the BBB site in the past three years, highlighting a concerning gap between complaint resolution and overall customer satisfaction.

What Customers Are Saying

  • BBB: A+ rating for responsiveness.
  • Trustpilot: Approximately 1.8 out of 5, indicating significant customer dissatisfaction.

While CarMax is quick to address complaints, numerous buyers report issues such as undisclosed prior accident histories and challenges with customer service. Conversely, satisfied customers appreciate the no-haggle pricing and the company's customer-friendly return policy.

If you’re considering a purchase from CarMax, it's wise to conduct an independent vehicle history check and arrange for a pre-purchase inspection if possible. Retaining all transaction documentation can also safeguard against potential issues.

CarMax’s customer feedback suggests it may have legitimate services, but substantial issues remain in customer experience.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
