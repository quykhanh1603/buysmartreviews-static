Abt Electronics is an independent, single-location (but large-scale) electronics and appliance retailer based in Glenview, Illinois, in business since 1936. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Abt has one of the stronger review records among appliance retailers we’ve checked — a legitimate, long-established, BBB-accredited business.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4 out of 5 from over 500 reviews.
  • ResellerRatings: around 4.65 out of 5 from over 13,000 reviews.
  • BBB: accredited.

Recurring themes

Positive: service and delivery quality are frequently praised across a large, consistent review volume.

Negative: a smaller share of reviews mention order cancellations and customer service responsiveness on specific issues, along with some warranty-process complaints.

Before you order

  • Since Abt ships nationwide from a single location, confirm delivery timelines for your specific region before ordering large appliances.

Bottom line

Abt Electronics has a consistently strong, long-standing review record. One of the more trustworthy appliance retailers we’ve evaluated.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
