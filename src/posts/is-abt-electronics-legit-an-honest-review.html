An Established Name in Electronics

Based in Glenview, Illinois, Abt Electronics has been serving customers since 1936. As a large-scale independent retailer, it has built a reputation for quality and service.

Customer Feedback Insights

Common Themes in Reviews

Positive Aspects: Many customers commend the quality of service and delivery, consistently highlighted across a wide range of reviews.

Negative Aspects: Some customers express concerns regarding order cancellations and responsiveness of customer service, as well as issues related to warranty processes.

Final Thoughts

With a commendable history and a strong review presence, Abt Electronics stands out as one of the more reliable appliance retailers available today.
