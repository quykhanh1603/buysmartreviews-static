Understanding Casper's Reputation

Casper is considered a significant player in the mattress industry, having established itself as one of the first direct-to-consumer brands. However, after its acquisition in 2024, there are indications that their customer service and product satisfaction may have taken a hit.

Customer Feedback Overview

  • Trustpilot: currently holds a “Poor” rating, averaging around 2 out of 5.
  • BBB: has an A- rating, yet the customer review score is concerningly low at approximately 1.16 out of 5.

Common Customer Complaints

Negative experiences: Many customers report issues such as foam deterioration leading to discomfort, claims about warranty denials despite visible issues, and difficulties in reaching customer service since the ownership change in 2024.

Important Considerations

  • Inquire with customer support regarding the current status of warranty processing and any specific requirements before making your purchase.
  • Make the most of the trial period; keep a record of any changes in comfort or support, including photos and dates, to support potential warranty claims.

Final Thoughts

While Casper is still a recognizable and legitimate brand, recent reviews imply a decline in service quality since their 2024 acquisition. It's wise to have realistic expectations about warranty support if you decide to proceed.

The ratings mentioned reflect publicly available information at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
