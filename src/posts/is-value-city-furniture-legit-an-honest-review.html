Value City Furniture is a US furniture retail chain. Here’s an honest look based on public review data — including a significant, recent development that changes our usual advice.

Quick verdict

Unlike most companies on this site, we’d advise real caution here. Beyond a consistently poor review record, public reports indicate Value City Furniture filed for bankruptcy in early 2026 and would not be fulfilling existing warranty claims. If accurate, this materially changes the risk of ordering from or holding a warranty with this company — we’d recommend independently confirming current operating status directly before any purchase.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: an F rating, with over 1,000 complaints on file, and not BBB accredited.
  • PissedConsumer: around 1.8 out of 5 from nearly 1,500 reviews.

Recurring themes

Negative: complaints include orders taken for products the company reportedly couldn’t deliver, along with delivery cancellations and rescheduling problems.

What to do

  • Before ordering anything, independently verify the company’s current operating and financial status (a quick search for recent news coverage is a reasonable first step).
  • If you already hold a warranty or pending order, document everything and consider contacting your state attorney general’s consumer protection office if the company is unresponsive.

Bottom line

Given the poor review record combined with reported bankruptcy filings, this is one of the few companies on this site we’d actively caution against ordering from until its operating status is independently confirmed.

Information above reflects publicly available data at the time of writing. Given the reported bankruptcy, please verify current status directly before acting on anything here.
