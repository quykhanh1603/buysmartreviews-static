Current Status of Value City Furniture

Value City Furniture has recently faced significant changes, primarily due to its bankruptcy proceedings. All physical locations of Value City Furniture and its sister brand, American Signature Furniture, are permanently closing. As a result, customers can take advantage of final sales while remaining stock lasts, with discounts reportedly reaching up to 90% off. However, it’s crucial to note that all sales are final, and neither returns nor exchanges are accepted at any location.

Order Fulfillment and Deposits

If you placed a deposit on an item, there’s a chance it may still be fulfilled if the product is in stock. However, the company has stated that they cannot guarantee the fulfillment of all orders. Customers who find themselves in this situation can file a claim for their deposits if the items are no longer available. For tracking your order, there is a designated order tracking feature on the company’s website.

Customer Policies and Support

Value City Furniture has issued a clear notice regarding their customer service operations amidst these transitions. Customers are encouraged to continue making financing payments on any furniture they financed prior to the closure. If you have questions regarding your order, the Customer Care Crew can be reached at (888) 751-8552. They are available to assist with inquiries, particularly those regarding order status.

Warranties and Gift Cards

As of now, Value City Furniture continues to offer extended warranties on new purchases. However, they have stopped accepting gift cards, which could be disappointing for customers holding such cards. The option for refunds on deposits is also limited, as they are not offering refunds at this time.

The Future of Value City Furniture

The closure of all retail locations significantly impacts the brand's future. With ongoing sales and stock clearance, it may be worthwhile for customers looking for discounted furniture. Nevertheless, transparency regarding customer service policies, order fulfillment, and warranty offerings is crucial for maintaining trust during this period of transition. It's advisable for potential buyers to remain cautious and informed before making purchases, especially considering the context of the company's current operations.
