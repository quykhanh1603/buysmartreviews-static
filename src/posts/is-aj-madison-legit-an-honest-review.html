A Closer Look at AJ Madison

AJ Madison operates out of Brooklyn, NY, offering a wide range of kitchen and laundry appliances for online purchase. While the company has been in business for a considerable time, customer experiences reveal a mix of satisfaction and challenges, particularly when it comes to deliveries.

Customer Feedback Summary

  • Trustpilot: They enjoy a good reputation on Trustpilot, being one of the more favorably rated appliance retailers, but customer feedback tends to be a bit of a mixed bag.
  • BBB: The Better Business Bureau profile indicates several complaints, notably relating to delivery times and communication difficulties.

What Customers Are Saying

Positive Aspects: Many customers have praised the live chat feature, which they found responsive and helpful. Buyers also appreciate clear pricing without hidden fees, as well as seamless delivery coordination, especially from those who have shopped with AJ Madison multiple times.

Negative Aspects: On the flip side, there are frequent reports of appliances arriving damaged, along with challenges in seeking resolution afterward. Some customers lamented extended delivery times, with delays reported to be as long as five weeks. Several reviewers pointed out that reaching customer support can become a hassle once issues arise after delivery.

Important Tips for New Customers

  • Make sure to thoroughly inspect your appliance right at the doorstep; refusing a damaged unit immediately is often much simpler than trying to resolve issues later.
  • Request written confirmation of your delivery window and the policies regarding returns or replacements for damaged items prior to finalizing your order.
  • If your installation is time-sensitive, allow for extra time in case of delivery delays.

Conclusion

AJ Madison appears to be a credible retailer in the appliance market, though it's crucial for customers to be aware of the significant disparities between the sales experience and post-sale support. Taking a few extra moments to inspect your delivery could save you considerable hassle down the line.
