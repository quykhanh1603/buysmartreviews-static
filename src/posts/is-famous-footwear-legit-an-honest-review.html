Famous Footwear is a long-running American shoe retailer, in business since 1917. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Famous Footwear is a legitimate, long-established retailer with genuinely mixed reviews — some smooth, fast experiences alongside real complaints about refunds and order handling.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: not accredited, despite a long operating history (over 100 years).
  • Trustpilot: mixed.

Recurring themes

Positive: some reviewers report fast, accurate multi-item orders (five pairs of shoes delivered correctly within a week, in one example) and friendly staff.

Negative: other reviewers describe damaged items on arrival with minimal compensation offered, order cancellations without explanation, and long refund wait times.

Before you order

  • Inspect items immediately on arrival and document any damage with photos before contacting support.
  • Keep your order confirmation in case of an unexplained cancellation.

Bottom line

Famous Footwear is a legitimate, long-established retailer with genuinely inconsistent order experiences. Document any issues immediately given the reports of minimal compensation for damaged items.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
