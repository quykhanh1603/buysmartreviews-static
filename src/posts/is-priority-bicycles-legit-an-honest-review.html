Priority Bicycles is a New York City-based direct-to-consumer bike brand known for low-maintenance belt-drive bikes. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Priority Bicycles has one of the strongest review records of any bike brand we’ve checked — a legitimate, highly regarded company by a wide margin.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4.8 out of 5 from nearly 8,000 reviews.

Recurring themes

Positive: product quality, fast shipping, and thorough customer service are consistently and repeatedly praised across a large review volume.

Negative: isolated complaints exist, as with any retailer, but don’t form a consistent pattern in available data.

Before you order

  • Priority bikes ship mostly assembled but still require some final setup — budget a bit of time or a local shop’s help if you’re not comfortable with basic bike assembly.

Bottom line

Priority Bicycles has an exceptionally strong, high-volume review record. One of the more trustworthy bike brands available.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
