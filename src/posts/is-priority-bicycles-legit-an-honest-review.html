Overview of Priority Bicycles

Based in New York City, Priority Bicycles is carving a niche in the cycling world with its direct-to-consumer approach, especially known for offering low-maintenance belt-drive bikes. This review dives into the overall customer feedback to give you an honest perspective on the brand.

Customer Feedback Highlights

  • Trustpilot: an impressive score of around 4.8 out of 5, derived from nearly 8,000 reviews.

What Customers Love

Positive Aspects: Many reviewers repeatedly highlight the exceptional product quality, speedy shipping, and responsive customer service. These elements contribute to a strong overall satisfaction among customers.

Concerns and Considerations

Negative Aspects: While there are some isolated complaints, they do not suggest a widespread issue across the customer base, a common pattern seen in many businesses.

Important Information

  • When ordering a bike, keep in mind that while they arrive mostly assembled, some final setup is necessary. If you’re not experienced with bike assembly, consider setting aside some time or getting assistance from a local shop.

Final Thoughts

With one of the most robust review records among bike brands, Priority Bicycles stands out as a trustworthy choice for consumers looking for reliable bikes.
