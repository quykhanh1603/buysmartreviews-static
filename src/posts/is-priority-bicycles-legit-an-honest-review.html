Should you consider Priority Bicycles?

If you're looking for a reliable bike, you might want to check out Priority Bicycles. Based in New York City, they focus on a direct-to-consumer model, making it easier to get low-maintenance belt-drive bikes that require less upkeep. Let’s take a look at what other customers are saying.

What do customers think about their experiences?

  • Trustpilot: They hold an impressive rating of around 4.8 out of 5 based on nearly 8,000 reviews, which certainly speaks to their credibility.

What are the highlights and concerns?

Positive feedback: Many customers rave about the high quality of the bikes, quick shipping, and helpful customer service. This seems to create a solid satisfaction level among riders.

Some things to watch out for: While there are a few complaints, they appear to be isolated incidents rather than indicative of a larger issue.

Just a heads-up when ordering: the bikes come mostly assembled, but you might need to do some final setup. If you're not handy, it could be worth getting a local shop's help to ensure everything's just right.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
