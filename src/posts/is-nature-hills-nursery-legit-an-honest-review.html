Nature Hills Nursery is a large online plant nursery based in Omaha, Nebraska, shipping trees and shrubs nationwide. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Nature Hills has a history of real customer service complaints, but the company reports having changed management and support processes recently — worth knowing both the past pattern and the recent changes.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: not accredited, with a documented history of dozens of complaints in past years about unreachable customer service.

Recurring themes

Negative (historical): customers reporting they couldn’t reach the company about a problem, warranty claims resulting only in store credit rather than refunds, and having to pay 50% of the price for a replacement even under warranty. Some reviewers also reported plants arriving dead or diseased.

Recent change: as of mid-2025, the company reports new management, new nursery supplier partnerships aimed at reducing shipment errors, and a newly added dedicated customer support team.

Before you order

  • Read the current warranty terms carefully before ordering, specifically what you get (refund vs. store credit) and any replacement fees.
  • Photograph plants immediately on arrival in case you need to file a claim.

Bottom line

Nature Hills has a documented history of service complaints, with the company stating it has recently made changes to address them. Read current warranty terms carefully given the past pattern.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
