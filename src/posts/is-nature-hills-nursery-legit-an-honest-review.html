Overview of Nature Hills Nursery

Nature Hills Nursery is an online gardening resource specializing in a wide range of plants, including trees, shrubs, and perennials. With over 2,000 varieties, they are dedicated to helping gardeners find the right plants to thrive in their specific growing zones. By using your ZIP code, you can receive personalized recommendations tailored to your area's climate and soil conditions.

A Diverse Range of Plants

The selection at Nature Hills is impressive. They offer various categories to meet different gardening needs, including:

  • Trees: This includes shade trees, flowering trees, evergreens, and fast-growing varieties. Some popular options are Japanese Maples, Dogwoods, and Magnolia trees.
  • Shrubs: Flowering shrubs, groundcovers, and hedges are available, with popular choices like Hydrangeas and Lilac bushes.
  • Roses: A comprehensive selection of roses, including Knock Out® and climbing varieties, is offered for those looking to add beauty to their gardens.
  • Perennials & Grasses: From spring-blooming flowers to ornamental grasses and edibles, the variety caters to different seasonal interests.
  • Edible Plants: Nature Hills also stocks a range of fruit trees and bushes along with vegetable plants, making it a great choice for home gardeners looking to grow their own food.

Standout Features

One of the standout features of Nature Hills is their Growing & Glowing Guarantee, which provides a 45-day guarantee on all live plants. This ensures that if your plant doesn’t thrive upon arrival, you can receive a replacement or refund, enhancing the shopping experience with peace of mind.

Additionally, Nature Hills offers free shipping on orders over $199, making it more economical for gardeners looking to purchase multiple plants at once. This can help to maximize the value of your investment in gardening.

Shop by Need

Nature Hills understands that different gardeners have different needs, which is why they have a Shop By Need section. This allows customers to find plants based on specific requirements such as:

  • Drought tolerance
  • Low maintenance
  • Pollinator-friendly options
  • Deer resistance

Customer Experience and Trust Signals

While specific Trustpilot data was not available, the nature of Nature Hills’ offerings and guarantees suggests a commitment to customer satisfaction. Their policies, including zone-matching and live-plant guarantees, indicate they prioritize helping customers succeed in their gardening endeavors.

Their website is also designed to be user-friendly, providing resources like a plant zone map and growing guides to assist customers in making informed choices. This dedication to education reflects positively on their legitimacy in the gardening community.

Conclusion

In summary, Nature Hills Nursery appears to be a legitimate and trustworthy online resource for gardeners seeking a wide variety of trees, shrubs, and perennials. Their focus on customer support, quality plants, and a generous shipping policy enhances their credibility. Whether you're a novice gardener or an experienced horticulturist, Nature Hills has the tools and resources to help you cultivate your dream garden.
