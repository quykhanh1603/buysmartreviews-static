What Should You Know About Nature Hills Nursery?

Located in Omaha, Nebraska, Nature Hills Nursery is a popular online destination for tree and shrub enthusiasts, with shipping available across the United States. However, be aware that their customer service track record is a bit mixed, which could impact your experience.

Are Customers Satisfied with Their Experience?

  • BBB Status: Nature Hills is not accredited by the Better Business Bureau and has received numerous complaints primarily focused on customer service issues.

What Can You Learn from Others' Experiences?

Historical Issues: Many customers have reported significant frustration trying to contact the company. Common complaints include warranty claims that are resolved with store credit instead of refunds and charges for replacement items, even when they were under warranty. Additionally, there's feedback about plants arriving dead or diseased.

Recent Improvements: On a brighter note, in mid-2025, Nature Hills announced a management change aimed at improving the quality of their shipments by partnering closely with suppliers. They've also set up a dedicated customer support team to better handle past complaints.

What Should You Consider Before Making Your Purchase?

  • Carefully read the current warranty conditions to understand your options regarding refunds, store credit, and potential fees for replacements.
  • Don’t forget to take photos of your plants as soon as they arrive; these can be valuable if you need to submit a claim.

Overall, while Nature Hills Nursery has had its share of complaints, those management changes might indicate a new direction towards better customer service. Just make sure you’re informed about warranty terms before you order.
