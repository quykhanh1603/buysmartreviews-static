The Nature of Nature Hills Nursery

Located in Omaha, Nebraska, Nature Hills Nursery is a prominent online destination for tree and shrub enthusiasts, offering shipping across the United States. However, potential customers should be aware of its mixed history regarding customer service.

Customer Feedback Insights

  • BBB Status: Nature Hills is not accredited by the Better Business Bureau and has a record of numerous complaints due to customer service issues.

Lessons from Past Customer Experiences

Historical Issues: Many customers have expressed frustration over difficulties in reaching the company for assistance. Complaints include warranty claims that resulted in store credit rather than refunds and charges for replacement items even under warranty. Some customers have also reported that their plants arrived in poor condition, either dead or diseased.

Recent Improvements: In mid-2025, the company announced a change in management and a new focus on building partnerships with suppliers to enhance the quality of shipments. Additionally, they've established a dedicated customer support team to address past complaints.

Things to Consider Before Ordering

  • Review the current warranty conditions thoroughly to understand the options available for refunds versus store credit and any potential replacement fees.
  • Take photos of your plants as soon as they arrive, which could be helpful if you need to file a claim.

Final Thoughts

Nature Hills Nursery has faced its share of complaints, but the company's recent management changes may signal a commitment to improving customer experiences. Take the time to familiarize yourself with the warranty terms before making a purchase, considering the company's past performance.
