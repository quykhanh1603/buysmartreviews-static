Jared (The Galleria of Jewelry) is a large jewelry chain, also part of Signet Jewelers alongside Kay and Zales. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Jared shows the same pattern as its sister Signet brands: a strong BBB letter grade paired with a considerably weaker independent review record — among the largest gaps in the jewelry category.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: A+ rating, but a customer review score around 1.1 out of 5.
  • Trustpilot: around 2.3 out of 5, with over 80% of reviews at 1-star.

Recurring themes

Negative: customer service complaints, questions about financing terms explained at time of sale, and quality issues including reports of stones falling out within months of purchase.

Before you order

  • Get financing terms in writing before signing anything.
  • Inspect settings carefully at purchase and again periodically, given the reported prong/stone-security complaints.

Bottom line

Jared is a legitimate, established chain, but its BBB accreditation doesn’t line up with independent customer sentiment. Treat the same way as its sister brands: read everything before signing.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
