Overview of Sunday Lawn Care

Sunday is a direct-to-consumer lawn care subscription service that provides pre-measured nutrient packets customized according to a soil test of your yard. Let's delve into what the reviews say about their service.

Customer Feedback Highlights

  • Company site: approximately 4.3 out of 5 stars based on around 6,000 reviews.
  • BBB: Holds an A+ rating.

What Users Like and Dislike

Pros: Many customers appreciate the convenience of doorstep delivery, the ease of use, and the emphasis on pet and kid-safe formulations.

Cons: Some users have noted issues with the sprayer nozzle not dispensing the product as expected, along with a few complaints about unexpected charges and challenges with canceling their subscriptions.

Important Tips for Potential Customers

  • Before starting, test the sprayer nozzle on a small area to ensure it works properly; reach out to customer support if you encounter any issues.
  • Be mindful of your renewal date and the cancellation policy if you decide you don’t wish to continue the subscription.

Final Thoughts

Overall, Sunday is well-regarded for its convenient and safe lawn care solutions. Just remember to verify the sprayer works effectively and keep track of your subscription dates.
