Sunday is a direct-to-consumer lawn care subscription brand, sending pre-measured nutrient pouches based on a soil test of your yard. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Sunday has a generally favorable reputation, particularly for convenience and pet/kid-safe formulations, though subscription billing and a specific product mechanism issue are worth knowing.

What customer ratings show

  • Company site: around 4.3 out of 5 from approximately 6,000 reviews.
  • BBB: A+ rating.

Recurring themes

Positive: convenience of doorstep delivery, ease of use, and pet/kid-safe formulation are the most consistent praise points.

Negative: a recurring complaint involves the sprayer nozzle failing to dispense product correctly, plus some reports of unexpected charges and difficulty canceling the subscription.

Before you order

  • Test the sprayer nozzle on a small area first, and contact support promptly if it’s not dispensing correctly — don’t assume application happened if you don’t see product coming out.
  • Note your renewal date and cancellation deadline if you don’t plan to continue the subscription.

Bottom line

Sunday has a generally positive reputation for convenience and safety. Check the sprayer mechanism works before relying on a full application, and track your subscription renewal date.
