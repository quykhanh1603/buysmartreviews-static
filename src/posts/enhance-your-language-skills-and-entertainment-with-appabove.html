Explore AppAbove's Unique Offerings

AppAbove, Inc. specializes in creating quality mobile applications and games that cater to a wide range of interests and needs. One of their standout products is the SPANISH ANYWHERE® app, which is designed to assist users in learning and communicating in both Spanish and English.

This app is perfect for travelers, students, business professionals, and anyone eager to enhance their language skills. With SPANISH ANYWHERE®, users can engage in speaking, reading, studying, pronouncing, and translating between the two languages at their convenience, making it an ideal tool for on-the-go language learning.

Engaging Games for All

In addition to their language app, AppAbove also develops an array of mobile games that are both entertaining and popular among users. Titles such as Armadillo Gold Rush, Jail Trail, Monster Smash, Blingster Battle, and Pocket Puppy are just a few examples of the action-packed, puzzle, and multiplayer games available.

These games are compatible with various platforms including BREW, J2ME, Windows Mobile, Blackberry, and iPhone, ensuring that a wide audience can access and enjoy the fun.
