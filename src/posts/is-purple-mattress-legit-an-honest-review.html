Purple is a direct-to-consumer mattress brand known for its distinctive gel-grid comfort layer. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Purple is a legitimate, established mattress brand, but recent reviews show real friction around warranty enforcement and return/exchange fees worth understanding before you buy.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: mixed, with a relatively small review sample for a brand of its size.
  • BBB: reviews skew negative.

Recurring themes

Negative: the clearest pattern involves warranty claims for sagging being denied under a strict measurement standard (the mattress has to show over 1 inch of indentation while unloaded), even when customers say it feels collapsed under normal use. Some customers in 2026 also reported unexpected return/exchange fees as high as $250.

Positive: customers who didn’t need to file a warranty or return claim generally report satisfaction with the mattress’s distinctive feel.

Before you order

  • Read the exact sagging/warranty measurement standard before you buy, not after a problem arises.
  • Confirm current return and exchange fee policy directly with support, since terms have reportedly changed.

Bottom line

Purple is a legitimate mattress brand, but warranty and return-fee friction are common enough in recent reviews that it’s worth understanding the fine print before you buy.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
