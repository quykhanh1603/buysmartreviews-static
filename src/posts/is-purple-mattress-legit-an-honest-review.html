Understanding Purple Mattress

Purple is a direct-to-consumer mattress brand that stands out thanks to its innovative gel-grid comfort layer. However, before diving into a purchase, it's essential to get the full picture based on customer reviews and experiences.

Customer Ratings Overview

  • Trustpilot: The feedback is somewhat mixed, and the volume of reviews for a brand of this scale is relatively limited.
  • BBB: Unfortunately, reviews here tend to tilt towards the negative side.

Common Customer Experiences

Challenges: A notable issue among reviewers involves warranty claims. Many customers have reported that their claims for sagging mattresses were denied, as the strict measurement requirement mandates an indentation of over 1 inch when the mattress is not in use. This has been frustrating for those who feel their mattress has collapsed during regular use. Additionally, a number of customers in 2026 mentioned unexpected return or exchange fees that could reach as high as $250.

Positive Feedback: On a brighter note, customers who have not had to navigate warranty or return processes generally express satisfaction with the mattress's unique feel and comfort.

What to Consider Before Purchasing

  • Familiarize yourself with the warranty standards regarding sagging to avoid surprises down the line.
  • Double-check the latest return and exchange fee policies by contacting customer support, as these terms have been known to change.

Final Thoughts

While Purple is a recognized name in the mattress market, the recent feedback indicates that understanding the particulars of their warranty and return policies is crucial before making a purchase.
