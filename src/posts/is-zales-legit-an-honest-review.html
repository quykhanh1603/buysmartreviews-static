Is Zales a Reliable Choice?

Zales has been in the jewelry game since 1924 and is part of Signet Jewelers, but just because it’s a well-known name doesn’t mean it’s all smooth sailing. It’s a good idea to pay attention to the reviews and ratings that customers have shared.

What Are Customers Saying?

  • BBB Rating: They have an A+ rating from the Better Business Bureau, but keep in mind that they've had nearly 600 complaints in the past three years.
  • Trustpilot Reviews: On Trustpilot, things are a bit mixed; you’ll find quite a few negative experiences from shoppers there.

What Should You Watch Out For?

Negative Feedback: A number of customers have mentioned problems like warranties not being honored and receiving jewelry back in a damaged state after repairs. There’s also been confusion regarding financing terms while making a purchase.

Positive Feedback: On the flip side, some people have had good experiences with the staff in-store, suggesting that your experience could vary depending on the location.

  • Make sure you get the financing terms documented clearly before signing anything—verbal explanations can lead to misunderstandings.
  • If you have to send your jewelry for repairs, take photos to document its condition before and after.

In summary, Zales has a long-standing reputation, but there are some significant service issues reflected in customer feedback despite their impressive BBB rating. It's smart to get everything in writing to protect yourself.

The ratings and reviews mentioned above are based on publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
