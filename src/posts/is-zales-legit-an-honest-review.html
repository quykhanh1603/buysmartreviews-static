Zales is a long-established mall-based jewelry chain, also part of Signet Jewelers, operating since 1924. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Zales is a legitimate, long-standing retailer, with the same pattern seen at sister brand Kay: a strong BBB letter grade paired with weaker independent review sentiment.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: A+ rating, though with nearly 600 complaints filed in the past three years.
  • Trustpilot: mixed, with a meaningful share of negative reviews.

Recurring themes

Negative: unhonored warranties, jewelry reported damaged after repair service, and financing terms some customers say were misunderstood at time of sale.

Positive: some reviewers describe positive in-store staff interactions, suggesting experience varies by location.

Before you order

  • Get financing terms explained and written down clearly before signing, rather than relying on a verbal summary.
  • If sending jewelry in for repair, photograph its condition before and after.

Bottom line

Zales is a legitimate, long-established chain, but independent reviews show consistent service complaints despite its BBB rating. Get financing and repair terms in writing.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
