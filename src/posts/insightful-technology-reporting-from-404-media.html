Who is 404 Media?

404 Media is an innovative independent media outlet created by a team of seasoned technology journalists, including Jason Koebler, Emanuel Maiberg, Samantha Cole, and Joseph Cox. The platform dives deep into how technology is affecting society, exploring a wide range of topics such as hacking, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and online privacy.

What They Offer

At 404 Media, readers can expect comprehensive investigative reports, long-form features, blogs, and timely scoops that dissect complex technological issues. The founders are dedicated to bringing serious inquiry into areas of technology that significantly impact everyday life, including consumer rights, surveillance, and the democratization of the internet.

Subscription Options

For readers looking to access the full depth of content that 404 Media provides, subscriptions are available on both monthly and annual terms. The pricing structure gives readers flexibility, allowing them to choose the duration that best suits their needs. Once a subscriber cancels, they will maintain access until the end of their current billing cycle, though it’s important to note that there are no pro-rata refunds for the remaining access time.

Managing Your Subscription

The subscription management system is designed to be user-friendly, placing full responsibility in the hands of subscribers to manage their accounts. To view billing history and download receipts, users can easily navigate to their account settings on the site. However, keep in mind that 404 Media currently employs a magic login link system via email rather than standard username/password credentials, which may pose challenges if you experience login issues.

The Founders’ Expertise

The founders of 404 Media bring a wealth of experience in technology journalism. Jason Koebler, for instance, was the editor-in-chief at Motherboard, where he cultivated significant editorial content and vision. His expertise, along with that of his co-founders, enhances the credibility and quality of the journalism presented on the platform.

Conclusion

404 Media stands out as an essential resource for individuals interested in understanding the nuanced relationship between technology and society. The platform's dedication to investigative journalism, coupled with the expertise of its founders, ensures that readers receive insightful and impactful content. While subscription management is straightforward, prospective subscribers should consider the lack of a free trial and pro-rata refund policy when deciding to commit.
