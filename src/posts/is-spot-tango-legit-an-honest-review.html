Understanding Spot & Tango's Reputation

Spot & Tango specializes in a gently-dried dog food subscription model, highlighted by their UnKibble products. Analyzing public reviews uncovers notable discrepancies in customer satisfaction across different review platforms.

Diving into Customer Feedback

  • Trustpilot: Users generally rate Spot & Tango positively, often commending the quality of the food and the responsiveness of customer service.
  • BBB: In stark contrast, they hold an F rating, with a troubling customer review score of about 1.3 out of 5 based on fewer submissions.

Common Customer Experiences

Positive Reactions: Many pet owners report that their dogs, including picky eaters, enjoy the meals, and there are mentions of noticeable improvements in digestion and coat health.

Negative Feedback: Complaints on the BBB often highlight issues where customers believed they were making a one-time purchase but were instead placed into a recurring subscription. A less frequent concern involves reports of gastrointestinal distress in dogs, which often resolved after ceasing the food but recurred upon reintroduction.

Things to Consider Before Buying

  • Carefully review the checkout details to ensure clarity on whether you are signing up for a subscription or a single order.
  • If your dog experiences digestive issues after a food switch, discontinue use immediately and consult your veterinarian.

Final Thoughts

While Spot & Tango receives favorable reviews for their dog food quality, the pattern of complaints regarding ambiguous subscription practices on the BBB warrants a careful examination of their checkout processes.
