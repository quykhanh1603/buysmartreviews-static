Spot & Tango is a fresh and gently-dried dog food subscription brand, known for its UnKibble product line. Here’s an honest look based on public review data — including a striking gap between platforms.

Quick verdict

Spot & Tango shows one of the largest gaps between Trustpilot and BBB of any company we’ve reviewed — worth understanding both sides before you order, particularly around subscription terms.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: generally positive, with reviewers frequently praising product quality and customer service.
  • BBB: an F rating, with a customer review score around 1.3 out of 5 from a smaller sample.

Recurring themes

Positive: dogs eating well (including picky eaters), easy-to-store UnKibble format, and improvements in digestion and coat are frequently cited.

Negative: BBB complaints center specifically on customers reporting they were enrolled in a recurring subscription when they believed they were making a one-time purchase. A smaller number of reviews also describe gastrointestinal symptoms in dogs that resolved when the food was removed and returned when reintroduced.

Before you order

  • Read the checkout page carefully to confirm whether you’re purchasing a one-time order or a recurring subscription before completing your purchase.
  • If you notice digestive symptoms after switching foods, stop and consult your vet before continuing.

Bottom line

Spot & Tango’s product itself gets good feedback, but the BBB complaint pattern around unclear subscription enrollment is serious enough to read the checkout terms very carefully.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication. This is not veterinary advice — consult your vet before changing your pet’s diet.
