Understanding Spot & Tango's Reputation

When it comes to dog food subscriptions, Spot & Tango has carved out a niche with its gently-dried meal options, particularly its UnKibble line. However, a close look at online reviews reveals a stark contrast in customer satisfaction across different platforms, raising questions about their overall reliability.

Diving into Customer Feedback

On Trustpilot, many users express a generally positive sentiment towards Spot & Tango, often praising the quality of the food and the helpfulness of customer service. In sharp contrast, the Better Business Bureau (BBB) paints a different picture with an F rating and a concerning customer review score of approximately 1.3 out of 5, albeit based on a limited number of submissions.

Common Customer Experiences

Pet owners often share positive experiences where their dogs, including those who are typically picky eaters, seem to enjoy the meals. Many report benefits such as improved digestion and healthier coats. However, complaints lodged on the BBB frequently highlight issues surrounding the subscription model, where customers believed they were making a one-time purchase, only to find themselves enrolled in a recurring subscription. Some customers also mentioned gastrointestinal issues in their pets, which tended to resolve upon discontinuation of the food but reappeared when reintroduced.

Final Thoughts

While Spot & Tango enjoys favorable feedback for the quality of its dog food, the recurring complaints about lack of clarity in their subscription practices, particularly noted on the BBB, suggest that potential buyers should approach the checkout process with caution. Understanding the specifics of what you’re committing to can help avoid potential pitfalls.
