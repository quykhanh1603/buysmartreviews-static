B&H Photo Video is a New York-based retailer specializing in cameras, video, and pro audio equipment, popular with photography and videography professionals. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

B&H stands out with one of the strongest review records of any retailer we’ve checked across any category — a legitimate, highly regarded company by a wide margin.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: a top rating from over 330,000 reviews, regularly ranking at the top of its category.
  • BBB: A+ accredited since 1987, with a customer review score around 4.8 out of 5 from over 2,100 reviews.

Recurring themes

Positive: knowledgeable staff, accurate product listings, and reliable shipping are the most consistent praise points from a very large, sustained sample size.

Negative: a small share of complaints on BBB mention return policy specifics and occasional customer service friction, but these appear to be a small fraction of overall volume.

Before you order

  • For used/open-box gear, read the specific condition grading terms before purchasing.

Bottom line

B&H Photo Video has an exceptionally strong, sustained review record. One of the most trustworthy electronics retailers we’ve evaluated.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
