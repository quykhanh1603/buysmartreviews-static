Boden is a UK-founded clothing brand with a large US operation, known for colorful, print-heavy womenswear (plus men’s and kids’ lines). Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Boden is a well-established, legitimate retailer with an unusually large and consistently positive review record for its US site — one of the largest sample sizes of any brand we’ve checked.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot (US site): over 50,000 reviews, with a TrustScore around 4.5 out of 5.

A review volume this large, combined with a consistently high score, is a meaningfully stronger signal than a brand with only a few hundred reviews — it’s much harder to sustain a high average across tens of thousands of reviews than a small sample.

Recurring themes

Positive: reviewers consistently praise fabric quality, distinctive prints/colors not commonly found at mass retailers, and a generally smooth ordering experience.

Negative: as with most clothing retailers, the most common complaints relate to sizing running differently across styles, and occasional shipping delays during peak seasons.

Before you order

  • Check the specific size guide for each item rather than assuming consistent sizing across the catalog — common feedback across most clothing brands, not unique to Boden.
  • Review the return policy for sale items specifically, as return windows sometimes differ from full-price items.

Bottom line

Boden is a legitimate, well-reviewed retailer, backed by one of the larger and more consistent review records we’ve seen. A safe bet for buyers who like its distinctive print-driven style.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
