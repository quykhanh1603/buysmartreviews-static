Is Kylie Cosmetics Trustworthy?

Kylie Cosmetics has garnered mixed reviews, raising questions about its reliability. While some customers report satisfaction, others experience significant issues with service and products. A closer examination of various customer feedback sources illustrates this complexity.

Customer Ratings Overview

On Trustpilot, Kylie Cosmetics has a rating of approximately 3.2 out of 5 from nearly 4,830 reviews. However, a more concerning rating appears on Sitejabber, with an average of around 1.2 out of 5 based on 242 reviews. The Better Business Bureau (BBB) holds an A+ accreditation, which is noteworthy, yet there are 165 unresolved complaints out of 196 total, indicating potential customer service challenges.

What Customers Are Saying

Negative feedback highlights significant issues, with many customers citing problems related to packages not arriving or arriving damaged and incomplete. Furthermore, numerous complaints indicate that customer service has been unhelpful when it comes to processing refunds for these situations. Conversely, there are positive reviews praising the quality of the products and effective customer service, illustrating that experiences can vary widely among customers.

As you consider shopping with Kylie Cosmetics, it's wise to be cautious, especially regarding payment methods and tracking your order. This level of vigilance could serve you well if issues arise.

The ratings mentioned above are based on publicly accessible data as of the time of writing and may have changed subsequently.
