Kylie Cosmetics is Kylie Jenner’s beauty brand, known for lip kits and a broad color cosmetics line. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Kylie Cosmetics has a real gap between its BBB accreditation and actual complaint resolution rate — worth understanding, since a large share of filed complaints reportedly went unresolved.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 3.2 out of 5 from nearly 4,830 reviews.
  • Sitejabber: around 1.2 out of 5 from 242 reviews.
  • BBB: A+ accredited, but reportedly with the large majority of filed complaints (165 of 196 in one count) unresolved.

Recurring themes

Negative: packages never arriving or arriving damaged/incomplete, and customer service reportedly refusing refunds in some of these cases, are the clearest complaint pattern.

Positive: other reviewers report positive experiences with both product quality and customer service — the reviews are genuinely mixed rather than uniformly negative.

Before you order

  • Pay by credit card so you have a chargeback option if a package never arrives and a refund is refused.
  • Keep tracking information and photograph any damaged/incomplete shipment immediately.

Bottom line

Kylie Cosmetics has genuinely mixed reviews, with a concerning pattern around unresolved BBB complaints for lost or damaged orders. Payment protection is a reasonable precaution.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
