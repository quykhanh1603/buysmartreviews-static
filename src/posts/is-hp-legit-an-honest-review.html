Is HP a Reliable Choice for Your Needs?

Thinking about ordering from HP? It’s worth diving into what customers are saying before making that decision.

What Do Customers Really Think?

  • Trustpilot: More than 9,500 reviews exist, with many users sharing negative experiences.
  • Reviews.io: The company has a low average score of around 1.24 out of 5, based on over 350 reviews.

What Issues Can You Expect?

Negative: A lot of customers have highlighted issues with printer connectivity, poor product quality, and significant challenges in reaching customer support. Some even mention having to involve the BBB or state attorney general just to elicit a response.

Tip: Before you buy, it’s a good idea to check out known problems specific to the model you’re interested in, whether it’s a printer or a PC. Also, keep all your purchase documentation. It could be vital if you need to escalate a support issue later on.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
