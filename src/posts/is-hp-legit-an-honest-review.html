HP is one of the largest computer and printer manufacturers in the world, selling direct to consumers online. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

HP is a massive, legitimate, publicly traded company, but its independent review record is among the weakest of any major electronics manufacturer we’ve checked — worth going in with realistic expectations about support.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 9,500 reviews, skewing strongly negative.
  • Reviews.io: around 1.24 out of 5 from over 350 reviews.

Recurring themes

Negative: printer connectivity issues, product quality complaints, and customer service described as very hard to reach through official channels — some reviewers report needing to file BBB or state attorney general complaints before getting resolution.

Before you order

  • Research known issues for the specific model (printer or PC) before buying, since certain product lines draw more consistent complaints than others.
  • Keep all documentation from day one, in case you need to escalate a support issue formally.

Bottom line

HP is a legitimate, massive manufacturer, but current review sentiment is notably weak. If ordering direct, research your specific model’s track record first and keep documentation in case of a support issue.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
