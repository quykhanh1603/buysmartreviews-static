The Manufacturer at a Glance

HP stands as one of the largest manufacturers of computers and printers in the world, engaging directly with consumers through online sales. However, potential buyers might want to take a closer look at the public review data before diving in.

Understanding Customer Feedback

  • Trustpilot: Over 9,500 reviews are available, with a majority leaning towards negative experiences.
  • Reviews.io: The company holds a low average score of approximately 1.24 out of 5, based on over 350 reviews.

Common Customer Concerns

Negative: Many customers report issues related to printer connectivity, subpar product quality, and significant difficulty in reaching customer support. Some users have mentioned needing to escalate their issues to the BBB or state attorney general just to get a response.

What to Consider Before You Buy

  • Look into known issues specific to the model you’re interested in (be it a printer or a PC) since some product lines tend to receive more complaints than others.
  • It’s wise to keep all purchase documentation from day one, as this may be necessary if you need to formally escalate a support issue.

Final Thoughts

While HP is a reputable and sizable manufacturer, the overall sentiment in customer reviews is notably negative. If you're thinking about ordering directly from them, it's prudent to research your specific model's reputation first and maintain your purchase records in case of support needs.
