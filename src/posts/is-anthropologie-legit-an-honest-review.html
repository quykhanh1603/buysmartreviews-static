Anthropologie is a lifestyle and clothing retailer known for bohemian-inspired women’s fashion and home goods. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Anthropologie is a legitimate, established retailer, but ratings vary dramatically by platform — worth checking more than one source before a larger purchase.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 10,700 reviews, mixed.
  • Sitejabber: around 1.4 out of 5 from a much smaller sample.
  • Yelp: around 3.5 out of 5 from nearly 5,900 reviews.

Recurring themes

Negative: customer service and shipping problems, reports of broken/damaged items arriving (sometimes repeatedly on replacement shipments), and a refund process some customers describe as slow (processing only after 30 days in some cases).

Before you order

  • Inspect fragile or breakable items immediately and document any damage before requesting a replacement.
  • Track your refund timeline and follow up if it exceeds the stated processing window.

Bottom line

Anthropologie is a legitimate retailer with a wide range of ratings depending on platform. Yelp paints a more favorable picture than some other review sites, so cross-checking is worthwhile.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
