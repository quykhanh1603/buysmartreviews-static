Understanding Anthropologie's Reputation

With an average rating of approximately 1.4 out of 5 on Sitejabber, customers have mixed feelings about their experiences with Anthropologie. This lifestyle and clothing retailer is known for its bohemian-inspired women’s apparel and home decor, yet trustworthiness seems to vary across various review platforms.

  • Trustpilot: Over 10,700 reviews with mixed sentiments.
  • Sitejabber: Average rating of around 1.4 out of 5 from a smaller sample.
  • Yelp: A more favorable score of about 3.5 out of 5 from nearly 5,900 reviews.

Common Customer Concerns

Many customers have reported issues with customer service, including delays in shipping and instances of receiving broken or damaged items, even with replacement orders. Additionally, some have found the refund process frustratingly slow, with some refunds only being processed after 30 days.

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Examine any fragile items right after delivery and document any damages prior to requesting a replacement.
  • Keep track of your refund timeline, and don't hesitate to follow up if it takes longer than the stated processing period.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
