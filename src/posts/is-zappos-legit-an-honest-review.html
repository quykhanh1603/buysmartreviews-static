Shocking Trustpilot Rating

Zappos' current average rating on Trustpilot is only 1.7 out of 5, a staggering drop from its once stellar reputation for customer service. This stark figure poses questions about the company's reliability during recent times.

Customer Experiences and Feedback

The feedback from customers reveals a sharp divide in experiences:

  • Positive experiences: Many satisfied customers appreciate the genuine products received and the reliable return policies in place.
  • Negative experiences: On the flip side, a growing number of complaints highlight issues like delayed refunds, incorrect items shipped, and reports of used or worn shoes being sent instead of new ones.

If you're thinking about ordering from Zappos, consider these tips: inspect your items as soon as they arrive for any signs of prior use, retain order confirmations and document any refund requests in case delays occur, and opt to pay by credit card to allow for a chargeback option if you face challenges with refunds.

While Zappos remains a legitimate retailer, the recent ratings indicate significant inconsistencies in service quality.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
