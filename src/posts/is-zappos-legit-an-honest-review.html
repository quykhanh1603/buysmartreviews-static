Zappos has been a well-known online shoe and clothing retailer since 1999, famous for its customer service culture. Here’s how that reputation holds up against more recent public review data.

Quick verdict

Zappos is a legitimate, long-standing retailer — over two decades in business isn’t something a scam operation typically achieves. That said, recent review data suggests customer satisfaction has slipped compared to the company’s historical reputation, so it’s worth going in with realistic expectations.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: a notably low recent rating, around 1.7 out of 5 across several hundred reviews — a sharp contrast to Zappos’ long-standing reputation for customer service.

This gap is worth calling out explicitly: a company can be legitimate and still be going through a rough patch in service quality. The two aren’t the same question.

Recurring themes

Positive: reviewers who had good experiences point to authentic, as-described products and a return policy that’s honored as advertised.

Negative: more recent complaints center on delayed refunds, wrong items shipped, and a number of reports of receiving used or worn shoes instead of new ones.

Before you order

  • Inspect items immediately on arrival and check for signs of prior use before wearing them.
  • Keep order confirmations and refund request timestamps in case a refund is delayed.
  • Pay by credit card so you have a chargeback option if a refund dispute drags on.

Bottom line

Zappos is a legitimate, established retailer, but recent customer feedback points to more service inconsistency than its reputation would suggest. Worth ordering from, but check your items carefully on arrival.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
