Sears is a historic American retailer that has significantly downsized since its 2018 bankruptcy, now operating a small number of remaining stores plus Sears Home Services and parts operations under Transformco. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Sears today is a much smaller operation than its historical reputation suggests. If you’re dealing with Sears Home Services (appliance repair) specifically, that’s a more active part of the business with its own separate review record.

What customer ratings show

  • Sears Home Services: around 4 out of 5 from over 14,600 reviews on Trustpilot — notably more active and positive than the main retail brand.
  • Sears.com (retail): minimal recent review activity, reflecting the company’s much-reduced retail footprint.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Positive: individual repair technicians are frequently praised by name for good service.

Negative: long wait times for service appointments, warranty handling issues, and difficulty reaching customer service by phone are the most cited complaints.

Before you order or book service

  • Confirm whether you’re dealing with Sears Home Services (appliance repair, still fairly active) versus Sears retail (much smaller footprint) before setting expectations.
  • Get your appointment window and any warranty terms in writing.

Bottom line

The Sears brand today is much smaller than its historical reputation. Sears Home Services has a more substantial, reasonably positive review record than the retail side.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
