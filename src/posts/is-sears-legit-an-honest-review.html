Overview of Sears

Sears has long been a household name in America, known for providing a wide range of products including Craftsman tools, Kenmore appliances, electronics, and lawn and garden equipment. They also offer services such as installation help through the Sears Blue Crew. However, the current online experience hints at serious issues with accessibility and customer service.

Customer Experience and Reviews

Recent reviews on Trustpilot have not been kind to Sears. The company holds a low rating of just 1.2 out of 5, based on a substantial number of over 1,700 reviews. Many customers express their frustration regarding poor service, missed appointments, and the challenges of dealing with warranties.

For instance, one reviewer detailed their negative experience with the Sears Home Warranty, noting that their technician deemed their microwave nonrepairable without follow-up communication. Another user lamented about the difficulty in getting support for their Kenmore refrigerator, which had issues while still under warranty.

What Products Does Sears Offer?

Sears is known for a diverse array of products:

  • Tools: Their Craftsman line is especially popular, offering everything from hand tools to power tools.
  • Appliances: Kenmore is a trusted name in home appliances, covering everything from refrigerators to washers and dryers.
  • Gardening Equipment: They provide mowers and other gardening tools for homeowners and garden enthusiasts.
  • Electronics: Sears stocks a variety of electronic goods, though specifics on current offerings were not accessible during the review process.

Issues with Service and Reliability

While Sears does offer a wide selection of goods, numerous customers report significant issues with their customer service. Many reviews highlight long wait times, lack of follow-up on repairs, and a general sense of frustration with their warranty service. Customers often feel that their Master Protection Plan does not deliver the promised support, with some claiming that coverage is not honored.

Conclusion: Is Sears Worth It?

With a long-standing history, Sears offers a variety of products and services that have traditionally been reliable. However, their current customer service reputation raises significant concerns. The combination of low Trustpilot ratings and frequent complaints suggests that potential buyers should proceed with caution. If you are considering purchasing from Sears, it may be wise to explore other options or be prepared for possible service hurdles.
