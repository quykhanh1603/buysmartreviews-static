Current State of Sears

After filing for bankruptcy in 2018, Sears operates on a much smaller scale, focusing mainly on Sears Home Services and parts operations under Transformco.

Customer Experiences and Ratings

  • Sears Home Services: enjoys approximately 4 out of 5 stars from over 14,600 reviews on Trustpilot, showing a more positive perception than the main retail brand.
  • Sears.com (retail): has very limited recent reviews, reflecting its reduced presence in retail.
  • BBB Rating: the company is not accredited.

Positive Feedback: Customers often praise individual technicians for their expertise and the quality of their work.

Negative Feedback: Issues like long service wait times, warranty handling problems, and customer service accessibility are common complaints.

  • Clarify whether you're seeking services from Sears Home Services, which is more active, or from the retail side, which has a diminished footprint.
  • Make sure to get your service appointment details and warranty terms in writing to avoid issues down the road.

Bottom Line

Sears has shifted focus significantly, with its home services seeing better customer feedback than its retail operations.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
