Overview of Sears Today

Sears is a historic American retailer that has significantly downsized since its 2018 bankruptcy, now operating a small number of remaining stores. The company has shifted its focus, running Sears Home Services and parts operations under Transformco. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Customer Feedback Insights

  • Sears Home Services: approximately 4 out of 5 stars from more than 14,600 reviews on Trustpilot, showcasing a more positive trend compared to the main retail brand.
  • Sears.com (retail): limited recent review activity, which mirrors the company’s significantly reduced retail operations.
  • BBB Rating: not accredited.

What Customers Are Saying

Positive Feedback: Many customers commend individual repair technicians for their professionalism and quality service.

Negative Feedback: Common complaints include long wait times for service appointments, challenges in warranty handling, and difficulty in reaching customer service by phone.

Important Considerations

  • Be clear about whether you’re engaging with Sears Home Services (which is relatively active) or the retail side (which has a much smaller presence) before setting your expectations.
  • Ensure that you receive your appointment window and any warranty terms in writing to avoid future misunderstandings.

Final Thoughts

Today, the Sears brand operates on a smaller scale than in its heyday, but Sears Home Services demonstrates a more robust and generally positive review record compared to its retail counterpart.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
