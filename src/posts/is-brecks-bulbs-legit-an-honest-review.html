Understanding Breck's Bulbs

Breck’s, based in Indiana, is a mail-order company that focuses on Dutch flower bulbs. The variety of customer experiences sheds light on what you might encounter when ordering from them.

Customer Feedback Overview

Reviews on platforms like Trustpilot reveal a spectrum of opinions, with approximately 101 reviews showing a mixed response, mirrored by a similar pattern of complaints noted on the Better Business Bureau (BBB) site. This suggests that potential buyers could face both positive and negative experiences when dealing with Breck’s.

Common Concerns and Praise

On the downside, many customers have expressed dissatisfaction, reporting that a small percentage of the bulbs they purchased actually grew, with some indicating germination rates as low as 10-30%. Additionally, issues such as lost orders, missing items in shipments, and unhelpful customer service have been highlighted. However, there are also numerous positive experiences; some customers commend Breck’s for timely deliveries and top-notch bulb quality, suggesting a significant variation in satisfaction levels.

Things to Keep in Mind

It’s important to note that Breck’s offers a satisfaction guarantee without a time limit on replacements or credits, making it wise to keep your order confirmation in case you need to utilize this guarantee due to poor germination. Furthermore, paying close attention to planting instructions based on your USDA hardiness zone is crucial, as improper planting can lead to disappointing results that might not be the retailer's fault.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
