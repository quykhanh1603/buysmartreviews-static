Understanding Breck's Bulbs

Breck’s is a mail-order company specializing in Dutch flower bulbs, based in Indiana. A close look at customer experiences provides insight into what you might expect.

Customer Feedback Overview

  • Trustpilot: about 101 reviews showing a mixed response.
  • BBB: a similar pattern of complaints is noted.

Common Concerns and Praise

Negative: Numerous customers mention that only a small percentage of the bulbs they purchased actually grew—some reported success rates as low as 10-30%. Other issues include lost orders, missing items in shipments, and unhelpful customer service interactions.

Positive: Conversely, there are customers who rave about timely deliveries and the quality of the bulbs they received, indicating that experiences may vary widely.

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Breck’s offers a satisfaction guarantee without a time limit on replacements or credits. It’s advisable to retain your order confirmation in case you need to utilize this guarantee due to poor germination.
  • Pay close attention to planting instructions according to your USDA hardiness zone, as improper planting can lead to issues that might unfairly reflect on the retailer.

Conclusion

There are genuinely mixed results reported by customers of Breck’s. Make sure to keep your receipt and consider using the satisfaction guarantee if you find the growth rates unacceptably low.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
