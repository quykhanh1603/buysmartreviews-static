UNTUCKit is a menswear brand built around shirts specifically designed to be worn untucked. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

UNTUCKit is a legitimate, recognizable brand, but its review record is one of the weaker ones we’ve seen in menswear, concentrated around customer service responsiveness.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 1.8–2.1 out of 5 across different regional sites.
  • SmartCustomer: around 1.4 out of 5 from a smaller sample.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Negative: the clearest and most concerning pattern is orders not arriving after extended periods with little to no customer service response — support described as unresponsive by email and phone in multiple reviews. Sizing consistency is also frequently cited.

Before you order

  • Order well ahead of any deadline given reported fulfillment delays.
  • Use a payment method with strong dispute protection (credit card) in case of an unresolved order issue.
  • Check current size-specific reviews before ordering, since fit consistency is a common complaint.

Bottom line

UNTUCKit is a legitimate, known brand, but its recent review record shows real customer-service risk. Order with payment protection and time buffer.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
