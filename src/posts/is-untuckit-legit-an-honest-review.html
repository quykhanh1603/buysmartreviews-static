Overview of UNTUCKit

UNTUCKit is a menswear brand focusing on shirts designed for an untucked look. While it's a recognizable name in casual fashion, the brand has faced challenges based on reviews from its customers.

Customer Ratings Breakdown

  • Trustpilot: approximately 1.8–2.1 out of 5 based on various regional scores.
  • SmartCustomer: about 1.4 out of 5 from a limited number of reviews.
  • BBB: currently not accredited.

Common Customer Feedback

Negative: A significant issue is that many customers report experiencing long delays in order delivery, often accompanied by a lack of communication from customer service. Multiple reviews highlight that support via email and phone has been unresponsive. Additionally, there are frequent mentions of sizing inconsistencies.

Things to Consider Before Ordering

  • Place your order well in advance due to noted fulfillment delays.
  • Utilize a payment method with robust dispute protection, like a credit card, to safeguard against potential order issues.
  • Review the latest size-specific feedback, as fit discrepancies are a common concern.

Final Thoughts

While UNTUCKit is a well-known brand, recent customer feedback raises concerns about their service reliability. It's advisable to order carefully, ensuring you have payment protections in place.
