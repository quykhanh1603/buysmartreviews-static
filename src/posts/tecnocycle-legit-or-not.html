A Trusted Source for Cycling Gear

Tecnocycle, based in Braga, Portugal, has been in operation since 2004, providing cycling enthusiasts with a range of top-quality products. As an official distributor for several prominent brands like Santa Cruz, DMT, and Focus, Tecnocycle is committed to offering authentic cycling gear and components to its customers.

Product Offerings

The company specializes in various cycling products, including:

  • Bikes: Tecnocycle features brands known for their performance and innovation, such as Focus bikes, which are designed for both road and gravel cycling.
  • Shoes: The DMT range includes performance cycling shoes that are highly regarded for their quality and comfort.
  • Accessories: From brakes to hydration systems, Tecnocycle offers essential components to enhance your cycling experience.

Focus on Quality and Authenticity

Tecnocycle emphasizes the importance of purchasing genuine products. They recommend that customers buy from authorized dealers to avoid counterfeit items, ensuring they receive high-quality gear. This focus on authenticity is crucial, especially in a market where imitations can sometimes be found at lower prices.

Customer Support and Service

Tecnocycle encourages customers experiencing issues with their bicycles to consult with local authorized dealers for repairs or inspections. This commitment to customer service ensures that cycling problems can be resolved effectively by experienced professionals.
