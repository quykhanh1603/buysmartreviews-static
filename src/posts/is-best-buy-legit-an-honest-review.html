Best Buy is the largest dedicated electronics retailer in the US, with hundreds of stores plus a large online catalog. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Best Buy is a large, legitimate, publicly traded company, but its independent review record shows meaningfully more dissatisfaction than its market position might suggest, concentrated in customer service.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: nearly 600 reviews, with most reviewers describing being let down by their experience.
  • BBB: profile covers headquarters and corporate-owned locations, with complaints about canceled orders.

Recurring themes

Negative: the most consistent complaint is customer service being difficult to reach or unhelpful once you do, along with reports of canceled orders where customers describe losing money in the process.

Positive: in-store Geek Squad and sales staff are more often cited favorably in individual, non-review-platform anecdotes, suggesting the online support channel is the weaker link.

Before you order

  • For a canceled order or refund issue, escalate quickly and keep all confirmation numbers and chat transcripts.
  • Consider in-store purchase and pickup for higher-value items to reduce shipping/cancellation risk.

Bottom line

Best Buy is a legitimate, major retailer. Its size means a large absolute number of complaints is expected; the online customer service experience is the area most worth managing proactively.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
