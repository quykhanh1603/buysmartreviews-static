Giant is one of the largest bicycle manufacturers in the world, selling a full range from road to e-bikes through a dealer network. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Giant is a legitimate, massive global manufacturer with a reasonably solid Trustpilot rating, though warranty and parts-availability complaints are common enough to plan around, especially for e-bikes.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4 out of 5 from over 1,200 reviews.
  • BBB: not accredited at the corporate level.

Recurring themes

Positive: bike quality and knowledgeable dealer staff are frequently praised.

Negative: warranty claims described as slow or denied in some cases, component durability concerns (particularly e-bike batteries), and difficulty sourcing replacement parts through some dealers.

Before you order

  • For e-bikes, ask your dealer directly about battery warranty terms and typical parts lead times before buying.
  • Keep your purchase and warranty registration documentation organized from day one.

Bottom line

Giant is a legitimate, major manufacturer with generally solid reviews. E-bike buyers in particular should ask about battery warranty and parts availability upfront.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
