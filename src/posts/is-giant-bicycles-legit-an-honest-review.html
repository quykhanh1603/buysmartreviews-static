Giant's Customer Approval Rating

With an impressive Trustpilot score averaging 4 out of 5 from over 1,200 reviews, Giant Bicycles shows a solid level of customer satisfaction.

Insights from Users

  • Trustpilot: A solid average of 4 out of 5 based on 1,200+ reviews.
  • BBB: Note that the company is not accredited at the corporate level.

Positive Aspects: Customers often praise the high quality of the bikes and the knowledgeable staff at local dealerships.

Common Concerns: Some issues raised include slow processing of warranty claims, occasional denials, durability concerns for components—particularly e-bike batteries—and finding spare parts from certain dealers.

Buying Tips

  • For those considering e-bikes, it’s crucial to discuss battery warranty specifics with your dealer and inquire about lead times for parts.
  • Keep all purchase and warranty registration documents organized to avoid potential issues down the line.

Conclusion

Giant is generally well-regarded in the biking community, but clarifying warranty and parts availability, especially for e-bikes, is essential.
