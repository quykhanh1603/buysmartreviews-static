Understanding REI's Reputation

As a major player in the outdoor and sporting goods market, REI offers everything from bicycles to camping gear. However, a closer look at customer feedback reveals a wide range of experiences.

Customer Feedback Snapshot

  • BBB: holds a D- rating due to failure to respond to 18 complaints.
  • Yelp: achieves a score of approximately 3.7 out of 5 based on over 17,000 reviews.
  • SmartCustomer: shows a lower score of about 1.9 out of 5 from a smaller group of users.

What Customers Are Saying

Complaints: Many customers report issues with order cancellations, delivery delays, and customer service challenges. Notably, there are complaints related to bicycles arriving with incorrect components.

Positive Experiences: In-store shopping tends to generate favorable reviews, especially with REI's membership dividend program, which aligns with its co-op ethos.

Tips for Shopping at REI

  • If you're purchasing a bicycle, it's a good idea to check it thoroughly in-store or right upon delivery to ensure all components are correct.
  • For online purchases, make sure to keep track of confirmation numbers and reach out quickly if issues arise.

Final Thoughts

While REI has a solid reputation in-store, their online presence and BBB rating leave something to be desired. For bicycle buyers, an in-person review of the product can help avoid potential problems.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
