REI is a large outdoor and sporting goods co-op retailer that sells bicycles alongside camping, hiking, and other outdoor gear. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

REI is a legitimate, well-known retailer, but ratings vary dramatically by platform — one of the wider gaps we’ve seen — so no single score tells the full story.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: a D- rating, citing failure to respond to 18 complaints filed.
  • Yelp: around 3.7 out of 5 from over 17,000 reviews.
  • SmartCustomer: around 1.9 out of 5 from a smaller sample.

Recurring themes

Negative: order cancellations, delivery issues, and customer service problems are the most cited complaints, including at least one bicycle-specific report of a bike arriving with incorrect components.

Positive: in-store shopping and membership dividend program are generally regarded favorably, consistent with REI’s long-standing co-op reputation.

Before you order

  • For a bicycle specifically, inspect it thoroughly in-store (or immediately on delivery) to confirm correct components before riding.
  • For online orders, keep confirmation numbers and follow up promptly if something seems off.

Bottom line

REI’s in-store reputation is generally stronger than its online/BBB record suggests. For bicycle purchases specifically, an in-person component check is a reasonable precaution.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
