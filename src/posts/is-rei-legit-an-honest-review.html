How Does REI Stack Up in Reputation?

When you’re considering where to shop for outdoor gear, REI stands out as a well-known name offering everything from bicycles to camping supplies. Yet, it’s important to dig a little deeper into customer experiences to understand what you might actually be getting into.

What Do Customers Think About REI?

  • BBB: holds a D- rating due to failing to address 18 complaints.
  • Yelp: boasts around 3.7 out of 5 stars from over 17,000 reviews.
  • SmartCustomer: reflects a lower score of about 1.9 out of 5 from a smaller group of users.

What Should You Look Out For?

Complaints: You might hear from customers who faced issues like order cancellations, delivery delays, and challenges with customer service. There are even cases where bicycles were delivered with missing or incorrect parts.

Positive Experiences: On the flip side, many people have enjoyed their in-store shopping experiences, especially with REI’s membership dividend program, which fits well with the co-op model they promote.

  • If you plan to buy a bicycle, definitely give it a thorough check in-store or right after it arrives to ensure everything's correct.
  • For online shopping, remember to keep track of your order confirmation numbers and don’t hesitate to reach out quickly if something goes awry.

In summary, while REI has a pretty solid reputation for in-store shopping, their online ratings and BBB status might make you think twice about ordering online. Checking out products in person, especially for larger items like bikes, could help you sidestep any unnecessary hassles.

Ratings mentioned here come from publicly available data at the time of writing and could change over time.
