Nordstrom's Dual Reputation

Nordstrom stands out as a prominent department store in America, offering a variety of women’s clothing and more, both in physical locations and through its online platform. However, there's a significant contrast between its in-store and online shopping experiences based on public reviews.

Understanding Customer Feedback

  • Trustpilot: More than 1,180 reviews, trending toward the negative side.
  • BBB: The company is not accredited and has garnered a customer review score of about 1.1 out of 5 on certain profiles.

Common Complaints

Negative Insights: Shoppers frequently report issues such as receiving damaged packages, theft due to improper delivery practices, and complications surrounding lost or cancelled orders. Customers have expressed frustration over the difficulty in resolving these issues with Nordstrom, even in cases involving police reports.

Positive Aspects: In contrast, the in-person shopping experience at Nordstrom tends to be highly rated, in line with the brand's longstanding reputation for excellent customer service on the sales floor.

Helpful Tips for Ordering

  • When purchasing high-value items, opt for options like signature-required delivery or in-store pickup instead of standard shipping, if available.
  • In case of delivery issues, document everything thoroughly—take photos of the packaging and, if theft is suspected, gather a police report to support your case.

Final Thoughts

While Nordstrom is esteemed for its in-store service, the online shopping experience does not always reflect this quality. For high-value purchases, consider picking them up in-store for added peace of mind.

The ratings mentioned reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
