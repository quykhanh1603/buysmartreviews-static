Nordstrom's Dual Reputation

With a disheartening customer review score of about 1.1 out of 5 on certain platforms, Nordstrom's online experience is under scrutiny. It's a stark contrast to its storied reputation for in-store shopping, where the experience generally receives high praise. This duality can leave potential buyers wondering what to expect when shopping with Nordstrom.

  • Trustpilot: More than 1,180 reviews, trending toward the negative side.
  • BBB: The company is not accredited and has garnered a customer review score of about 1.1 out of 5 on certain profiles.

Understanding Customer Feedback

Shoppers frequently report issues such as receiving damaged packages, theft due to improper delivery practices, and complications surrounding lost or canceled orders. Customers have expressed frustration over the difficulty in resolving these issues with Nordstrom, even in cases involving police reports.

Common Complaints

In contrast, the in-person shopping experience at Nordstrom tends to be highly rated, in line with the brand's longstanding reputation for excellent customer service on the sales floor. For those who prefer the online route, there are some precautions to consider.

Helpful Tips for Ordering

  • When purchasing high-value items, opt for options like signature-required delivery or in-store pickup instead of standard shipping, if available.
  • In case of delivery issues, document everything thoroughly—take photos of the packaging and, if theft is suspected, gather a police report to support your case.

While Nordstrom excels in its in-store service, the online shopping experience does not always reflect this quality. For high-value purchases, consider picking them up in-store for added peace of mind.
