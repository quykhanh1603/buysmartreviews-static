ASOS is a UK-based online fashion retailer with a large presence in the US market, selling both its own labels and third-party brands. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

ASOS is a legitimate, publicly traded retailer, but its review record shows consistent friction points worth knowing before you order, particularly around returns and account issues.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: a very large review volume, with a meaningful share of negative reviews.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Negative: dissatisfaction with the return policy, sizing/quality not matching the listing, delivery delays even on paid expedited shipping, and some reports of accounts being closed over disputed terms-of-service issues.

Before you order

  • Read the current return policy in full before ordering, since it’s a common source of complaints.
  • Keep order and account communication documented in case of a dispute.

Bottom line

ASOS is a legitimate, large fashion retailer, but return-policy and account-related complaints are common enough that it’s worth reading the fine print before you order, especially for larger purchases.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
