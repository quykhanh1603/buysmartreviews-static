What’s Going on with Rad Power Bikes?

It seems like Rad Power Bikes has hit a rough patch lately, which has raised some eyebrows about their reliability and customer service. After examining customer feedback and their recent company developments, it's worth discussing whether they're still a good choice for you.

How are Customers Feeling About Them?

  • Trustpilot: They’re sitting at a 1.5 out of 5 rating based on 175 reviews, and notably, they haven't received any 5-star ratings since December 2024.
  • BBB: They hold an A+ rating and are accredited, but keep in mind that the BBB rating doesn’t factor in customer reviews, which could be a red flag.

What Issues Should You Be Aware Of?

Many customers are complaining about water intrusion into the motor and battery compartments, which can lead to serious operational problems. Plus, if you're out of warranty, be prepared for some hefty repair costs, and several folks are reporting that getting help from customer service can be a major hassle.

Before you make any moves, it might be wise to double-check things like the company's current ownership, their warranty policies, and how available replacement parts are, especially since they’ve recently filed for bankruptcy. If you already own one of their bikes, make sure to keep track of any issues and verify if your warranty is still good under the new management.

The information shared reflects publicly available data at the time of writing. Given their recent bankruptcy, it’s really important to verify their current status directly before you commit to a purchase.
