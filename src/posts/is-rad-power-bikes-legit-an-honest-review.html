Understanding Rad Power Bikes' Current Situation

Rad Power Bikes operates as a direct-to-consumer electric bike brand. However, recent events have cast a shadow on its reliability and product support, prompting a closer look at customer feedback and company developments.

Customer Feedback Overview

  • Trustpilot: The company holds a rating of around 1.5 out of 5 based on 175 reviews, notably lacking any 5-star reviews since December 2024.
  • BBB: It has an A+ rating and accreditation—but bear in mind that the BBB scoring system overlooks customer review scores, which is important to note.

Common Issues Reported

Negative Insights: A recurring issue highlighted by customers involves water getting into the motor and battery compartments, leading to operational failures. Additionally, many users report high costs for out-of-warranty battery repairs and challenging experiences with customer service.

Advice for Potential Buyers

  • Before making any purchases, it’s crucial to verify the company's current ownership, warranty policies, and availability of parts in light of the recent bankruptcy.
  • If you already own a Rad Power bike, ensure you keep records of any issues and confirm if your warranty remains valid under the new ownership.

Final Thoughts

With a troubling review history coupled with the recent bankruptcy filing, we strongly recommend exercising caution with Rad Power Bikes until you can independently verify their warranty and operational status.
