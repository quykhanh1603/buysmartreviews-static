Rad Power Bikes is a direct-to-consumer electric bike brand. Here’s an honest look based on public review data — including a significant recent development.

Quick verdict

Unlike most brands on this site, we’d advise real caution here. Rad Power Bikes filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in December 2025; while the brand is reported to continue under new ownership, this materially changes the risk profile for warranty support on existing or new purchases, and we’d recommend independently confirming current operating and warranty status before buying.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 1.5 out of 5 from 175 reviews, with reportedly no 5-star reviews since December 2024.
  • BBB: A+ rating and accreditation (note: BBB’s letter grade doesn’t factor in customer review scores, which is a notable gap here).

Recurring themes

Negative: a specific, repeated design complaint involves water intrusion into the motor and battery compartment causing malfunctions, along with expensive out-of-warranty battery repairs and difficult customer service experiences.

What to do

  • Before buying, independently verify the company’s current ownership, warranty honoring policy, and parts availability given the bankruptcy filing.
  • If you already own a Rad Power bike, keep documentation of any issues and check whether your warranty is still being honored under new ownership.

Bottom line

Given the poor review record combined with a recent bankruptcy filing, this is one of the few brands on this site we’d actively caution against buying from until warranty and operating status are independently confirmed.

Information above reflects publicly available data at the time of writing. Given the reported bankruptcy, please verify current status directly before acting on anything here.
