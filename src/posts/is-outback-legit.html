Overview of Outback Plunge Pools

Outback specializes in high-quality plunge pools manufactured in Australia, ensuring that nearly 90% of their components are sourced locally. Their pools are made from durable materials like 316 stainless steel and COLORBOND® steel, making them suitable for various climate conditions. This focus on local craftsmanship not only supports the economy but also guarantees a faster response time for customers.

Variety of Sizes and Customization

When it comes to selecting a plunge pool, Outback offers over 21 different sizes. Customers can explore a 3D pool visualizer on their website, which allows users to customize their pool with different layouts, sizes, and color options. This flexibility means that whether you have a large backyard or a compact space, you can find a pool that fits your needs.

Durability and Maintenance

Outback pools are designed to be long-lasting. They utilize premium materials and are built to withstand the harsh Australian climate, minimizing the need for resurfacing and regular maintenance. The internal linings are made from Aqualux commercial-grade vinyl, which is resistant to chemicals and designed for longevity. Additionally, customers can choose between mineral water and freshwater options, promoting a lower-chemical approach to pool care.

Installation and Warranty Information

One of the key selling points of Outback plunge pools is their relatively quick installation time, typically taking between 6 to 10 weeks from order to delivery. Each pool comes with a 15-year structural warranty on the shell, providing peace of mind regarding the pool's durability. However, buyers should be aware that they are responsible for ensuring compliance with local council regulations, including obtaining necessary approvals and installing appropriate safety barriers.

Final Thoughts

Outback's plunge pools present a solid option for anyone considering a new swimming pool. Their commitment to Australian manufacturing, alongside a diverse range of customizable options and robust warranty, positions them as a trustworthy choice. However, potential buyers should factor in the additional costs of delivery and the need for compliance with local regulations.
