Understanding The Farmer’s Dog

The Farmer's Dog offers a subscription service that delivers fresh, human-grade dog food directly to your doorstep, catering to the desires of pet owners seeking healthier dietary options for their furry friends. This service has gained considerable traction due to its focus on quality ingredients and nutrition, making it a popular choice in the pet food industry.

Customer Feedback Overview

When it comes to customer satisfaction, The Farmer’s Dog holds an average rating of approximately 4 out of 5 stars on Trustpilot, based on over 1,600 reviews. Additionally, the Better Business Bureau has accredited the service since September 2024, reflecting a commitment to addressing customer concerns. Collectively, over 83% of around 2,800 reviews from platforms such as BBB, Trustpilot, and Google are positive, indicating a generally favorable reception among users.

What Customers Are Saying

Positive experiences from pet owners reveal that many dogs eagerly enjoy their meals, leading to noticeable benefits like improved digestion, better coat quality, and enhanced vitality. However, it's important to consider the other side of the coin. Some customers have reported gastrointestinal issues, including vomiting and diarrhea, shortly after beginning the food. There are also serious concerns raised about cases of pancreatitis among a few dogs. In addition, billing issues have surfaced, particularly regarding unexpected charges following attempts to cancel subscriptions.

Considerations Before You Order

For pet owners contemplating a switch, it’s recommended to transition gradually to a new dog food over a span of 5-7 days, mixing it with the current food to avoid potential digestive upset. Should any adverse symptoms occur, it’s wise to discontinue use immediately. Furthermore, given the recurring billing complaints, obtaining written confirmation of cancellation requests is paramount to prevent unanticipated charges.

While The Farmer’s Dog has built a reputation as a reliable source for dog food delivery, pet owners should remain vigilant due to reports of serious digestive issues.
