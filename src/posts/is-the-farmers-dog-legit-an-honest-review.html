The Farmer’s Dog is a fresh, human-grade dog food delivery subscription service. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

The Farmer’s Dog is a legitimate, well-regarded company with a large majority of positive reviews, but a specific and serious complaint pattern around digestive illness is worth knowing before you switch your dog’s diet.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4 out of 5 from over 1,600 reviews.
  • BBB: accredited since September 2024.
  • Over 83% of roughly 2,800 reviews across BBB, Trustpilot, and Google are reported positive.

Recurring themes

Positive: most customers report dogs eating enthusiastically, with improved digestion, coat, and energy over time.

Negative: a consistent, serious complaint pattern involves gastrointestinal illness — vomiting, diarrhea, and in some reported cases pancreatitis — appearing within days to weeks of starting the food. Billing complaints (charges after cancellation requests) also appear repeatedly.

Before you order

  • Transition your dog to any new food gradually (mixing with current food over 5-7+ days) to reduce digestive upset risk, and stop immediately if symptoms appear.
  • Confirm your cancellation went through with a written confirmation, given the billing complaints on file.

Bottom line

The Farmer’s Dog is a legitimate, well-reviewed company overall, but the digestive-illness complaint pattern is specific and serious enough to warrant a slow diet transition and close monitoring when starting.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication. This is not veterinary advice — consult your vet before changing your pet’s diet, especially if you notice any adverse symptoms.
