Understanding The Farmer’s Dog

The Farmer's Dog is a subscription service that provides fresh, human-grade dog food directly to your door. With its commitment to quality, it has garnered significant attention among pet owners looking for healthier meal options for their furry companions.

Customer Feedback Overview

  • Trustpilot: approximately 4 out of 5 stars from over 1,600 reviews.
  • BBB: accredited since September 2024.
  • Positive feedback makes up over 83% of around 2,800 reviews collected from BBB, Trustpilot, and Google.

What Customers Are Saying

Positive Remarks: Many users report that their dogs happily devour the meals, leading to improvements in digestion, coat quality, and overall vitality.

Concerns Raised: On the flip side, there is a notable pattern of complaints regarding gastrointestinal issues, such as vomiting and diarrhea, appearing shortly after starting the food. Some customers have even reported cases of pancreatitis. Additionally, there are recurring issues related to billing, particularly involving charges after cancellation attempts.

Considerations Before You Order

  • When transitioning to a new dog food, it's advisable to do so gradually over a period of 5-7 days by mixing it with your dog's current food to minimize the risk of digestive upset. Discontinue use immediately if any adverse symptoms occur.
  • Make sure to obtain written confirmation of your cancellation to avoid any unexpected charges, given the recurring complaints about billing.

Final Thoughts

The Farmer’s Dog has established itself as a credible choice for dog food delivery, with generally favorable reviews. However, the specific and serious reports of digestive complications suggest that pet owners should approach the switch with caution, monitoring their pets closely during the transition.
