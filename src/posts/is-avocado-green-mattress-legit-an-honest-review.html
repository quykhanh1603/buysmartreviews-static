Avocado Green Mattress is a brand focused on organic and natural materials. Here’s an honest look based on public review data — which shows a pattern worth taking seriously.

Quick verdict

Unlike some brands where a low Trustpilot score contrasts with a clean BBB record, Avocado’s ratings are consistently poor across both platforms — a stronger warning sign than the usual single-platform dip.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: rated “Bad,” around 1.3 out of 5, with the large majority of reviews being one-star.
  • BBB: customer review score around 1.07 out of 5 (unaccredited A+ letter grade, which reflects complaint-handling process rather than customer sentiment).

Recurring themes

Negative: orders not shipping with no clear timeline from support, incomplete orders, long unexplained delays, and difficulty getting replacements for defective mattresses (some reviewers report needing a formal BBB complaint to get resolution).

Before you order

  • Given the consistency of shipping-delay complaints, confirm current lead times directly with support before paying, and get an estimated ship date in writing.
  • Keep thorough records of your order and any communication in case you need to escalate.

Bottom line

Avocado’s ratings are weak across multiple independent platforms, not just one. If you order, get shipping timelines in writing and be prepared to follow up proactively.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
