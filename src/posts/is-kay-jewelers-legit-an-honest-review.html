Kay Jewelers is one of the largest mall-based jewelry chains in the US, part of Signet Jewelers. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Kay is a legitimate, massive retailer, but there’s a stark gap between its BBB accreditation and actual customer review sentiment — one of the more pronounced gaps we’ve seen.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: A+ rating (accredited), but a customer review score around 1.1 out of 5.
  • Trustpilot: around 2 out of 5, with over 80% of reviews being 1-star.

Recurring themes

Negative: metal discoloration shortly after purchase, warranty plans customers paid for being difficult to actually use, refusal on resizing requests, and complaints about insurance add-on charges being applied without clear consent.

Before you order

  • Read warranty/protection plan terms closely before purchasing one, and keep the paperwork.
  • Review your receipt line-by-line for any add-on charges (like insurance) before leaving the store.

Bottom line

Kay Jewelers is a legitimate, established retailer, but its BBB accreditation doesn’t reflect actual customer sentiment. Read every line of your purchase agreement carefully.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
