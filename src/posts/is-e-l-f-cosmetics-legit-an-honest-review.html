e.l.f. Cosmetics is a well-known US drugstore beauty brand built around affordable pricing. Here’s an honest look at how it holds up based on public review data — including a notable gap between two different sources.

Quick verdict

e.l.f. is a legitimate, established beauty brand — there’s no indication of fraud or scam activity. But there’s a real gap between its formal business accreditation and its day-to-day customer service reviews, which is worth understanding before you order directly from their website.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot (US site): rated “Poor,” around 2.4 out of 5.
  • BBB: A+ accredited, with only a handful of complaints on file, all reported as resolved.

These two numbers looking so different isn’t unusual — BBB accreditation reflects how a company handles formal complaints, while Trustpilot reflects broader day-to-day customer sentiment, including issues people never bother escalating to the BBB.

Recurring themes

Positive: reviewers who are happy consistently mention the formulas performing well for the price point, plus fast shipping when orders go smoothly.

Negative: the clearest pattern in negative reviews is customer service responsiveness and website/order issues, along with a share of reviewers reporting longer-than-expected shipping times.

Before you order

  • For a smoother experience, consider buying e.l.f. products through a major retailer (Target, Walmart, Ulta) rather than the brand’s own site, which sidesteps most of the shipping/customer-service complaints tied to direct orders.
  • If ordering direct, keep your order confirmation and be prepared to follow up proactively if you don’t hear back about an issue.

Bottom line

e.l.f. Cosmetics is a legitimate, established brand. The product itself has a solid reputation; most of the friction reported by reviewers is specifically about ordering direct from elfcosmetics.com rather than the makeup itself.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
