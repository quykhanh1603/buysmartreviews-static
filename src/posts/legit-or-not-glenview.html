About Glenview

Glenview has been a cornerstone in the development of Ottawa for over five decades. Established in 1966, this family-owned company specializes in owning, developing, and managing both commercial and residential real estate in the Ottawa region. The Shabinsky family has built a legacy in this sector, ensuring that the company maintains a personal touch in all its dealings.

What Sets Glenview Apart?

What really distinguishes Glenview is its commitment to in-house, personalized service. Unlike many larger real estate entities, Glenview prides itself on integrity and uncompromising quality, ensuring that each interaction feels direct and tailored to the client's needs. This personal approach is a hallmark of their business model, as they strive to foster strong relationships with their clients.

The Glenview Team

The Glenview team is composed of highly skilled professionals who bring years of experience and expertise to the table. With three generations of leadership from the Shabinsky family, the team combines knowledge and agility, making them responsive to the ever-changing market demands. This seamless blend of experience ensures that clients receive the best service possible.
