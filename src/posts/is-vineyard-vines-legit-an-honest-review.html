Vineyard Vines is a preppy American lifestyle brand known for its whale logo and New England-inspired designs. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Vineyard Vines is a legitimate, well-known brand with a genuinely mixed review record — strong loyalty from some longtime customers, alongside real complaints about recent quality and customer service.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 130 reviews, mixed.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Positive: some longtime customers describe consistently good experiences with both product and customer service, calling the brand “old school” in a positive sense.

Negative: other reviewers describe fabric and construction quality as not matching the price point, including reports of items (like a baseball cap) fading noticeably after light use, plus customer service that’s hard to reach via chat or email.

Before you order

  • Read recent, item-specific reviews before ordering, since experience appears to vary by product and possibly by manufacturing batch.
  • Use email for support requests and follow up if you don’t hear back within a few business days.

Bottom line

Vineyard Vines is a legitimate brand with a loyal following, but recent reviews are genuinely mixed on quality and support responsiveness. Worth checking recent, item-specific feedback before a larger order.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
