Quality Fencing and Decking Solutions

Lovewell Fence & Deck is recognized as the premier provider for fencing and decking solutions in Eastern Iowa and the Quad Cities. Established in 1973, this family-operated business has evolved into a trusted name in the industry, becoming employee-owned in 2023. With decades of experience, Lovewell has built a reputation for delivering high-quality, durable, and low-maintenance outdoor products.

Extensive Product Offerings

Lovewell offers a wide variety of fencing options to suit any residential or commercial need. Customers can choose from:

  • Vinyl Fencing: Durable and low-maintenance options are available, including styles like the Ellingwood white vinyl with black pickets.
  • Chain Link Fencing: Perfect for secure enclosures, available in black for a modern look.
  • Wood Fencing: Classic scalloped picket fencing enhances the charm of any property.
  • Aluminum Fencing: Elegant and resilient options for both contemporary and traditional aesthetics.

In addition to fencing, Lovewell provides various decking solutions, such as replacement vinyl decking that is designed to outlast traditional wood or composite options. Their decking services also include customized railings made from aluminum or vinyl to match your home's style. For those looking to enhance their outdoor space further, Lovewell installs traditional shade pergolas, modern pergolas, and decorative eyebrow pergolas for increased curb appeal.

Commitment to Customer Satisfaction

Lovewell Fence & Deck prioritizes customer service, offering free estimates both online and in-home to help potential clients assess their options without any pressure. Their installation teams are composed of highly trained professionals dedicated to ensuring that every project meets the highest standards. This customer-centric approach has helped Lovewell grow into the largest fence and deck company in Iowa, serving a wide geographical area that includes Cedar Rapids, Iowa City, and the surrounding Illinois Quad Cities.

Commercial Services and Expertise

Beyond residential projects, Lovewell also extends its services to commercial clients, offering solutions like chain link fencing for industrial areas, automated gate installations, and multi-family railings for apartments and senior living facilities. Their expertise in commercial fencing ensures that businesses can secure their properties effectively while maintaining aesthetic value.

Connect with Lovewell Fence & Deck

If you're considering enhancing your outdoor space with new fencing or decking, Lovewell Fence & Deck offers a streamlined process to get started. Their expert teams are ready to discuss your vision and provide tailored solutions. To get started, visit their website for a free estimate or contact one of their locations in Davenport or Cedar Rapids/Iowa City.
