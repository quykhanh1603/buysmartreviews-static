Overview of Brooks Brothers

With a rich legacy as one of the oldest menswear brands in America, Brooks Brothers is celebrated for its classic tailoring and quality dress shirts. However, recent customer feedback raises some questions about the shopping experience.

Customer Impressions

  • Trustpilot: approximately 179 reviews indicate mixed feelings.
  • Yelp: holds a rating of around 3.3 out of 5 based on over 1,800 reviews.

What Customers Appreciate

Positive Aspects: Many customers highlight the excellent fabric selection and praise the brand's non-iron shirts as top-notch.

Areas of Concern

Negative Feedback: A notable number of reviewers point out that customer service appears to have declined. Issues with refund processing and inconsistency in quality—especially with certain knitwear items—also feature prominently in reviews.

Tips for Shoppers

  • When receiving your order, take extra care to inspect knitwear and sweaters due to noted durability issues.
  • Document all orders and refund requests, as there have been delays reported in processing.

Final Thoughts

While Brooks Brothers continues to be a recognized and legitimate brand in menswear, customers should be ready for potentially slower service than what the brand's reputation might promise.

The ratings mentioned above are based on publicly available data at the time of writing and could change over time.
