Brooks Brothers is one of the oldest menswear brands in America, known for classic tailoring and dress shirts. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Brooks Brothers is a legitimate, historic brand with a well-regarded core product, but reviewers consistently describe a decline in customer service quality worth being aware of.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 179 reviews, mixed.
  • Yelp: around 3.3 out of 5 from over 1,800 reviews.

Recurring themes

Positive: the fabric selection and the brand’s non-iron shirt finish are frequently singled out as best-in-class.

Negative: customer service is repeatedly described as having declined, refund processing frustrates a meaningful share of reviewers, and some reviewers note quality inconsistency on specific items (e.g., sweaters developing holes quickly).

Before you order

  • Inspect knitwear and sweaters closely on arrival given the specific durability complaints noted.
  • Keep your order and any refund request documented given reported processing delays.

Bottom line

Brooks Brothers remains a legitimate, historically strong menswear brand with well-regarded core products, but be prepared for slower customer service than the brand’s reputation might suggest.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
