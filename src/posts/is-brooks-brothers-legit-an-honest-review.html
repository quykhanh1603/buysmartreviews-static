Surprising Customer Service Issues

Despite its rich history as one of America's oldest menswear brands, Brooks Brothers has seen troubling feedback regarding its customer service, with many customers feeling let down.

Customer Sentiments

  • Trustpilot: roughly 179 reviews show a mix of satisfaction and dissatisfaction.
  • Yelp: has a rating of about 3.3 out of 5 from more than 1,800 reviewers.

What Customers Like: Many shoppers rave about the quality fabric choices, highlighting the non-iron shirts as particularly impressive.

Concerns Raised: Nevertheless, numerous customers report that customer service has faltered, mentioning issues with refund processes and inconsistent quality in certain knitwear items.

  • Inspect knitwear and sweaters carefully upon arrival due to reported durability concerns.
  • Keep a record of all orders and refund requests, as delays have been noted.

Bottom Line

While Brooks Brothers is a legitimate brand, its recent customer service reputation is concerning.
