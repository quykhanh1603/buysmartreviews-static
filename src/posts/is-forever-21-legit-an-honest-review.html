Forever 21 is a fast-fashion retailer targeting younger shoppers, with both physical stores and an online presence. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Forever 21 is a legitimate, established retailer, but its BBB record is notably weak, and combined with Trustpilot complaints, this is one of the lower-confidence brands in the clothing category.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 1,300 reviews, mixed to negative.
  • BBB: an F rating, citing failure to respond to a large number of filed complaints.

Recurring themes

Negative: packages with no tracking movement and refund refusals, a $9.99 return fee some customers found frustrating, clothing quality that some describe as ripping easily, and limited return/exchange flexibility on some items.

Before you order

  • Understand the current return fee and policy before ordering, particularly for online purchases.
  • Keep tracking numbers and order confirmations in case a package stalls in transit.

Bottom line

Forever 21 is a legitimate retailer, but its weak BBB complaint-response record is a real yellow flag. Budget-price fast fashion, with return terms worth reading carefully.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
