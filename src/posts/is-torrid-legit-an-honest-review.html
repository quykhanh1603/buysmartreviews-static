Is Torrid Trustworthy?

Torrid has a mixed reputation, which means shoppers should exercise caution when purchasing online. Customer feedback highlights both positive experiences in-store and challenges with online orders.

Customer Feedback Summary

Torrid specializes in women’s plus-size clothing and operates both online and through physical locations. Approximately 500 reviews on Trustpilot reflect a blend of experiences, with some positive remarks about in-store service, where many customers appreciate friendly staff and helpful interactions. However, it's important to note that commonly reported issues include lengthy customer service hold times, difficulties with order cancellations, receiving incorrect items, and delays in refunds. Furthermore, the Better Business Bureau indicates that Torrid is not accredited, which may raise some flags for potential customers.

Ratings mentioned here are based on publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since then.
