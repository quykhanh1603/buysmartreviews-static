A Closer Look at Torrid

Torrid specializes in women’s plus-size clothing and operates both online and through physical locations. While it's a recognized name in this niche, a dive into customer feedback reveals some important insights.

Customer Feedback Overview

  • Trustpilot: Approximately 500 reviews, showing a mix of positive and negative experiences.
  • BBB: The company is not accredited.

Common Customer Experiences

Challenges: Many customers report issues such as lengthy hold times when contacting customer service, difficulties with order cancellations, receiving incorrect items, and delays in receiving refunds.

Positive Aspects: On a brighter note, shoppers often highlight enjoyable in-store experiences, citing friendly and helpful staff.

Things to Consider Before Buying

  • If you have a Torrid store nearby, it might be worth shopping in person, given the disparity between online and in-store reviews.
  • For online purchases, make sure to keep your order confirmation numbers accessible and consider following up via email if any issues arise, as this provides a documented record of your interactions.

Final Thoughts

While Torrid is a legitimate retailer with a solid customer base, shoppers may find that online orders come with some complications. Visiting a local store could provide a more satisfying shopping experience.
