Newegg is a long-running US online retailer specializing in computer hardware and consumer electronics. Here’s an honest look at its reputation based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Newegg is a well-established, legitimate electronics retailer with a strong reputation in the tech/PC-building community. Ratings vary somewhat by source, but the overall picture is positive.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: reported ratings vary depending on the source and time checked, generally landing in the high-3s to mid-4s out of 5.
  • BBB: holds an A- rating.

Recurring themes

Positive: long-time customers frequently mention competitive pricing, a wide product catalog, and reliable support — several reviewers describe years of purchases without major issues.

Negative: a smaller share of reviews mention shipping delays, product condition on arrival, and occasional mismatches between listed specs and what actually arrives.

Before you order

  • Double-check third-party marketplace sellers on Newegg’s platform versus items sold and shipped directly by Newegg — experience can differ between the two.
  • Read the exact product listing (not just the title) for specs, especially on components like RAM, storage, or GPUs where small differences matter.
  • Keep your order confirmation email in case you need to reference exact SKU/spec details in a dispute.

Bottom line

Newegg is a legitimate, established electronics retailer that’s generally reliable, especially for buyers already familiar with PC components. As with most large marketplaces, paying attention to whether you’re buying from Newegg directly or a third-party seller matters.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
