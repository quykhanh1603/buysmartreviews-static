Chubbies is a casual apparel brand known for its shorts and playful branding, founded in 2011. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Chubbies is a legitimate, established DTC brand with a modest but mixed review record, primarily around shipping speed and support responsiveness.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: a relatively small review sample, mixed.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Positive: product quality and fit/feel of the clothing are frequently praised by satisfied customers.

Negative: shipping that runs slower than the stated timeline, and customer service described as slow to respond (long chat queues, automated responses) when an order needs attention.

Before you order

  • If you need an item by a specific date, order with a meaningful buffer given reported shipping delays.
  • Try email over chat for support requests if you don’t get a timely response through one channel.

Bottom line

Chubbies is a legitimate casual-apparel brand. Product feedback is generally positive; shipping timeline and support responsiveness are the areas to plan around.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
