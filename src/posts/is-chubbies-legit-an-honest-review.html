Chubbies Lacks BBB Accreditation

Founded in 2011, Chubbies has gained recognition for its casual wear, particularly their popular shorts and upbeat branding. Yet, despite its growing presence in the direct-to-consumer apparel market, the absence of Better Business Bureau accreditation may raise concerns about its legitimacy.

Customer Feedback Overview

The sentiment from customers about Chubbies reflects a wide range of experiences.

  • Trustpilot: There are limited reviews that reveal a mix of positive and negative feedback.
  • BBB: The brand is not accredited, highlighting a potential gap in customer trust.

What Customers Appreciate: Many praise the quality and comfort of the clothing, emphasizing the favorable fit and feel as major selling points.

Common Complaints: On the flip side, customers frequently mention shipping delays as a significant issue, noting that items can take longer than anticipated to arrive. Additionally, some have reported challenges with customer service, experiencing slow response times through both chat and automated replies.

Shopping Tips

  • To avoid delays, order ahead of time, especially if you have a specific deadline to meet.
  • If you need support and encounter delays in chat responses, consider reaching out via email for potentially quicker assistance.

Final Verdict

While Chubbies offers quality products, the lack of BBB accreditation and reported service issues warrant caution for potential buyers.
