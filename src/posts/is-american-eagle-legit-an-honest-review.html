American Eagle Outfitters is a well-known American casual clothing retailer, founded in 1977. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

American Eagle is a legitimate, long-established company, but its review record (where available) skews notably negative, concentrated around returns and order accuracy.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: A- rating, not accredited, with unanswered complaints noted on file.
  • Trustpilot: a smaller review sample, skewing negative.

Recurring themes

Negative: incorrect items shipped, refusals to send replacements, receiving wrong quantities, and return/refund policy frustration are the most cited issues.

Before you order

  • Check your shipment against your order confirmation immediately, and report discrepancies right away.
  • Read the current return policy before ordering, particularly for sale items.

Bottom line

American Eagle is a legitimate, established retailer, but order-accuracy and return complaints appear often enough in available reviews to warrant careful order-checking.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
