Understanding American Eagle Outfitters

Founded in 1977, American Eagle Outfitters has become a staple in American casual clothing. While it is a reputable retailer, a closer look at customer feedback reveals some concerns.

Customer Feedback Overview

  • BBB Rating: A- rating, although it is not accredited and has several complaints that remain unanswered.
  • Trustpilot: The review landscape here is smaller but leans negatively.

Common Issues Raised by Customers

Key complaints: Customers frequently report issues such as receiving incorrect items, problems with replacement requests, receiving wrong quantities, and dissatisfaction with the return and refund policies.

Important Considerations Before Buying

  • Immediately verify your shipment against your order confirmation and alert the company to any discrepancies.
  • Familiarize yourself with the current return policy, especially for discounted items.

Final Thoughts

While American Eagle is a legitimate retailer, the prevalence of complaints regarding order accuracy and the return process suggests that shoppers should double-check their orders carefully.
