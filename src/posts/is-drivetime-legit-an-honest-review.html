DriveTime is a used-car dealer chain known for financing buyers with limited or poor credit history. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

DriveTime is a legitimate, large dealer network, well-suited to its core purpose (financing access), but vehicle quality complaints show up often enough to warrant a careful pre-purchase inspection.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: nearly 11,000 reviews, with reported averages ranging from around 3.6 to 4 out of 5 depending on when the data was pulled.
  • BBB: A+ rating, though with 788 complaints logged in the past three years.

Recurring themes

Positive: reviewers who needed financing with a limited credit history frequently cite DriveTime as one of few options that approved them.

Negative: the clearest complaint pattern involves vehicles developing significant repair issues shortly after purchase, in some cases described as safety-related.

Before you buy

  • Get an independent pre-purchase inspection if DriveTime’s return window allows it.
  • Read the specific terms of any as-is warranty or limited warranty included with your purchase before signing.
  • Budget for a maintenance/inspection buffer given the frequency of early-repair complaints in the review record.

Bottom line

DriveTime is a legitimate dealer serving a real gap in the market (financing for buyers with limited credit), but vehicle condition complaints are common enough that a careful inspection before or immediately after purchase is worth the effort.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
