Understanding DriveTime's Position

In the realm of used car dealerships, DriveTime has carved out a niche by primarily serving individuals with limited or poor credit histories. This focus allows them to provide financing solutions where many traditional lenders might turn away potential buyers, making them a significant player in the market.

Customer Feedback Overview

When it comes to customer feedback, DriveTime has garnered a mixed bag of reviews. On Trustpilot, they have nearly 11,000 reviews with average ratings that oscillate between approximately 3.6 and 4 out of 5. Their standing on the Better Business Bureau (BBB) is notable as well, holding an A+ rating, although it’s essential to be aware of the 788 complaints that have been registered over the last three years.

What Customers Are Saying

Feedback from customers presents a spectrum of experiences. On the positive side, many users appreciate that DriveTime is one of the few dealerships willing to finance buyers with challenging credit backgrounds, thus filling a vital service gap for many. On the flip side, a recurring concern among several customers is the issue of vehicles facing significant repair problems shortly after the purchase. Some of these issues have raised safety alarms, indicating potential risks for buyers.

Important Considerations

While DriveTime serves a crucial market by helping those who need it most, potential buyers should proceed with caution. It is advisable to arrange for an independent pre-purchase inspection when considering a vehicle, especially if it aligns with DriveTime's return policy timeline. Additionally, reviewing the terms of any as-is warranty or limited warranty is crucial before finalizing a purchase. Given the feedback regarding frequent repair needs, allocating a budget for potential maintenance or inspections could be a wise move.

The ratings and information provided above are based on publicly available data as of the time of writing and could have changed since.
