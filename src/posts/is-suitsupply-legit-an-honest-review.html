Suitsupply is a menswear retailer known for well-fitted, affordably priced suits sold through showrooms and online. Here’s an honest look based on public review data — including a caveat about its rating.

Quick verdict

Suitsupply’s core product (the suits themselves) is consistently well-regarded, but Trustpilot itself has flagged a possible review-solicitation issue with the company, so treat the very high headline score with some caution.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: a very high rating from over 12,300 reviews — but Trustpilot has noted the company may be requesting reviews in a way that doesn’t fully comply with its guidelines, which can skew results.

Recurring themes

Positive: suit quality relative to price and in-store staff experience are consistently praised by reviewers.

Negative: the clearest complaint pattern involves online returns and refunds — some customers report sending items back but not receiving a refund, plus difficulty reaching support by phone or email.

Before you order

  • If possible, buy in-store or use in-store fitting even for an online order, since staff experience is more consistently praised than the online support channel.
  • Keep your return shipment tracking number and confirmation, and follow up proactively if a refund doesn’t post within the stated window.

Bottom line

Suitsupply’s product is well-regarded, but given Trustpilot’s own note about the company’s review-solicitation practices, weigh the headline rating a bit more skeptically and pay close attention to the return process if ordering online.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
