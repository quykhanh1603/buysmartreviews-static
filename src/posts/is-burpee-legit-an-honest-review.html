Burpee is one of the oldest and best-known seed and plant companies in the US. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Burpee is a legitimate, historic company, but its review record shows consistent complaints about product performance and customer service worth planning around.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 23,500 reviews, with a consistent pattern of complaints.
  • BBB: not accredited, with unanswered complaints on file.

Recurring themes

Negative: low germination rates on some seed orders, damaged or undersized plants on arrival, slow or missing customer service responses, and billing for orders that were never shipped.

Positive: some customers report reliably good products and fast delivery on other orders — experience appears to vary by order/season.

Before you order

  • Order seeds and plants early in the season rather than waiting, given reported shipping delays during peak demand.
  • Keep your order confirmation and follow up on any charge that isn’t matched by a shipping notification.

Bottom line

Burpee is a legitimate, long-established seed company, but germination and fulfillment complaints are common enough that ordering early and tracking your order closely is worthwhile.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
