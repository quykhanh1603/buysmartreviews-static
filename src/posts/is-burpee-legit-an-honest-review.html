A Look at Burpee's Reputation

Burpee stands out as one of the oldest and best-known seed and plant companies in the U.S., but how well does it meet the expectations of today’s consumers? We dove into the available reviews to get a clearer picture.

Customer Feedback Overview

  • Trustpilot: More than 23,500 reviews show a notable trend of complaints regarding product quality.
  • BBB: The company is not accredited and has multiple unresolved complaints listed.

Common Complaints and Praise

Negative Experiences: Many customers report issues such as low germination rates, receiving damaged or undersized plants, slow responses from customer service, and even being charged for orders that never shipped.

Positive Notes: On the flip side, a segment of customers has had positive experiences, citing good product quality and timely delivery on certain orders. It seems that the experience may vary significantly based on factors like timing and specific products.

Tips for Preparing Your Order

  • Consider placing your orders for seeds and plants early in the season to avoid the shipping delays often reported during peak demand times.
  • Always keep your order confirmation handy and follow up on any charges that don’t correspond with a shipping notification.

Final Thoughts

While Burpee enjoys a long-standing reputation in the seed and plant market, the prevalence of germination issues and fulfillment complaints suggests that consumers should be proactive when ordering. Planning ahead and closely monitoring your order can enhance your buying experience.
