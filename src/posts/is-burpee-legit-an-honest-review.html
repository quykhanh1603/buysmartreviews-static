Burpee Has Over 23,500 Reviews with a Trend of Complaints

As one of the oldest and most recognized seed and plant companies in the U.S., Burpee is not without its controversies. The sheer volume of customer feedback reveals that many have faced issues that may leave potential buyers wary.

Customer Experience Insights

  • Trustpilot: A massive 23,500 reviews on this platform indicate a notable trend of dissatisfaction, particularly regarding product quality.
  • BBB: Burpee is not accredited by the Better Business Bureau and has multiple unresolved complaints on record, which raises flags for potential customers.

Negative Feedback: Many consumers express frustrations over low germination rates, receiving damaged or undersized plants, and inadequate customer service responses. Just as concerning, some reported being charged for orders that never shipped.

Positive Experiences: While negative feedback predominates, a portion of customers share positive experiences, noting satisfactory product quality and timely deliveries. Their experiences appear to hinge on specific products and timing.

  • To improve your chances of a smooth order process, consider placing your orders early in the season to avoid common delays during high-demand periods.
  • It’s wise to keep your order confirmation accessible and be proactive in following up on any charges that appear without corresponding shipping notifications.

In Summary

Despite Burpee's long-standing reputation, its recurring issues with germination and order fulfillment suggest caution is advisable for consumers.

The ratings mentioned reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
