Nike is one of the largest athletic footwear and apparel brands in the world, selling direct via Nike.com alongside its retail partners. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Nike is a massive, legitimate, publicly traded company, but its direct-to-consumer online store has a notably weak independent review record, concentrated in customer service and returns.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 12,000 reviews, skewing negative.

Recurring themes

Negative: customer service described as very difficult to reach or resolve issues through, order fulfillment and delivery delays, refund process complaints, and billing errors (being charged for returned items) are the most cited issues.

Before you order

  • Keep your return tracking number and confirmation, and follow up if a refund doesn’t post promptly.
  • Consider ordering through a major retail partner (rather than Nike.com directly) if the retailer’s own customer service has a stronger reputation in your experience.

Bottom line

Nike is a legitimate, massive brand, but its direct online store’s customer service and returns process draw consistent complaints. Document your order and follow up proactively if something goes wrong.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
