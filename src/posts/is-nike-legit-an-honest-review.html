Assessing Nike's Trustworthiness

Nike is generally trustworthy, but potential buyers should be aware of some concerning customer feedback regarding its online store. As one of the largest and most recognized athletic footwear and apparel brands globally, Nike has built a substantial market presence through its own website, Nike.com, and various retail partners.

Insights into Customer Experience

Despite being a legitimate and publicly traded company, the feedback surrounding Nike's direct-to-consumer online store tells a different story. More than 12,000 reviews on Trustpilot indicate a negative trend, highlighting several common complaints. Many customers have experienced difficulties reaching customer service for support, delays in order fulfillment and deliveries, and issues with the refund process, including being charged for items that were returned. If you're considering a purchase from Nike.com, it's wise to stay proactive with documentation, such as saving your return tracking number and confirmation, as this could be crucial if refunds don't show up in a timely manner. For those who have had better experiences with a different retailer's customer service, it might be worth considering purchasing through them instead.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
