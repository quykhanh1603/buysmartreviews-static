Morphe's Trustpilot Rating is Alarmingly Low

Morphe's average rating on Trustpilot is a disappointing 1.8 out of 5, raising serious questions about their customer service and shipping practices. Despite being known for their vibrant eyeshadow palettes and makeup brushes, their reputation is tarnished by numerous negative experiences reported by customers.

Evaluating Customer Feedback

  • Trustpilot (US site): rates a dismal “Poor,” averaging between 1.8 and 1.9 out of 5.
  • Yelp: holds a better score of approximately 3.8 out of 5, based on a smaller pool of just 86 reviews.

In comparison, Morphe's UK site has an average rating around 3.1/5, indicating that the customer experience can vary greatly depending on the region.

Issues Customers Commonly Face

Negative feedback: Customers frequently mention long shipping delays, lack of tracking information, missed delivery deadlines, incomplete orders, and slow responses from customer service, particularly from the US site.

Shopping Recommendations

  • It's wise to order with a generous buffer time considering the ongoing shipping issues reported.
  • If you need Morphe products by a specific date, purchasing from a major retailer like Ulta or Target might be a more reliable option than ordering directly from the US site.

In Summary

Ordering directly from Morphe's US site comes with significant risks regarding shipping delays.
