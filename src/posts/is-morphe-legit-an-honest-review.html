Morphe is a color cosmetics brand known for eyeshadow palettes and makeup brushes. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Morphe’s US site has one of the weaker review records of any beauty brand we’ve checked, concentrated heavily in shipping reliability.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot (US site): rated “Poor,” around 1.8–1.9 out of 5.
  • Yelp: around 3.8 out of 5 from a much smaller sample (86 reviews).

Interestingly, Morphe’s UK site rates noticeably better (around 3.1/5 “Average”) than its US site — a reminder that service quality for the same brand can differ by region/fulfillment operation.

Recurring themes

Negative: long shipping delays with limited tracking updates, unmet delivery timeframes, incomplete orders, and slow customer service responses are the most cited issues on the US site specifically.

Before you order

  • Order with significant buffer time given the consistent shipping-delay pattern reported on the US site.
  • Consider buying Morphe through a major retailer (Ulta, Target) rather than the brand’s own US site if you need it by a specific date.

Bottom line

Morphe’s product itself has a reasonable reputation, but ordering direct from the US site carries real shipping-delay risk based on current reviews. A retailer purchase may be more reliable for time-sensitive needs.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
