Understanding Morphe's Reputation

Morphe is a color cosmetics brand known for eyeshadow palettes and makeup brushes. However, its review history reveals some notable concerns, especially regarding shipping and customer service.

Customer Ratings Breakdown

  • Trustpilot (US site): rates a dismal “Poor,” averaging between 1.8 and 1.9 out of 5.
  • Yelp: holds a better score of approximately 3.8 out of 5, based on a smaller pool of just 86 reviews.

A comparison with Morphe's UK site shows an average rating of around 3.1/5, indicating a significant variance in customer experience based on location.

Common Complaints

Negative feedback: Customers frequently mention long shipping delays, lack of tracking information, missed delivery deadlines, incomplete orders, and slow responses from customer service, particularly from the US site.

Recommendations for Shopping

  • It's wise to order with a generous buffer time considering the ongoing shipping issues reported.
  • If you need Morphe products by a specific date, purchasing from a major retailer like Ulta or Target might be a more reliable option than ordering directly from the US site.

Final Thoughts

While Morphe's products are generally well-regarded, ordering directly through the US site poses a legitimate risk of shipping delays. For urgent needs, shopping through a reputable retailer could offer a more dependable experience.

The ratings mentioned reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
