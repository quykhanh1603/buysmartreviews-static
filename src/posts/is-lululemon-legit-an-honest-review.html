Lululemon is a premium athletic apparel brand known for leggings, activewear, and its Align and Define product lines. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Lululemon is a legitimate, well-established brand with a large loyal following, though a number of recent reviewers report the product feeling less consistent than it used to be at this price point.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 1,270 reviews, mixed.
  • Yelp: around 3.5 out of 5 from nearly 12,000 reviews.

Recurring themes

Positive: the Align and Define lines specifically are frequently praised for fabric feel and durability, with reviewers noting minimal pilling even after repeated washing.

Negative: some reviewers report inconsistent sizing between styles, seam or pilling issues (especially reported on Align leggings), occasional sheerness complaints, and a general sense among some long-time customers that quality has slipped somewhat as prices have risen.

Before you order

  • Check recent reviews for the specific product line/color, since quality complaints seem concentrated in particular styles or batches rather than across the board.
  • Take advantage of in-store try-on where available, since sizing consistency is the most cited issue.

Bottom line

Lululemon is a legitimate, well-regarded brand with genuinely loyal customers. If ordering online, check recent, style-specific reviews given some reports of inconsistent quality.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
