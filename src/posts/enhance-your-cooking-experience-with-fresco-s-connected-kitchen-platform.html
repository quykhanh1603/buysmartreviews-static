What is Fresco?

Fresco is a groundbreaking platform tailored for appliance brands, designed to enhance the cooking experience through connected technologies. By merging artificial intelligence with cooking, Fresco not only simplifies the process for home cooks but also enables brands to foster enduring relationships with their customers. This platform integrates appliances, valuable content, and intelligence into a cohesive system, making cooking more manageable and enjoyable.

Key Features of the Fresco Platform

The Fresco platform comprises three integral layers that work together to create a superior cooking experience:

  • AI Cooking Companion: This guided experience assists users across various appliances and applications, providing support and enhancing confidence in the kitchen.
  • KitchenOS: Serving as the foundational technology, KitchenOS facilitates rapid launches of connected cooking experiences while minimizing complexity for engineering teams.
  • Connected Kitchen Ecosystem: This framework connects different brands’ appliances, allowing users to experience seamless cooking across various devices, regardless of brand.

Why Choose Fresco?

Leading appliance brands opt for Fresco for several compelling reasons:

  • Connected Kitchen by Default: Instead of building solutions for a singular brand, Fresco encourages a collective ecosystem that maximizes user engagement.
  • Speed Without Compromise: Fresco's onboarding process allows appliance brands to get up and running in less than two weeks, a significant advantage in the competitive appliance market.
  • Cost Efficiency: Brands can reduce research and development costs by up to five times, enabling more resources for innovation.
  • Data-Driven Insights: The platform provides valuable cooking insights that help brands make informed product decisions and enhance customer satisfaction.

Proven Effectiveness

Fresco has demonstrated its capability at scale, supporting global appliance brands with notable statistics:

  • Offers support in 18 different languages.
  • Maintains a 50% retention rate after one year, indicating strong customer loyalty.
  • Facilitated the exploration of over 12 million recipes in just three years.
  • Achieves an 80% activation rate for appliances, showcasing its effectiveness in engaging users.

Conclusion

Fresco stands out in the connected kitchen space, providing appliance brands with an effective platform to engage home cooks. With its innovative approach to cooking technology, fast onboarding, and extensive support capabilities, Fresco is an ideal partner for brands looking to thrive in a rapidly evolving market.
