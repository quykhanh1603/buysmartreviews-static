Understanding MyClean's Services

Founded in 2009, MyClean is a cleaning service based in New York City, dedicated to providing professional house and apartment cleaning. With a strong reputation, they have garnered over 3,652 verified 5-star reviews, indicating widespread customer satisfaction. MyClean stands out by employing trained professionals as W-2 employees rather than gig workers, which sets the tone for accountability and quality in service.

Key Features of MyClean

MyClean distinguishes itself through several standout features:

  • Consistent Cleaning Team: Each home is cleaned by the same team, ensuring familiarity with your preferences and spaces.
  • Transparent Pricing: They offer straightforward pricing with no hidden fees, starting from $92 for a standard cleaning. Payment is easy with card transactions, including tips.
  • Performance Tracking: MyClean utilizes a proprietary app to generate a Cleaner Score after each visit, providing real-time performance data that informs their operations and ensures high-quality service.
  • Recurring Clean Options: Customers can schedule one-time or recurring cleans, maintaining a consistently clean environment.

Types of Cleaning Services Offered

MyClean offers a variety of cleaning options to cater to different needs:

  • Standard Clean: Starting at $92.00, this service focuses on high-traffic areas and common surfaces.
  • Enhanced Essentials: For $140.00, this option provides additional attention to detail and commonly used areas.
  • Deep Cleaning: Ideal for thorough seasonal cleaning, starting at $318.00, this service includes cleaning hard-to-reach areas and additional tasks like cleaning the fridge and oven.
  • Moving Clean: Also starting at $318.00, this service ensures a spotless environment when moving in or out.

Why Choose MyClean?

With a commitment to excellence, MyClean has established itself as a reliable cleaning service in NYC. Their approach focuses on treating cleaners as professionals, leading to satisfied customers who appreciate the quality and consistency of service. Reports from customers often highlight the attention to detail and the personalized nature of the service, enhancing overall satisfaction.

Customer Support and Scheduling

MyClean provides convenient online booking and live support. Customers can schedule cleanings to fit their busy lives, with real-time updates and reminders. Whether you need to reschedule or have questions, their support team is readily available to assist, ensuring a seamless experience from start to finish.

Final Thoughts

For residents of New York City seeking a dependable and high-quality cleaning service, MyClean offers a compelling option. With experienced staff, exceptional customer service, and transparent pricing, they have built a strong reputation in the industry. If you value consistent quality and professional service, MyClean is certainly worth considering.
