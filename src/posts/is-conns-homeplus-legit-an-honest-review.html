The End of an Era

After 134 years of operation, Conn’s HomePlus is no longer in business. This prominent retailer, which offered a variety of home goods, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in July 2024 and officially closed its doors on October 31, 2024. Currently, there are no plans for a relaunch or online-only operation; it exists solely as a bankruptcy entity.

Customer Feedback: What the Record Shows

  • Trustpilot: Across more than 400 reviews, feedback was predominantly negative, highlighting issues that existed even before the company's closure.
  • BBB: The Better Business Bureau had suspended Conn’s accreditation, indicating significant concerns over their business practices.

Common Complaints Identified

Before its closure, several patterns emerged in customer complaints, including:

  • High interest rates associated with financing options.
  • Aggressive collection practices for missed payments.
  • Frequent reports of products breaking shortly after purchase, with warranty claims often denied.
  • Allegations of sharing personal information with credit agencies without proper consent.

What This Means for Former Customers

If you had an account or warranty with Conn’s HomePlus:

  • Look out for official communications from the bankruptcy estate detailing how to file potential claims.
  • If you financed a purchase through Conn’s credit, be sure to find out who is currently servicing that account, as they are usually sold to third-party companies after a retailer shuts down.

Final Thoughts

Conn’s HomePlus is definitively closed as of late 2024. This review aims to inform anyone still searching for Conn's regarding its current status, rather than to recommend continued patronage.

Information above reflects publicly available data at the time of writing. Company status can be confirmed via recent news coverage or bankruptcy court records.
