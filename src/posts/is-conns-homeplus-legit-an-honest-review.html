Closure of Conn's HomePlus

After 134 years of service, Conn's HomePlus has officially closed, filing for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in July 2024 and ceasing operations on October 31, 2024. Currently, there are no plans for a relaunch or online-only operation; it exists solely as a bankruptcy entity.

  • Trustpilot: Over 400 reviews with predominantly negative feedback.
  • BBB: Conn's accreditation suspended due to significant concerns.

Customer Feedback Overview

In the lead-up to its closure, the feedback from customers highlighted several persistent issues with Conn's HomePlus. The predominant sentiment fell far from favorable, with many customers expressing frustrations about their experiences long before the company filed for bankruptcy.

Understanding the Implications

For those who had accounts or warranties with Conn’s HomePlus, here are a few considerations:

  • Watch for official communications from the bankruptcy estate regarding potential claims.
  • If you financed a purchase, determine who is servicing that account, as these are often sold to third-party companies.

This review aims to clarify Conn's current status for anyone still looking for information, rather than suggesting ongoing patronage.
