Conn’s HomePlus was a furniture, electronics, and appliance retailer that had operated for 134 years. Here’s an important update before anything else: this company is no longer in business.

Quick verdict

Conn’s HomePlus filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in July 2024, and by October 31, 2024, every remaining location had closed. As of our research, there has been no announced relaunch, online-only pivot, or franchise revival — the company exists only as a bankruptcy shell. If you’re researching this name, you likely can’t place a new order here, and existing warranty or gift card claims may not be honored.

What the review record showed (historical)

  • Trustpilot: over 400 reviews, overwhelmingly negative even before the closure.
  • BBB: accreditation was suspended, not accredited.

Recurring themes (historical)

Prior to closure, common complaints included high interest rates and aggressive collections on missed payments, products breaking shortly after purchase with warranty claims denied, and reports of personal information being shared with credit agencies without clear consent.

If you have an existing Conn’s account or warranty

  • Check for official communication from the bankruptcy estate about how to file a claim, rather than trusting unofficial sources.
  • If you financed a purchase through Conn’s in-house credit, confirm who currently services that account, since accounts are often sold to a third-party servicer after a retailer closes.

Bottom line

Conn’s HomePlus is fully closed as of late 2024. This entry exists to help anyone still searching for the name understand its current status rather than to recommend shopping there.

Information above reflects publicly available data at the time of writing. Company status can be confirmed via recent news coverage or bankruptcy court records.
