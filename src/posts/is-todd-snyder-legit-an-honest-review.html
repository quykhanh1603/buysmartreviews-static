Todd Snyder is a menswear designer brand known for classic essentials with a modern twist, plus custom suiting. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Todd Snyder has a solid overall rating for a menswear brand, though a meaningful minority of reviews point to responsiveness issues when something goes wrong with an order.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4 out of 5 from over 210 reviews.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Positive: quality, timely shipping, and design are consistently praised by the majority of reviewers.

Negative: a smaller set of reviews describe receiving defective or missing items paired with unresponsive customer service when trying to resolve it.

Before you order

  • Check your order against the packing slip on arrival, and reach out promptly (with photos, if defective) if something’s wrong.
  • Keep order confirmation numbers handy in case you need to escalate a support request.

Bottom line

Todd Snyder has one of the better overall ratings in menswear. The main risk is what happens if your specific order has an issue, given some unresponsive-support reports.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
