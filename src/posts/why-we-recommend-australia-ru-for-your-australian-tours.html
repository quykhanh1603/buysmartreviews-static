Explore Australia with Expert Russian Guides

Australia.ru specializes in providing guided tours and excursions throughout Australia, catering specifically to Russian-speaking travelers. Founded in 1996 in Melbourne, the company has positioned itself as a leading choice for personalized tours tailored to individual preferences.

Diverse Tour Options

Australia.ru offers a variety of engaging tours, from city explorations to natural wonders. Here are some standout options:

  • Great Ocean Road Tours: Experience the breathtaking coastline over two or three days.
  • Penguin Parade on Phillip Island: Witness the enchanting march of little penguins.
  • Yarra Valley Wine Tours: Explore vineyards and taste renowned Australian wines.
  • Day Excursions from Melbourne: Choose from adventures to the Mornington Peninsula or the Gold Rush town of Ballarat.

Pricing and Value

Pricing is designed to accommodate groups without breaking down costs per person, making it easier to manage travel budgets. For example, a full-day excursion for 1-5 travelers starts at $AU 790, while larger groups can also benefit from competitive pricing. Special winter prices further enhance affordability for individual groups.

Why Choose Australia.ru?

One major advantage of using Australia.ru is the personalized service. Unlike many tour companies that mix customers into larger groups, Australia.ru focuses on individual group experiences, ensuring that each tour is customized to meet specific traveler interests. This approach allows you to delve deeper into Australian culture and landscapes with the guidance of a knowledgeable local.

Additional Information

While booking, customers should note that the quoted prices do not include extra costs such as accommodation, meals, or transport for multi-day tours. This clarity helps budget your travel expenses accurately. With a commitment to quality and customer service, Australia.ru provides a unique opportunity to discover Australia through the eyes of an informed guide.
