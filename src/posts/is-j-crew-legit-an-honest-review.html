J.Crew is a well-known American clothing retailer offering both men’s and women’s collections. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

J.Crew is a legitimate, long-established retailer, with ratings that vary widely by platform — worth checking more than one source.

What customer ratings show

  • Sitejabber: around 1.7 out of 5 from 133 reviews.
  • Yelp: around 3.3 out of 5 from over 1,300 reviews.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Negative: customer service and credit card billing issues are the most specifically cited complaints, along with some quality concerns on specific items like cashmere sweaters.

Positive: reviewers who didn’t hit a service issue generally rate the clothing quality favorably.

Before you order

  • Check your billing statement against your order after purchase, given the frequency of billing-related complaints.
  • Read recent reviews for the specific item/material (e.g., cashmere) rather than the brand overall.

Bottom line

J.Crew is a legitimate, established retailer. Ratings vary a lot by platform, so cross-checking before a larger purchase is worthwhile.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
