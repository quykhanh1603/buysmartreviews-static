Abercrombie & Fitch is a well-known American casual clothing retailer with a large network of stores. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Abercrombie & Fitch is a legitimate, large, established retailer, with complaints concentrated specifically around refund timing and inventory accuracy rather than the core product.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 500+ reviews, mixed.
  • BBB: profile covers headquarters and corporate-owned locations across 600+ stores.

Recurring themes

Negative: the most specific complaint pattern is refunds taking months with repeated delays, and items shown as in-stock online turning out to be backordered after purchase.

Positive: product feedback itself (fit, style) is more mixed-to-positive when no fulfillment issue is involved.

Before you order

  • If refund timing matters to you, be aware this is the single most cited complaint — consider in-store returns where possible for a faster resolution.
  • Double check stock status directly with a store if an online item shows as available but you need it urgently.

Bottom line

Abercrombie & Fitch is a legitimate, established retailer. Most complaints center on refund speed and stock accuracy rather than the clothing itself.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
