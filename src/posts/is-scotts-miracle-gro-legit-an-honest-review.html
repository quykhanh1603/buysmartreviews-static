Scotts Miracle-Gro is one of the largest lawn and garden product manufacturers in the US, sold through major retailers and its own site. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Scotts is a legitimate, massive manufacturer, though available review data is thinner than most brands we cover — the clearest documented complaints involve specific product effectiveness issues rather than shipping or fraud concerns.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: not accredited at the corporate level.
  • Independent review platform data for the brand directly is more limited than for pure e-commerce retailers, since most Scotts products are purchased through third-party stores rather than scotts.com.

Recurring themes

Negative: BBB complaints include reports of specific weed-control products damaging or killing desirable plants (roses, hibiscus, hydrangea) even when used according to label instructions, and difficulty reaching a live customer service line.

Before you use their products

  • Read product labels carefully regarding which plants are sensitive to a given herbicide, and consider spot-testing on a small area first.
  • If you experience unexpected plant damage, document it with photos and your product lot number before contacting support.

Bottom line

Scotts is a legitimate, massive manufacturer. Since most purchases happen through retailers rather than direct, the main risk to manage is product-specific label compliance rather than an ordering/shipping concern.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
