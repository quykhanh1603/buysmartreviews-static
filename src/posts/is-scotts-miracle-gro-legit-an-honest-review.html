Understanding Scotts Miracle-Gro

Scotts Miracle-Gro stands out as one of the largest manufacturers of lawn and garden products in the United States, available at major retailers as well as through their own website. However, the customer feedback landscape presents a mixed picture.

What the Ratings Indicate

  • BBB Status: The company is not accredited by the Better Business Bureau.
  • Data from independent review platforms is more limited compared to pure e-commerce sites, likely due to the distribution of products mainly through third-party retailers.

Common Complaints

Negative Feedback: A recurring theme in customer complaints is the effectiveness of certain weed-control products, which have reportedly caused damage to desirable plants like roses and hibiscus, even when the instructions are followed. Additionally, some users have expressed frustration in reaching customer service.

Tips Before Using Their Products

  • Pay close attention to product labels, particularly concerning which plants may be sensitive to particular herbicides. It’s wise to test on a small area first.
  • If you encounter any unexpected damage, make sure to document it with photos and your product lot number prior to reaching out for support.

Final Thoughts

While Scotts Miracle-Gro is definitely a legitimate and prominent manufacturer, it's essential to manage risks associated with specific product usages rather than concerns over ordering or shipping.

The ratings mentioned above are based on publicly available data at the time of this writing and may have changed since then.
