Madewell is a denim-focused women’s clothing brand, part of the same parent company as J.Crew. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Madewell is a legitimate, established brand with a well-regarded core product (jeans and denim in particular), but review platforms show real friction in customer service and refund handling.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 2.2 out of 5.
  • Yelp: around 3.7 out of 5 from over 1,700 reviews — notably more favorable.
  • BBB: an A letter grade, not accredited.

Recurring themes

Positive: the clothing itself — especially jeans — is frequently rated highly by reviewers who didn’t run into a service issue.

Negative: customer service and billing/credit card issues, along with shipping problems on accessories like handbags and difficulty getting refunds processed.

Before you order

  • Keep your order confirmation and any billing statements handy in case of a credit card discrepancy.
  • For higher-value items (bags, outerwear), track shipping closely.

Bottom line

Madewell’s product itself is well-regarded, but service and refund-related friction shows up often enough in reviews to warrant careful order tracking.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
