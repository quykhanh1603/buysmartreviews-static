What Should You Know About Madewell?

Madewell is a popular women’s clothing brand known for its denim, and it shares a parent company with J.Crew. But how does it stack up in the eyes of customers?

Is Madewell Worth Your Money?

  • Trustpilot: around 2.2 out of 5.
  • Yelp: approximately 3.7 out of 5 based on over 1,700 reviews, indicating more positive feedback here.
  • Better Business Bureau (BBB): holds an A rating, though it's not accredited.

What Are Customers Saying?

Positives: Many shoppers love the quality of Madewell's clothing, especially their jeans, provided they don’t run into issues with customer service.

Negatives: Common problems people mention include customer service complaints, billing issues, shipping delays—particularly with accessories like handbags—and struggles with refunds.

Keep in mind that the ratings mentioned reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
