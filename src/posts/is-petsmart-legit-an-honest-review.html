Trustworthiness of PetSmart

PetSmart has a mixed reputation, making it important for consumers to do their homework before making a purchase or visiting a store. While they are a legitimate player in the pet supply industry, experiences can vary significantly based on location.

Customer Ratings Overview

On Trustpilot, PetSmart has amassed over 1,800 reviews that highlight a blend of opinions. Many customers commend their experiences, particularly with grooming services and helpful staff at certain locations. However, there are notable criticisms regarding animal care, product advice, and issues with shipping and order fulfillment for online purchases. It’s wise to read reviews specific to your nearest store, as experiences can differ widely based on local management and resources.

Overall, checking customer feedback for your specific location can provide valuable insights into what you can expect from your experience at PetSmart.

The ratings mentioned above are based on publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
