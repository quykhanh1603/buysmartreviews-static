PetSmart is one of the largest pet supply retailers in the US, competing directly with Petco on both products and in-store services. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

PetSmart is a legitimate, large retailer with a genuinely mixed review record, similar in pattern to its main competitor.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 1,800 reviews, mixed.

Recurring themes

Positive: some reviewers specifically praise positive grooming experiences and helpful staff at particular locations.

Negative: concerns about animal treatment/care in some stores, product recommendation quality, and shipping/order fulfillment issues on online purchases are the most cited complaints.

Before you order or book a service

  • Check reviews for your specific local store, since experience appears to vary significantly by location.
  • For grooming appointments, ask about specific policies for handling anxious or reactive pets before booking.

Bottom line

PetSmart is a legitimate, major retailer with experience varying a lot by location. Checking your specific local store’s reviews is more useful here than the brand-wide average.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
