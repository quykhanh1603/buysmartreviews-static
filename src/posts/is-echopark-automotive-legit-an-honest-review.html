A Closer Look at EchoPark Automotive

EchoPark Automotive operates as a used-car retail chain under Sonic Automotive, specializing in late-model vehicles. While the dealership has made strides in the market, it has received a mix of reviews that potential buyers should consider.

Mixed Customer Experiences

Public feedback about EchoPark reveals a varied landscape: many customers express satisfaction, while others share significant concerns. Here’s a breakdown:

  • Trustpilot: approximately 500 reviews with mixed sentiments.
  • BBB: The company runs over 19 locations, with complaint trends mirroring those seen on Trustpilot.

Key Complaints and Highlights

Negative: Notable issues include undisclosed vehicle faults (such as transmission problems), reported delays in vehicle registration that can exceed two months, and unclear mandatory add-on packages costing around $699 which are not always transparent during the sale.

Positive: Customers who were pleased with their experience often commend the knowledgeable, low-pressure approach of the sales staff.

Important Tips for Potential Buyers

  • Be clear about any mandatory add-on packages included in the price, and request a detailed price breakdown before you sign.
  • Consider an independent pre-purchase inspection, especially for critical components like the transmission.
  • Get a written confirmation of the expected registration timeline to avoid any surprises.

Final Thoughts

In summary, EchoPark Automotive is a legitimate player in the used car market, but buyer vigilance is essential. Addressing common complaints by asking detailed questions about fees and securing an independent vehicle inspection can lead to a better buying experience.
