EchoPark Automotive is a used-car retail chain (part of Sonic Automotive) focused on late-model used vehicles. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

EchoPark is a legitimate, established dealer chain with a genuinely mixed review record — some clearly satisfied buyers, and some serious, specific complaints worth knowing about.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 500 reviews, mixed.
  • BBB: operates 19+ locations with a BBB presence; complaint themes are consistent with the Trustpilot record.

Recurring themes

Negative: the clearest complaint patterns are vehicles failing inspections for issues (like transmission problems) that weren’t disclosed at time of sale, registration delays reported to run over two months in some cases, and an add-on package reported to cost around $699 being applied to sales without always being made clear upfront.

Positive: reviewers who had a good experience frequently mention knowledgeable, low-pressure sales staff.

Before you buy

  • Ask explicitly whether any mandatory add-on packages are included in the price, and get an itemized breakdown before signing.
  • Get an independent pre-purchase inspection, particularly checking the transmission and other major components.
  • Confirm expected registration timeline in writing.

Bottom line

EchoPark is a legitimate dealer chain with a mixed track record. Asking pointed questions about add-on fees and getting an independent inspection addresses the two most common complaint categories.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
