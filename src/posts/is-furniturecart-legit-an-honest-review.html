Understanding FurnitureCart's Reputation

FurnitureCart is an online retailer based in the US that focuses on discounted brand-name furniture and home accessories. Evaluating their reputation can help you decide if they are worth your time and money.

Customer Feedback Overview

  • Trustpilot: approximately 4 out of 5 from around 1,000 reviews.
  • BBB: not accredited, with a profile that includes some complaints regarding order timelines.
  • ResellerRatings: averages around 3 out of 5 from a smaller pool of feedback, highlighting the variability in ratings across platforms.

What Shoppers Are Saying: Customers have given mixed feedback on their experience with FurnitureCart. Positive comments include the careful packaging of deliveries, prompt communication after order confirmation, and a tendency for customers to make repeat purchases, especially for bedroom furniture sets. However, a notable downside is the occasional delays related to backorders and insufficient updates on item availability. Some shoppers have suggested that FurnitureCart may operate like a drop-shipper, meaning longer wait times for certain products.

Key Considerations Before Making a Purchase

  • Check current stock levels and lead times directly with customer support before committing to a purchase, particularly for larger items.
  • Ensure that you receive written confirmation of delivery windows and the return policy.
  • Opt for payment via credit card instead of a direct bank transfer to maintain options for disputing charges should any issues arise.

While FurnitureCart seems to be a legitimate option, their drop-ship model can lead to unpredictable order fulfillment timelines.

The ratings mentioned reflect publicly available data as of the time of writing and may have changed since.
