FurnitureCart.com is a US-based online retailer selling brand-name furniture and home accessories at discounted prices. Here’s an honest look at whether it’s worth ordering from, based on publicly available customer feedback.

Quick verdict

FurnitureCart appears to be a legitimate, established online furniture retailer, but with more inconsistent order fulfillment than some competitors. Order timing and communication matter more here than with a typical big-box retailer.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: rated around 4 out of 5 across roughly 1,000 reviews.
  • BBB: not accredited; profile includes some complaints about order timelines.
  • ResellerRatings: a noticeably lower average (around 3/5) on a smaller sample of reviews — a reminder that ratings can vary a lot depending on which platform you check.

Recurring themes

Positive: customers frequently mention well-packaged deliveries, helpful communication once an order is confirmed, and repeat purchases (particularly bedroom furniture sets).

Negative: the most common complaint is backorder delays combined with slow updates on availability. Some reviewers allege the company operates more like a drop-shipper — relaying orders to manufacturers rather than holding its own inventory — which can explain longer, less predictable lead times on certain items.

Before you order

  • Confirm current stock/lead time directly with support before paying, especially for larger items.
  • Get delivery windows and return policy in writing.
  • Pay by credit card rather than direct bank transfer so you have dispute options if something goes wrong.

Bottom line

FurnitureCart looks legitimate rather than a scam, but the drop-ship-style model reported by some customers means lead times can be unpredictable. Reasonable for patient buyers; less ideal if you need furniture on a tight timeline.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
