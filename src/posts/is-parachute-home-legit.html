Experience the Comfort of Percale Sheets

Parachute Home specializes in creating high-quality bedding and home essentials, and their Percale Sheet Set has become a standout product in their collection. Crafted in Portugal from 100% premium, certified long-staple Egyptian cotton, these sheets are designed to provide a cool and crisp sleeping experience. The percale fabric is lightweight yet durable, ensuring you not only get comfort but also longevity from your purchase.

Key Features of the Percale Sheet Set

The Percale Sheet Set includes a fitted sheet, a top sheet, and a pillowcase. The sheets are available in a range of colors, including classic white, cream, and dusk, allowing you to customize your bedroom aesthetic. Additionally, you can choose from various sizes such as Twin, Queen, King, and California King to fit your bed perfectly.

One of the most appealing features is the breathable nature of the fabric, making these sheets particularly suitable for those who tend to get hot during the night. With a soft and smooth texture, they promise to enhance your sleep quality while feeling cool against the skin. Plus, these sheets only get better with time, offering increasing comfort with each wash.

Pricing and Value

Pricing for the Percale Sheet Set ranges from $239 to $299, depending on the size you choose. While this price point may be higher than budget options, the quality of the materials and craftsmanship justifies the investment. Furthermore, Parachute Home frequently offers bundle discounts, allowing you to save 15% when purchasing multiple bedding items at once.

Shipping and Return Policies

Parachute Home offers free shipping on all orders, making it straightforward for you to receive your products without extra costs. They ship via UPS Ground for most orders and USPS for those to PO Box addresses. If you're in a hurry, you can opt for faster shipping for a fee of $40 or 15% of your total order amount, whichever is greater.

When it comes to returns, Parachute Home provides a generous 60-day return policy. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you can return your sheets by mail, at a retail location, or via a Happy Returns Bar. Refunds are issued to the original payment method, although it's important to note that shipping and handling fees are nonrefundable.

Their offerings are designed to enhance your home experience, making you feel more at ease in your living space. Overall, Parachute Home seems to deliver on its promises, making it a legitimate choice for discerning shoppers.
