Micro Center is a computer and electronics retailer known for large physical superstores popular with PC builders, alongside an online store. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Micro Center is a legitimate, well-regarded retailer, particularly for in-store shopping and PC components, though technical support and repair services draw more mixed feedback.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4 out of 5, generally positive.
  • ResellerRatings: around 4.2 out of 5 from a very large sample (over 84,000 reviews).
  • PissedConsumer: around 2 out of 5 (note: this platform specifically attracts dissatisfied customers, so treat it as a complaint log rather than a representative average).

Recurring themes

Positive: in-store pricing, selection, and knowledgeable staff are consistently praised, especially by PC-building customers.

Negative: repair and technical support services draw more specific complaints than product purchases themselves.

Before you order

  • If you’re near a physical location, in-store shopping appears to be the stronger experience based on available reviews.
  • For repair services specifically, confirm turnaround time and get a written estimate before authorizing work.

Bottom line

Micro Center is a legitimate, generally well-regarded retailer, especially in-store. Its repair/technical-support arm is the area with the most inconsistent feedback.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
