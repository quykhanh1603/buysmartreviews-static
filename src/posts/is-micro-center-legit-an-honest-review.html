What's the Micro Center shopping experience like?

Micro Center is well-known for its impressive selection of computers and electronics, both in-store and online. This retailer has garnered a diverse range of customer feedback that can help you decide if it's worth your time and money.

How do customers rate Micro Center?

  • Trustpilot: scores hover around 4 out of 5, showing generally positive sentiments.
  • ResellerRatings: boasts an average of about 4.2 out of 5 from over 84,000 reviews, suggesting a solid customer base.
  • PissedConsumer: has a lower average of around 2 out of 5, which is common for platforms that capture more negative experiences.

What are the pros and cons of shopping there?

Positive Comments: Many shoppers rave about Micro Center's competitive prices, wide range of products, and friendly, knowledgeable staff—particularly useful for those into PC building.

Negative Feedback: On the flip side, customers often express dissatisfaction with the repair and technical support services, which can leave a sour taste compared to their overall shopping experience.

Overall, Micro Center stands out as a credible retailer, especially for in-store visits. Just keep in mind the mixed reviews surrounding their repair services, which might require a cautious approach.

The ratings mentioned above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and are subject to change.
