Understanding DSW's Reputation

DSW (Designer Shoe Warehouse) is a prominent shoe retailer owned by Designer Brands Inc., featuring hundreds of physical stores and the convenience of online shopping. Let's delve into what customer experiences reveal about this retailer.

Customer Ratings Overview

  • App Store: Approximately 4.9 out of 5 based on more than 770,000 reviews.
  • BBB: Roughly 1 out of 5 from a smaller sample, and the business is not accredited.
  • Sitejabber: About 1.8 out of 5.

The Good and the Bad

Negative Feedback: Customers frequently report issues like order cancellations, shipping delays, and receiving incorrect items, alongside complaints about slow customer service.

Positive Feedback: The app itself receives high ratings, indicating that most shoppers enjoy a smooth purchasing experience — the issues seem to arise primarily when orders go awry.

What to Keep in Mind

  • Verify your size and product details before finalizing your order; many reviews highlight problems with incorrect sizes or items.
  • Save your order confirmations, as they may be necessary for resolving issues related to cancellations or delays.

Final Thoughts

DSW is a legitimate retailer with a solid app rating indicating that many transactions go well. However, when problems do occur, resolution can be slower than some customers would like.

The ratings mentioned reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
