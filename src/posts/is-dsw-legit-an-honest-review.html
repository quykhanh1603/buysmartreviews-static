DSW (Designer Shoe Warehouse) is a large shoe retailer owned by Designer Brands Inc., with hundreds of physical stores plus online ordering. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

DSW is a legitimate, real retailer with genuine brand partnerships — not a scam — but there’s a striking gap between its mobile app rating and its Trustpilot/BBB scores worth understanding.

What customer ratings show

  • App Store: around 4.9 out of 5 from over 770,000 reviews.
  • BBB: around 1 out of 5 from a much smaller sample, not accredited.
  • Sitejabber: around 1.8 out of 5.

Recurring themes

Negative: order cancellations, shipping delays, and wrong items shipped are the most cited complaints on review platforms, plus customer support delays.

Positive: the app-based shopping experience itself is rated very highly, suggesting most transactions go smoothly and the negative reviews concentrate around the subset of orders that go wrong.

Before you order

  • Double-check size and item details before confirming your order, since “wrong item/size” is a frequently cited complaint.
  • Keep order confirmations handy in case you need to dispute a cancellation or delay.

Bottom line

DSW is a legitimate, real retailer. Most transactions appear to go fine (per the very high app rating), but when something goes wrong, review platforms suggest resolution can be slow.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
