What’s the Deal with DSW’s Reputation?

If you're looking to shop at DSW (Designer Shoe Warehouse), you’re stepping into a well-known shoe retailer, part of Designer Brands Inc. With tons of physical locations and online shopping options, you might be curious about what actual customers think of them.

How Do Customers Rate DSW?

  • App Store: Around 4.9 out of 5 based on over 770,000 reviews.
  • BBB: A low score of 1 out of 5 from a smaller sample, and keep in mind that they’re not accredited.
  • Sitejabber: About 1.8 out of 5.

What Should You Know About the Good and the Bad?

Negative Feedback: Many customers voice frustrations about order cancellations, shipping delays, and sometimes even incorrect items. Plus, slow customer service responses can add to the hassle.

Positive Feedback: On the flip side, the DSW app has excellent ratings, showing that for many shoppers, the purchasing process is smooth. It seems like the issues really crop up when there are problems with orders.

Things to Keep in Mind:

  • Make sure to double-check your size and product details before you hit that checkout button; many reviews mention problems in this area.
  • Hang onto your order confirmations, as they’ll be useful if you hit any snags with cancellations or delays.

In short, DSW has a solid reputation overall based on app ratings, but if something goes wrong, you might find it takes longer than you'd like to sort it out.

Keep in mind that these ratings reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and could change.
