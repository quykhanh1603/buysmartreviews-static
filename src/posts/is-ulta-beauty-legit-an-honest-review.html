Overview of Ulta Beauty

Ulta Beauty is a major player in the U.S. beauty market, known for its vast range of products and in-store salon services. However, customer experiences reveal a mix of satisfaction and frustration.

Customer Ratings Breakdown

  • Trustpilot: more than 2,100 reviews posted.
  • Reviews.io: holds a rating of roughly 2.82 out of 5 from 490 reviews.

Common Issues Reported

Negative Feedback: Customers frequently report challenges with refunds and reshipping items, as well as missing products. Service failures have been noted across various channels including online, phone, chat, and in-store interactions. Additionally, there are notable complaints related to appointment cancellations for salon services.

Tips for Shopping or Booking a Service

  • Always keep your order confirmation and any communication records handy in the event you need to follow up on a refund.
  • If you're scheduling a salon service, it’s wise to double-check your appointment with the store a day before to ensure everything is set.

Final Thoughts

While Ulta Beauty is indeed a legitimate retailer, the frequent complaints regarding refunds and service issues suggest that consumers should document their transactions carefully.
