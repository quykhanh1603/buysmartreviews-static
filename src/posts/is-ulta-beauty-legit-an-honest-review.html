Ulta Beauty is one of the largest beauty retailers in the US, combining a wide product selection with in-store salon services. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Ulta is a legitimate, large retailer, but review platforms show a consistent pattern of complaints around refunds and multi-channel service failures.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 2,100 reviews.
  • Reviews.io: around 2.82 out of 5 from 490 reviews.

Recurring themes

Negative: refusal to refund or reship items in some reported cases, missing products, and service failures reported across online, phone, chat, and in-store channels. For salon services specifically, appointment cancellation complaints appear as well.

Before you order or book a service

  • Keep your order confirmation and any chat/email transcripts in case you need to escalate a refund dispute.
  • For salon appointments, confirm the booking directly with the store a day ahead if the service is important to you.

Bottom line

Ulta is a legitimate, major retailer, but refund and multi-channel service issues appear often enough in reviews to keep thorough documentation of any order problem.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
