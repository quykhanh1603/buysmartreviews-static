Is Ulta Beauty a good choice for your beauty needs?

When considering where to buy your beauty products, Ulta Beauty comes to mind as a big player in the U.S. market. They offer a wide range of products along with in-store salon services, but you'll find a mix of customer experiences that might give you pause.

What do customers really think about Ulta Beauty?

  • Trustpilot: more than 2,100 reviews posted with varied opinions.
  • Reviews.io: has an average rating of around 2.82 out of 5 based on 490 reviews.

What issues should you be aware of?

It’s not all sunshine and roses; many customers have reported issues with refunds and getting their items reshipped. There are also complaints about missing products and service failures when trying to resolve issues online, over the phone, or even in-store. If you plan to use their salon services, keep in mind that appointment cancellations have been mentioned quite a bit as well.

To make shopping or scheduling a service easier, hold onto your order confirmation and any communication records—you may need them if something goes wrong. And if you’re booking a salon appointment, double-check with the store the day before to confirm everything is still on track.

Overall, while Ulta Beauty is a legitimate retailer, it might be wise to proceed with caution given the frequency of complaints regarding refunds and service. Make sure you keep track of your transactions!
