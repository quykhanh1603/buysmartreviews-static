Men’s Wearhouse is a large suit and formalwear retailer, known for both purchase and rental services. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Men’s Wearhouse is a legitimate, large, established retailer, with complaint patterns concentrated around alterations quality and tuxedo rental policy communication — worth extra attention if you’re renting for an event.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 270 reviews, mixed.
  • BBB: a business profile covering 324+ locations, with complaints on file.

Recurring themes

Positive: some reviewers specifically praise knowledgeable in-store staff for fittings.

Negative: tuxedo/rental return policy communication described by some as unclear or bordering on deceptive, alterations issues, and delayed orders — particularly concerning for time-sensitive events like weddings.

Before you order

  • For rentals tied to a specific event date, confirm the exact return deadline and any late fees in writing well ahead of time.
  • Schedule alterations with enough buffer before your event in case a follow-up fitting is needed.

Bottom line

Men’s Wearhouse is a legitimate, established retailer, but if you’re renting for a time-sensitive event, get all policy details in writing well in advance.
