Glossier is a direct-to-consumer beauty brand known for minimalist, “no-makeup makeup” products. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Glossier is a legitimate brand with generally well-regarded products, but customer service responsiveness and its return/business-model policies draw specific criticism.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 365 reviews, mixed.
  • BBB: not accredited, with complaints on file.

Recurring themes

Positive: product formula and packaging quality are frequently praised.

Negative: some reviewers specifically criticize business-model choices (e.g., limited return flexibility) separately from product quality, along with delivery-related complaints and customer service responsiveness.

Before you order

  • Read the current return/exchange policy before ordering, since some complaints are specifically about policy terms rather than product defects.

Bottom line

Glossier is a legitimate brand with well-regarded products. Understanding the return policy upfront addresses the most common source of complaints.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
