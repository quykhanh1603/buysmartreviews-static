Customer Feedback is Mixed

With about 365 reviews on Trustpilot, Glossier showcases a variety of customer opinions, illustrating both satisfaction and frustration.

What Customers Are Talking About

  • Trustpilot: Approximately 365 reviews show a diverse range of opinions.
  • BBB: Not accredited, with several complaints noted.

Positive Feedback: Users often praise the quality of Glossier's product formulations and their appealing packaging.

Areas of Concern: Criticism tends to focus on the brand's return policy, which some find rigid, along with occasional delivery issues and customer service responsiveness.

  • Be sure to familiarize yourself with Glossier's return and exchange policies, as these have been a point of contention for some customers.

Final Take

Glossier is a legitimate brand but be aware of their return policy to avoid potential issues.
