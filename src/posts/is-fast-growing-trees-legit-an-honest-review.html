Is Fast Growing Trees Worth Your Investment?

Fast Growing Trees has shipped over 2 million plants since its start in 2005, making it one of the largest online nurseries in the U.S. They specialize in delivering trees, shrubs, and various plants directly to your doorstep.

Customer Feedback Overview

The feedback from customers paints a generally positive picture of the nursery's services. A significant number of reviews on Trustpilot highlight the nursery's broad customer base and its reputation in the market.

  • Positive Insights: Customers often praise the health of the plants upon arrival, noting effective packaging and timely delivery. The customer support team also receives accolades for their responsiveness to inquiries.
  • Areas for Improvement: However, some customers have reported slow response times when addressing issues. There are also complaints regarding plants arriving in poor condition, like broken branches, and concerns related to the warranty process.

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Photograph your plants immediately upon arrival; this could be essential for any warranty claims.
  • Review the warranty terms for replacements and proof requirements before placing your order.
  • Schedule your orders to coincide with local planting seasons and avoid shipping during extreme weather conditions to reduce stress on the plants.

Fast Growing Trees offers a credible service, but be cautious of potential risks when shipping live plants.
