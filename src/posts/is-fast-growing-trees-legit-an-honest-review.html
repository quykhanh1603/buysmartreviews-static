Is Fast Growing Trees a Reliable Option?

Fast Growing Trees ranks among the largest online plant nurseries in the United States, specializing in shipping trees, shrubs, and various plants directly to customers' homes. With over 2 million customers since its inception in 2005, it’s clear that this nursery has made a significant impact in the market.

Customer Feedback Overview

According to customer reviews across various platforms, Fast Growing Trees showcases a generally positive reputation. Notably, a large number of reviews on Trustpilot indicate a sizable customer base, underscoring the nursery's reach in the industry.

What People Are Saying

  • Positive Insights: Many customers commend the health of the plants upon arrival, highlighting effective packaging and prompt delivery. Additionally, the customer support team receives praise for being helpful with follow-up inquiries.
  • Areas for Improvement: On the flip side, some customers have experienced issues such as slow response times when resolving problems. There are also reports of plants arriving in less than ideal condition, including broken branches and concerns regarding the warranty process.

Considerations Before Purchasing

  • Take immediate photographs of your plants as soon as they arrive, which can be crucial if you need to make a warranty claim later.
  • Make sure to review the warranty terms regarding replacements and required proof before finalizing your order.
  • Plan your orders to align with your local planting season and avoid shipping during extreme weather, which can put additional stress on your plants.

Final Thoughts

Overall, Fast Growing Trees seems to be a credible, established nursery that can be a good choice for customers aware of the inherent risks of shipping live plants. Keeping a record of your plants’ condition upon arrival can smooth the warranty claim process if issues arise.
