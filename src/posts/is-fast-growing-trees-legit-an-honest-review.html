Fast Growing Trees is one of the largest online plant nurseries in the US, shipping trees, shrubs, and plants directly to buyers’ homes. Here’s what the public review data shows.

Quick verdict

Fast Growing Trees appears to be a legitimate, high-volume nursery with a large, mostly satisfied customer base. Live plants are inherently more failure-prone in shipping than most products, so a small share of complaints is expected — what matters more is how the company handles issues when they happen.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: thousands of reviews, reflecting one of the largest customer bases of any company in this category. The company states it has served over 2 million customers since starting in 2005.

Recurring themes

Positive: most reviewers describe healthy plants that were well-packaged and arrived quickly, along with helpful customer support for follow-up questions.

Negative: a subset of customers report slow response times when something goes wrong, along with complaints about how the company’s plant warranty is applied in practice. Some reviewers also reported plants arriving with broken branches or in poor condition.

Before you order

  • Photograph your plants immediately on arrival, before unpacking further — useful if you need to file a warranty claim.
  • Read the plant warranty terms (replacement window, proof required) before you buy, not after something goes wrong.
  • Time orders around your local planting season rather than shipping in extreme heat or cold, which increases stress on live plants regardless of the retailer.

Bottom line

Fast Growing Trees looks like a legitimate, established nursery well-suited to buyers who understand that shipping live plants always carries some inherent risk. Documenting condition on arrival makes any warranty claim much easier if needed.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
