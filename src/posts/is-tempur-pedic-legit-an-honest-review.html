Tempur-Pedic is one of the best-known memory foam mattress brands, sold through its own stores, website, and major retailers. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Tempur-Pedic is a legitimate, long-established brand, but there’s a significant gap between its BBB letter grade and its actual customer review scores — worth understanding since the two measure different things.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: A+ accredited (note: BBB’s letter grade doesn’t factor in customer reviews), but a customer review score around 1.08 out of 5.
  • Trustpilot: mixed, with recurring complaints in specific categories.

Recurring themes

Negative: warranty claim denials, delivery process complaints, and sagging concerns are the most cited issues.

Positive: despite the negative review skew, a number of long-term customers report mattresses lasting well over a decade with continued satisfaction — suggesting the negative reviews cluster heavily around specific problem cases (warranty disputes especially) rather than the product broadly failing.

Before you order

  • Read the exact sagging/warranty measurement criteria before buying.
  • Keep your purchase receipt and any product registration on file from day one, since warranty disputes are the most common complaint category.

Bottom line

Tempur-Pedic is a legitimate, long-standing brand with real long-term fans, but warranty claim friction is common enough in reviews that understanding the exact terms before buying is worthwhile.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
