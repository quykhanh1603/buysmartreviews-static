Tuft & Needle is a direct-to-consumer mattress brand, now owned by Serta Simmons Bedding. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Tuft & Needle is a legitimate, established brand, but there’s a large gap between its formal BBB rating and its Trustpilot customer sentiment worth understanding.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: A+ rating with a 5/5 customer review score on that platform.
  • Trustpilot: rated “Poor,” around 2.4 out of 5.

Recurring themes

Independent lab-style review sites tend to rate the mattress itself favorably for motion isolation and responsiveness, which suggests the product is solid but reviewer experience diverges depending on whether an issue (delivery, warranty, customer service) came up along the way.

Before you order

  • Cross-check both BBB and Trustpilot reviews rather than relying on either alone, given how much they diverge here.
  • Keep your order and any warranty documentation organized from day one.

Bottom line

Tuft & Needle is a legitimate mattress brand with a well-regarded core product, but the gap between its BBB and Trustpilot scores suggests inconsistent post-purchase experience for some buyers.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
