Ownership Surprises

Tuft & Needle is owned by Serta Simmons Bedding, a major player in the mattress industry. This surprising ownership ties a relatively young brand to a well-established legacy.

Customer Feedback Analysis

  • BBB: Holds an impressive A+ rating with a perfect 5/5 customer review score.
  • Trustpilot: Conversely, it garners a rating of “Poor” with an average of around 2.4 out of 5.

The mattress itself often receives high marks for features like motion isolation and overall responsiveness. However, customer experiences can vary significantly, especially when it comes to aspects like delivery, warranty service, and customer support.

  • Examine the reviews on both BBB and Trustpilot to get a balanced view, as their ratings show significant divergence.
  • Maintain all order and warranty documents from the outset to help streamline any potential issues.

While Tuft & Needle is undoubtedly a legitimate mattress brand with a solid product offering, the noticeable difference between its BBB and Trustpilot scores indicates that some customers may experience challenges after their purchase.

Tuft & Needle's mixed reviews suggest you should proceed with caution.
