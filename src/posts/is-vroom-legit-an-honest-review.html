What’s the Deal with Vroom's Reputation?

When it comes to buying a used car online, Vroom definitely made waves by offering a way to purchase vehicles without ever seeing them in person. However, when you dig into customer reviews, there’s a lot to be wary of.

Are Customers Happy with Their Vroom Experience?

  • Trustpilot: The reviews on this platform lean heavily negative, showcasing widespread dissatisfaction among buyers.
  • BBB: The Better Business Bureau entries can be confusing, with some suggesting Vroom doesn't operate as effectively as it once did.

What Common Issues Should You Be Aware Of?

Negative Experiences: A number of complaints point out problems like cars arriving with unreported damages, disputes over warranty claims, and frustrating delays—some customers reported waiting over four months for title and registration paperwork.

If you've had an issue with a previous purchase, be sure to document everything and consider reaching out to your state attorney general if you can't get Vroom to respond. And for any future car purchases, especially those you can't see in person, getting an independent inspection could save you from a headache.

The experiences shared by former customers indicate that Vroom has more significant complaints than many rivals. Given their past issues, it might be wise to proceed with caution if you’re considering them.

The ratings mentioned are based on publicly available information at the time of writing and may have changed since.
