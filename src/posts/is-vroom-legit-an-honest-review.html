Understanding Vroom's Reputation

Vroom entered the used-car market by allowing customers to purchase vehicles without seeing them in person, relying on online transactions and home delivery. However, an exploration of customer feedback reveals a concerning trend.

Customer Feedback Insights

  • Trustpilot: The feedback here is predominantly negative, highlighting customer dissatisfaction.
  • BBB: The Better Business Bureau rating and status can vary, with some listings indicating the company is no longer operating as initially intended.

Common Customer Issues

Negative Experiences: Notable complaints frequently mention vehicles arriving with hidden damages, disagreements regarding warranty services, and prolonged delays — with some waiting times reported to exceed four months — for title and registration paperwork.

Important Considerations

  • If you are facing unresolved issues from a previous Vroom purchase, keep thorough documentation and consider reaching out to your state attorney general's office for consumer guidance if you can't get a response from Vroom.
  • For future car purchases, especially sight-unseen ones, it's advisable to arrange for an independent inspection to avoid potential pitfalls.

Final Thoughts

The experience shared by former customers suggests that Vroom has significantly more serious complaints than many of its competitors, and since its car-selling operations have ceased, caution is warranted for anyone considering their history.

The ratings mentioned are based on publicly available information at the time of writing and may have changed since.
