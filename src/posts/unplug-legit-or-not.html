What is Unplug?

Unplug is a meditation service that offers a variety of classes and resources for individuals looking to incorporate mindfulness and relaxation into their daily lives. Whether you're interested in guided meditation, sound baths, or breathwork, Unplug provides several formats to choose from, including in-person sessions in Los Angeles and a mobile app for meditation on-the-go.

What They Offer

At Unplug, you can start your meditation journey with their app or by attending live classes at their studio. Their offerings include:

  • Live Studio Classes: Attend in-person sessions at their location for a more immersive experience.
  • Unplug App: Download their meditation app available on iOS and Android for guided sessions anytime, anywhere.
  • Corporate Wellness Programs: Tailored solutions for businesses looking to enhance employee well-being.
  • Meditation Teacher Training: Comprehensive training for those interested in becoming meditation instructors.

Pricing and Membership

The starting price for classes at Unplug is $99, which gives access to their various offerings. There are different membership options available, making it flexible for individuals looking to meditate regularly. Unplug aims to provide solutions that fit different lifestyles and budgets, appealing to both beginners and seasoned practitioners alike.

Parking and Location

If you're planning to visit the Unplug studio in Los Angeles, it’s conveniently located at 12401 Wilshire Blvd, Suite 101. There is a parking lot behind the building that charges $3 per hour, with validation available. Street parking is also an option, but users should be cautious of metered spots and street cleaning schedules.

Class Policies

When attending a class, it's important to note the studio's policies. Participants must arrive on time, as there is a strict no-entry policy once a class has started. This is designed to maintain a peaceful environment for all meditators. Additionally, electronics are not allowed in the meditation room, so it’s advisable to check in phones and other devices beforehand.

Getting Started

Unplug makes it simple to begin your meditation journey. New users can sign up for a free course that promises to teach the basics of meditation within just seven days. The process begins by booking a class or downloading the app, followed by daily practice to start reaping the benefits of meditation.

Final Thoughts

Unplug appears to be a well-rounded option for individuals looking to delve into meditation, offering both guided classes and an app for convenience. The experience is tailored to create a calming environment, guided by skilled teachers. However, potential visitors should be mindful of the strict policies regarding class attendance to ensure a smooth experience.
