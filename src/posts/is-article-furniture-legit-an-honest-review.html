Overview of Article Furniture

Article is a direct-to-consumer, online-only modern furniture brand that has been in the market since 2013. Based on public review data, here's what you should know.

Customer Satisfaction Ratings

  • Trustpilot: approximately 4.7 out of 5 based on over 400 reviews.
  • BBB: holds an A grade.

One noteworthy aspect of Article is its proactive approach in addressing negative feedback, with most complaints receiving responses within a week, often including resolutions, which is commendable for a brand in the furniture space.

Common Customer Feedback

Positive Aspects: Shoppers frequently highlight the appealing modern designs offered at lower prices compared to traditional retailers, along with an attentive customer service team.

Areas for Improvement: Some recent reviews indicate occasional shipping delays and issues with the quality or materials of certain items, though this seems to affect a minority of customers.

Things to Consider

  • Before making a purchase, check reviews specific to the item or collection you're interested in, as quality can vary significantly.
  • If you have specific delivery requirements, such as a moving date, it's wise to confirm current lead times.

Final Thoughts

Overall, Article stands out with a strong review history among furniture retailers and a reputation for responsive customer service. It remains a solid option for those seeking modern furniture.

The ratings above are based on publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
