What is Article Furniture All About?

Article is a modern furniture brand that sells directly to consumers through their online platform. Since launching in 2013, they’ve made quite a name for themselves with stylish, contemporary pieces.

How Do Customers Feel About Their Purchases?

  • Trustpilot: around 4.7 out of 5 based on over 400 reviews.
  • BBB: maintains an A grade.

One thing you’ll notice is that Article actively engages with customer feedback. They tend to respond to negative reviews within a week and often work toward solutions, which is a rarity in the furniture industry.

What Should You Watch Out For?

Pros: Many shoppers rave about the chic modern designs and the competitive pricing compared to traditional stores. Plus, their customer service is noted for being attentive.

Cons: On the flip side, some users have pointed out occasional shipping delays and concerns regarding the quality of certain items, though this seems to be an issue for only a small number of customers.

It’s definitely worth your time to check reviews specific to any item you’re thinking about to ensure it meets your expectations. And if you have a tight timeline for delivery, make sure to verify lead times.

Keep in mind that the ratings mentioned are based on publicly available data as of now and could change over time.
