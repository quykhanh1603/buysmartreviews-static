Article is a direct-to-consumer, online-only modern furniture brand that has operated since 2013. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Article stands out with a notably strong review record compared to most furniture retailers we’ve covered — a legitimate, well-regarded brand, though recent reviews suggest slightly more inconsistency than in the past.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4.7 out of 5 from over 400 reviews.
  • BBB: an A grade.

Article also reportedly responds to the large majority of negative reviews within a week, often with a resolution offered — a meaningfully better response pattern than most companies in this category.

Recurring themes

Positive: modern design at a lower price point than comparable traditional retailers, plus a responsive customer service team, are the most consistent praise points.

Negative: a smaller but real set of recent reviews mention shipping delays and material/quality issues on certain pieces.

Before you order

  • Read reviews for the specific item/collection you’re considering, since quality feedback varies by product line.
  • Confirm current lead times before ordering if you have a deadline (e.g., moving date).

Bottom line

Article has one of the stronger review records of any furniture retailer we’ve checked, backed by a responsive customer service track record. A reasonable pick for modern furniture shoppers.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
