Trustworthiness of Raymour & Flanigan

Raymour & Flanigan generally proves to be a trustworthy option for furniture shopping, thanks to its solid reputation and positive customer feedback. Established in 1947 and operating over 140 showrooms across the Northeast US, the brand is well-regarded in the furniture and mattress market.

Customer Feedback Overview

On platforms like Trustpilot, Raymour & Flanigan enjoys an impressive score of around 4 out of 5 based on nearly 62,000 reviews, which indicates a solid customer base and feedback. Additionally, the company has earned an A+ rating from the Better Business Bureau (BBB), reinforcing its credibility. Many customers commend the knowledgeable sales staff and appreciate the straightforward, hassle-free in-store buying process, a likely advantage of the company's long-standing presence in the region. However, it’s worth noting that, like many large retailers, complaints can arise, particularly regarding delivery times, product quality for specific items, and the handling of disputes or refunds at individual locations. Therefore, it is wise to check reviews specific to your local showroom as experiences can vary significantly between branches. Make sure to get any delivery timelines and warranty terms in writing at the time of your purchase.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
