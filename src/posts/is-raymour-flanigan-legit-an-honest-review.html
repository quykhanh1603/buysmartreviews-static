Overview of Raymour & Flanigan

Established in 1947, Raymour & Flanigan operates over 140 showrooms across the Northeast US, making it a well-known player in the furniture and mattress market. Let's delve into what customers are saying about their experience.

Customer Feedback Highlights

  • Trustpilot: boasts an impressive score of around 4 out of 5 from nearly 62,000 reviews, indicating a solid customer base and feedback.
  • Better Business Bureau (BBB): has earned an A+ rating, reinforcing its credibility.

Common Customer Insights

Positive Aspects: Many customers commend the knowledgeable sales staff and appreciate the straightforward, hassle-free in-store buying process, which is likely bolstered by the company's long-standing presence in the region.

Areas for Improvement: Like many large retailers, complaints can surface, particularly regarding delivery times, product quality for specific items, and the handling of disputes or refunds at individual locations.

Things to Consider Before You Buy

  • It's wise to check reviews specific to your local showroom, as experiences can vary significantly between branches.
  • Make sure to get any delivery timelines and warranty terms in writing at the time of your purchase.

Final Thoughts

With a reputable standing and a long history in the industry, Raymour & Flanigan generally shines among large furniture chains. Just be sure to consider the reputation of your local showroom before making a decision.
