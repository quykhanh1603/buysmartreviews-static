Understanding Skechers' Reputation

With an average rating of 1.6 out of 5 on SmartCustomer, Skechers has garnered mixed reviews from buyers. This footwear brand is known for comfort but is facing challenges with quality perceptions among its consumers.

  • Trustpilot: More than 2,200 reviews indicate a mix of satisfaction and disappointment.
  • SmartCustomer: Current average rating sits at approximately 1.6 out of 5 based on 163 reviews.

The Customer Feedback Landscape

Positive Notes: Long-time users, some of whom have been loyal for over 20 years, express ongoing satisfaction with the comfort and durability of their shoes.

Negative Insights: On the flip side, there are increasing reports of declining quality, with issues like soles wearing out or becoming slippery after just a few months. Some shoppers have also noted less-than-helpful staff in certain stores.

Shopping Tips

  • Before making a purchase, it's wise to look at recent reviews for specific shoe models, as complaints tend to focus on particular lines.
  • Retain your receipt; this can come in handy if your shoes wear out faster than expected and you want to take action.

Final Thoughts

While Skechers remains a legitimate brand with a committed fanbase, recent customer concerns about quality warrant a careful approach before making a purchase.
