Skechers is a major athletic and casual footwear brand known for comfort-focused shoes. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Skechers is a legitimate, large brand with a loyal long-term customer base, but a number of recent reviews describe quality declining relative to price.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: over 2,200 reviews, mixed.
  • SmartCustomer: around 1.6 out of 5 from 163 reviews.

Recurring themes

Positive: some long-time customers (20+ years in some cases) report continued satisfaction with comfort and reliability.

Negative: more recent reviews describe quality decline — soles wearing out or becoming slick within a few months — along with unhelpful in-store staff at some locations.

Before you order

  • Check recent reviews for the specific shoe model/line rather than the brand overall, since quality complaints seem concentrated in certain product lines.
  • Keep your receipt in case a pair wears out unusually fast and you want to raise it with the retailer.

Bottom line

Skechers is a legitimate, established brand with genuine long-time fans, but recent quality complaints are common enough that checking model-specific reviews is worthwhile before buying.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
