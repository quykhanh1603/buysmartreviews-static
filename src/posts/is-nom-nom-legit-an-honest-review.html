Is Nom Nom Worth It for Your Pet?

If you're looking into feeding your furry friend fresh, human-grade meals, you might have come across Nom Nom. This subscription service has been making waves among pet owners who want the best for their cats and dogs. But what are customers really saying about it?

What Do Customers Think About Nom Nom?

  • Trustpilot: Nom Nom scores an impressive average of about 4.2 out of 5 based on nearly 1,600 reviews.
  • BBB: With an A+ rating, they have a low number of complaints relative to their size, though be aware that there may be some inconsistencies on different BBB listings.

What Should You Watch Out For?

Positive Notes: Many pet parents love how excited their pets get about the meals, many also report noticeable health benefits and praise the customer service.

Concerns: On the downside, some have experienced issues like late deliveries, lost packages, and sporadic reports of food being spoiled upon arrival.

Just a heads up: make sure to check the temperature and packaging of the food when it arrives, especially in hot weather, and reach out if you see anything wrong. And if you decide to cancel your subscription, do it in writing to ensure it's processed correctly.

Overall, Nom Nom has built a strong reputation in the fresh pet food market and is especially noted for its service. Still, it's smart to keep an eye on your deliveries since some customers have flagged spoilage issues.

Ratings and feedback mentioned here reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may vary over time.
