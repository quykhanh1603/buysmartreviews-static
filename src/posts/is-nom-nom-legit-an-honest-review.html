Nom Nom is a fresh, human-grade dog and cat food subscription delivery service. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Nom Nom is a legitimate, well-reviewed company with strong customer service feedback, and a lighter complaint pattern than some competitors in the fresh-pet-food category.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 4.2 out of 5 from nearly 1,600 reviews.
  • BBB: A+ rating, with a low complaint count relative to its size (note: search results show some inconsistency between two separate BBB listings for the company, so complaint counts vary by source).

Recurring themes

Positive: pets eagerly eating the food, visible improvements in coat and health, and responsive, friendly customer service are the most consistent praise points.

Negative: delivery delays or lost orders, occasional reports of spoiled food on arrival, and some cancellation/refund friction.

Before you order

  • Check the food’s temperature and packaging integrity immediately on arrival, especially in hot weather, and report spoilage right away.
  • Confirm cancellation in writing if you decide to stop the subscription.

Bottom line

Nom Nom has a solid overall review record with a notably strong customer service reputation. Check deliveries promptly for freshness given occasional spoilage reports.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
