How Nom Nom Works: Customized Meal Plans

Getting started with Nom Nom is simple:

  1. Tell Us About Your Dog: Provide details about your dog's size, age, and dietary preferences to help Nom Nom create a customized meal plan.

  3. Delivery: Meals are delivered directly to your home according to a schedule you set, allowing for convenient mealtime solutions.
