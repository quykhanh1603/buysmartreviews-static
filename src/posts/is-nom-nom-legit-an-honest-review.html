An Overview of Nom Nom

Nom Nom is a subscription service that delivers fresh, human-grade food for dogs and cats. As pet owners increasingly seek quality nutrition for their furry friends, Nom Nom has generated notable attention. Let’s explore what customers think based on various public reviews.

Customer Feedback Highlights

  • Trustpilot: Nom Nom has earned a commendable score of around 4.2 out of 5 from nearly 1,600 reviewers.
  • BBB: With an A+ rating, the company exhibits a low complaint count relative to its size, although discrepancies exist between different BBB listings.

Common Themes in Reviews

Positive Feedback: Many customers rave about their pets enthusiastically eating the food, as well as visible health improvements and excellent customer service.

Areas for Improvement: Some customers have reported issues like delivery delays, lost orders, and occasional concerns regarding spoiled food upon arrival.

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Upon delivery, make sure to check the food's temperature and packaging, especially during hot weather, and report any spoilage immediately.
  • If you choose to cancel your subscription, be sure to do so in writing to confirm the cancellation.

In Summary

Overall, Nom Nom boasts a strong reputation in the fresh pet food market, particularly for its customer service. However, it’s wise to inspect your deliveries promptly due to some reports of spoilage.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
