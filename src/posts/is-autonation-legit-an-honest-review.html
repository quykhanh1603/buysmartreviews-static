Is AutoNation Trustworthy?

AutoNation's reputation is a mixed bag, and potential customers should exercise caution based on largely unfavorable ratings. The experiences shared by customers indicate significant concerns that could impact your decision.

Understanding Customer Feedback

On Trustpilot, AutoNation has an approximate rating of 1.6 out of 5 stars, while the Better Business Bureau lists it with an average score of roughly 1.3 out of 5, without any accreditation. The negative feedback frequently centers around unexpected fees that arise during the sales process, along with the difficulties customers encounter when attempting to resolve issues after multiple contacts with the dealership. However, some customers report satisfactory experiences, particularly during routine maintenance visits, suggesting that experiences can vary widely depending on the situation.

It's advisable to acquire a total out-the-door price in writing before visiting a dealership to ensure it includes all fees and aligns with advertised prices. Additionally, researching reviews specific to your local AutoNation location can help, as the quality of service may differ significantly from one dealership to another.

Ratings mentioned are based on publicly available information at the time of this writing and may have changed since then.
