AutoNation is one of the largest new and used car dealer groups in the US, operating under multiple brand names across many states. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

AutoNation is a large, legitimate, publicly traded dealer group, but its review record across independent platforms is notably weaker than its size and market position might suggest.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 1.6 out of 5.
  • BBB: around 1.3 out of 5 customer review average, without BBB accreditation.

Recurring themes

Negative: the most common complaints involve unexpected fees or charges added during the sales process, and difficulty getting issues resolved after multiple attempts to contact the dealership.

Positive: as with most large dealer groups, individual location experience varies — some reviewers report satisfactory service, particularly for routine service department visits rather than vehicle purchases.

Before you buy

  • Get the full out-the-door price (including all fees) in writing before you visit, and compare it against the advertised price.
  • Check reviews for your specific local AutoNation-affiliated dealership, not just the parent brand, since experience can vary significantly by location.

Bottom line

AutoNation is a legitimate, major dealer group, but independent review platforms consistently show more customer dissatisfaction than its size would suggest. Getting pricing in writing upfront is a reasonable precaution.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
