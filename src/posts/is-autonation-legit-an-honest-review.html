Understanding AutoNation's Reputation

As one of the largest new and used car dealership groups in the United States, AutoNation operates under various brand names across numerous states. However, the customer review data presents a more nuanced picture than you might expect from such a sizable entity.

Customer Ratings Overview

  • Trustpilot: approximately 1.6 out of 5 stars.
  • BBB: an average rating of about 1.3 out of 5, with no BBB accreditation.

Common Feedback Patterns

Negative Feedback: A significant number of complaints focus on unexpected fees that appear during the sales process and the challenges customers face when trying to resolve issues after contacting the dealership multiple times.

Positive Feedback: It's worth noting that experiences can vary greatly. Some customers report satisfactory interactions, especially relating to routine maintenance visits rather than purchases.

What to Consider Before Your Purchase

  • Obtain the total out-the-door price in writing before visiting, ensuring it includes all fees and compares with the advertised price.
  • Research reviews specific to your local AutoNation dealership, as experiences can differ widely from one location to another.

In Conclusion

While AutoNation stands as a legitimate and influential dealer group, the mixed reviews from independent platforms highlight a level of customer dissatisfaction that may not align with its market stature. It's wise to get clear pricing in advance to avoid surprises.

Ratings mentioned are based on publicly available information at the time of this writing and may have changed since then.
