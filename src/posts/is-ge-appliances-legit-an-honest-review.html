GE Appliances is a major American appliance manufacturer, selling through retailers and directly online. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

GE Appliances is a legitimate manufacturer — the website itself checks out as safe and non-fraudulent — but review sentiment is weak, concentrated in product reliability rather than any scam concern.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 100 reviews, skewing negative.
  • BBB: committed to BBB Standards for Trust, with complaints on file about durability.

Recurring themes

Negative: premature breakdowns and defects present from initial use are the most consistently cited issues.

Before you order

  • Check recent reliability data for the specific model/line you’re considering rather than the brand overall, since appliance reliability tends to vary a lot by product line and manufacturing period.
  • Register your appliance and keep documentation from day one in case of a warranty claim.

Bottom line

GE Appliances is a legitimate manufacturer with real product-reliability complaints in recent reviews. Model-specific research is worth the extra step before a large appliance purchase.
