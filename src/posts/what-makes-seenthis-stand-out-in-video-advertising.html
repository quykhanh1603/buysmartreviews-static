Revolutionizing Video Advertising

SeenThis stands out as a trailblazer in the video advertising landscape, particularly on the open web. Founded in 2009 by three innovative friends, Kristofer, Marcus, and Jesper, the company has always aimed to explore the potential of technology in advertising. Their proprietary adaptive streaming technology is designed to remove the common trade-offs in video advertising, allowing brands to deliver premium quality video that loads instantly.

Key Features and Benefits

SeenThis offers a suite of features that make it a compelling choice for advertisers:

  • Instant Loading: Videos load without delay, ensuring that users engage from the very first look.
  • Increased Engagement: Their platform has shown a remarkable 152% increase in click-through rates (CTR), proving its effectiveness in capturing user attention.
  • Affordable Pricing: SeenThis claims that its video solutions can be up to 70% cheaper than traditional video advertising, providing premium performance without the premium price tag.
  • Seamless Integration: The technology works effortlessly with all major ad platforms and servers, allowing clients to implement campaigns smoothly.
  • Real-Time Insights: Built-in measurement tools provide advertisers with real-time KPIs and insights, enhancing campaign performance tracking.

Proven Results

SeenThis collaborates with over 5,000 brands, agencies, and publishers globally, demonstrating its massive reach and impact in the industry. Their innovative approach is not only focused on delivering high-quality video but also emphasizes measurable outcomes. Client testimonials highlight a consistent delivery of superior engagement and attention levels, showcasing SeenThis as a reliable partner in enhancing brand visibility.

Why Choose SeenThis?

One of the standout advantages of working with SeenThis is their commitment to delivering results without compromise. Unlike traditional video advertising, where advertisers often have to sacrifice speed for quality or vice versa, SeenThis eliminates those barriers. They allow brands to produce engaging and interactive video content that performs across various stages of the customer journey—from raising awareness to driving action.

Moreover, SeenThis is passionate about empowering creativity and protecting the independence of the open web. They believe that this space holds immense potential for advertisers and publishers alike, promoting diverse voices and creative expressions that can thrive without being tethered to large platforms.

Conclusion

For brands and agencies looking to enhance their video advertising strategies, SeenThis presents a robust solution that prioritizes performance, engagement, and cost efficiency. With instant video loading and proven results, they have positioned themselves as a forward-thinking partner in the advertising industry.
