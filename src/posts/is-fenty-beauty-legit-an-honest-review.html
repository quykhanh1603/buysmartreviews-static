Fenty Beauty is Rihanna’s inclusive beauty brand, known for a wide foundation shade range and high-performance formulas. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Fenty Beauty’s products are consistently well-regarded, but order fulfillment speed is a real and specific complaint pattern worth knowing before you order for a deadline.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: mixed, with reviews ranging from very positive to negative.
  • BBB: not accredited, with complaints on file about shipping delays and refunds.

Recurring themes

Positive: product performance (lip products, highlighters, complexion products) is consistently praised for pigment and wear, though some reviewers note it’s less ideal for dry/sensitive skin.

Negative: shipping delays are the most specific and recurring complaint — at least one documented case of an order still not shipped two weeks after purchase, with repeated “ships tomorrow” messaging.

Before you order

  • If you’re ordering for a specific date, order with significant buffer time given the documented shipping delay pattern.
  • If shipping is unusually delayed, request a specific ship date in writing rather than accepting repeated vague promises.

Bottom line

Fenty Beauty’s products are well-regarded; shipping speed is the main risk factor, so build in extra time for any order.
