Should You Trust Fenty Beauty?

Thinking about ordering from Fenty Beauty? You're not alone; many people are curious about their products and service. Fenty Beauty is Rihanna’s inclusive beauty line known for its wide range of foundation shades and high-performance formulas. Let’s see what customers are saying about their experiences with the brand.

What Do Customers Think?

  • Trustpilot: Reviews are quite mixed, showing both positive and negative experiences.
  • BBB: The brand isn’t accredited and has received several complaints about shipping and refund issues.

Are There Any Concerns to Note?

Positive Feedback: Many users rave about the quality of products like lipsticks, highlighters, and complexion items, noting their vibrant pigmentation and durability. Yet, there are mentions that these products may not be the best fit for those with dry or sensitive skin.

Negative Feedback: A common complaint is around shipping delays, with reports of orders that take over two weeks to ship, often accompanied by misleading 'ships tomorrow' promises.

  • To avoid any disappointment, especially if you need your order by a specific date, be sure to allow enough time for delivery considering the delay reports.
  • If you experience significant shipping delays, it’s best to ask for a specific shipping date in writing rather than relying on vague responses.

Fenty Beauty has a strong reputation for quality, but shipping issues can be a real headache. Just keep this in mind and plan ahead if you decide to make a purchase.

These ratings and insights are based on publicly available data at the time of writing and may change over time.
