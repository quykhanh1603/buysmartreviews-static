State Bicycle Co. is an Arizona-based direct-to-consumer bike brand known for affordable fixed-gear, single-speed, and gravel bikes. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

State Bicycle Co. shows very strong ratings on its own site and third-party review widgets, though independent third-party coverage (like Trustpilot) is thinner for the US site specifically, so weigh the very high self-reported numbers with a bit of caution.

What customer ratings show

  • Judge.me (independent review platform): around 4.8 out of 5 from over 15,700 reviews.
  • BBB: not accredited.

Recurring themes

Positive: value for price, style/customization options, and build quality relative to cost are the most consistent praise points.

Negative: a smaller number of reviews (mostly on the UK Trustpilot site) mention delivery and refund-process complaints.

Before you order

  • Since these are direct-to-consumer bikes, budget for either your own assembly or a local shop’s build/tune fee.
  • Read reviews for your specific model, since experience can vary between the brand’s different bike lines (fixed-gear vs. gravel, for example).

Bottom line

State Bicycle Co. has strong ratings overall, concentrated more on the brand’s own review platform than fully independent third-party sites. Good value-focused option, with the usual DTC-bike assembly consideration.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
