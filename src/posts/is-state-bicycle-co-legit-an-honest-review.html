Impressive Customer Ratings

State Bicycle Co. boasts an impressive average rating of approximately 4.8 out of 5 from over 15,700 reviews, showcasing strong customer satisfaction. However, while their own site reflects high ratings, independent evaluations are not as abundant, particularly in the US market.

Customer Insights

  • Judge.me: 4.8 out of 5 from over 15,700 reviews.
  • Better Business Bureau (BBB): The company is not accredited.

Positive Aspects: Customers frequently commend the value for money, appealing design, and solid build quality offered by State Bicycle Co.'s bikes.

Negative Aspects: Some reviews from UK users on Trustpilot point out challenges with delivery and the refund process.

Considerations

  • As a direct-to-consumer brand, buyers will need to either assemble the bike themselves or pay for a local shop's assembly service.
  • It’s wise to read reviews specific to the bike model you’re interested in, as customer experiences can vary significantly across different lines.

State Bicycle Co. provides good value for budget-conscious buyers, but potential customers should be aware of assembly requirements.

Ratings mentioned reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
