About State Bicycle Co.

Based in Arizona, State Bicycle Co. is known for its direct-to-consumer approach, offering a variety of bikes including fixed-gear, single-speed, and gravel models—all at affordable prices. This article takes an insightful look into customer feedback and overall perceptions.

Customer Feedback Overview

Ratings from State Bicycle Co.'s own site and various review platforms are notably high, indicating a generally positive reception. However, it's important to approach these self-reported ratings with a discerning eye, as independent reviews from sites like Trustpilot are not as abundant for the US market.

Ratings Breakdown

  • Judge.me: Approximately 4.8 out of 5 from over 15,700 reviews, showcasing strong customer satisfaction.
  • Better Business Bureau (BBB): The company is not accredited.

What People Are Saying

Positive Aspects: Common praises revolve around the value for money, appealing style, and solid build quality, especially relative to the price point.

Negative Aspects: A smaller subset of reviews, particularly from the UK Trustpilot, highlight issues with delivery and the refund process.

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Being a direct-to-consumer brand means you'll need to either assemble the bike yourself or consider a local shop's build and tune-up fee.
  • It’s a good idea to check reviews specific to the model you are interested in, as experiences can differ significantly across the various bike lines.

Final Thoughts

Overall, State Bicycle Co. enjoys strong customer ratings, particularly on their own review platform, though there’s less independent verification. They provide good value for budget-conscious buyers, but potential customers should be aware of assembly requirements.

Ratings mentioned reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
