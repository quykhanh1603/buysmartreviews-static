Trustworthiness of PetFlow

PetFlow generally comes across as a trustworthy option for pet supplies, backed by a lot of positive customer feedback. With over 65,000 reviews on Trustpilot and an A+ rating from the BBB, the retailer shows a strong commitment to customer satisfaction, despite not being accredited by the BBB.

Customer Insights

Many customers appreciate the variety of products available and the reliable delivery services. However, some have raised concerns about unexpected changes in subscription billing and delays in customer service responses during issues. Hence, it's wise for shoppers to regularly check subscription settings and keep order confirmations handy to avoid potential billing problems. Overall, PetFlow maintains a solid reputation, but a keen eye on subscription details is advisable.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
