PetFlow is an online pet supply retailer specializing in recurring/subscription delivery of pet food and supplies. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

PetFlow has one of the stronger review records among pet supply retailers we’ve checked, with a large, consistent review volume.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: a strong rating from over 65,000 reviews.
  • BBB: A+ rating (not accredited, but committed to BBB Standards for Trust).

Recurring themes

Positive: selection variety, delivery reliability, and speed are consistently praised across a large review volume.

Negative: a smaller share of reviews mention billing changes on recurring/subscription orders and customer service responsiveness when an issue comes up.

Before you order

  • Review your subscription/auto-ship settings periodically to catch any pricing or frequency changes.
  • Keep order confirmations handy in case of a billing discrepancy.

Bottom line

PetFlow has a strong, high-volume review record. Subscription billing is the main area worth double-checking periodically.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
