About Ferlux's Transition

Ferlux, with over 15 years of experience, has made a significant shift in its business model. As of March 31, 2023, the company has ceased its production and marketing of fruit extracts, which were previously rich in polyphenols. This decision marks a pivotal change for the company as they now concentrate exclusively on the production of Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) for the BIOCODEX group, which they belong to.

Customer Engagement and Appreciation

Throughout its operational years, Ferlux has engaged with its customers in research and development, demonstrating a commitment to understanding the specialized market of fruit extracts. The management has expressed sincere gratitude towards their loyal customers who supported them during this journey and to the staff whose professionalism contributed to the company’s operations.

Changes in Product Availability

As of the end of March 2023, Ferlux has regrettably announced that they can no longer fulfill any requests for their fruit extract products. This may pose challenges for customers who previously relied on Ferlux for these specific products. Instead, the company encourages customers to seek alternative suppliers to meet their needs.

What This Means for Customers

For those looking for fruit extracts, Ferlux's new focus means they are no longer a viable source for such products. The company's dedication to developing APIs may appeal to a different clientele within the pharmaceutical sector, but it significantly narrows their market reach. Customers interested in Ferlux's previous offerings will need to explore other options in the marketplace.
