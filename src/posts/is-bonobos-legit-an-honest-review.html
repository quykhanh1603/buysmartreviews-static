Bonobos is a menswear brand known for fit-focused tailored and casual clothing, now owned by Walmart. Here’s an honest look based on public review data — including a rating pattern worth understanding before you shop.

Quick verdict

Bonobos is a legitimate, established menswear brand with generally positive reviews, but the rating distribution is more polarized than average — worth knowing before you order.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: rated around 4.3 out of 5 across 477 reviews.
  • Yelp: around 3.9 out of 5 across 641 reviews.

One detail worth flagging: Bonobos’ Trustpilot rating breaks down to roughly 60% five-star reviews but also about 24% one-star reviews, with relatively few reviews in between. That kind of polarized, bimodal pattern usually means the product experience itself is strong, but something specific — often sizing or a particular order issue — is driving a vocal minority of very unhappy customers.

Recurring themes

Positive: the most consistent praise is around fit — many reviewers specifically say Bonobos clothes fit better than other mainstream brands, along with good in-store/guide-shop fitting service where available.

Negative: the main complaints are inconsistent quality between item types and, again, mis-sizing — likely a meaningful driver of that one-star segment.

Before you order

  • Use their sizing tools/guide-shop fitting appointment if available in your area before ordering online, since fit issues are the most cited complaint.
  • Order multiple sizes for a first purchase if the return policy allows, since fit seems to run inconsistent between categories (shirts vs. pants vs. outerwear).

Bottom line

Bonobos is a legitimate brand with strong fit-focused design, but the polarized rating pattern suggests sizing accuracy is hit-or-miss for a meaningful minority of buyers. Worth it if you can verify sizing before committing to a full order.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
