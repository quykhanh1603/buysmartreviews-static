J.Jill is a multi-channel women’s fashion retailer selling apparel, accessories, and footwear. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

J.Jill is a legitimate, established retailer with a loyal customer base for its sizing consistency, but refund processing time and billing practices are recurring complaint areas.

What customer ratings show

  • Trustpilot: around 186 reviews, mixed.
  • BBB: registered, with complaints concentrated on billing/credit practices.

Recurring themes

Positive: several reviewers specifically praise J.Jill’s sizing consistency and garment construction quality as above average for the category.

Negative: refund processing reported to take close to a month in some cases, often requiring multiple follow-up calls and emails, plus some BBB complaints about billing/late-fee practices.

Before you order

  • Keep a record of your return tracking number and follow up proactively if a refund hasn’t posted within a couple weeks.
  • Review any credit account terms carefully if you’re considering J.Jill’s store credit/billing options.

Bottom line

J.Jill has a loyal following for consistent sizing and construction quality, but refund timing and billing practices are worth watching closely based on available reviews.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
