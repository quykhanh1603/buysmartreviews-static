Express is a mall-based clothing retailer selling both men’s and women’s fashion, founded in 1980. Here’s an honest look based on public review data.

Quick verdict

Express is a legitimate, long-established retailer, but its return policy and online customer service are the most consistent friction points in reviews.

What customer ratings show

  • BBB: not accredited, with a high volume of complaints on file.

Recurring themes

Positive: many reviewers note that the clothing fits well and quality is decent for the price.

Negative: online customer service is frequently described as unhelpful or script-bound, and the return policy (generally a 30-day window) frustrates some customers who miss it, along with general shipping/order complaints.

Before you order

  • Note the exact return deadline at time of purchase and set a reminder if you might need to return an item.
  • For online order issues, consider visiting a physical store if one’s nearby, since in-person resolution is sometimes faster than online support.

Bottom line

Express is a legitimate, established retailer. Keep close track of your return window, since that’s the most consistently cited source of customer frustration.

Ratings referenced above reflect publicly available data at the time of writing and may have changed since publication.
