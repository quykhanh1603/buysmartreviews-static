Overview of Express

Founded in 1980, Express is a prominent clothing retailer with a strong presence in malls, offering a diverse range of men’s and women’s fashion.

  • BBB Rating: Not accredited and has received a significant number of complaints.

Customer Experiences

While many shoppers appreciate the fit and quality of Express's clothing relative to its price, there's a notable downside in customer service. Reviewers frequently express frustration with online customer support, describing it as unhelpful. Additionally, the 30-day return policy can complicate matters for those who miss the deadline, and issues involving shipping and order fulfillment are common complaints.

Important Considerations

If you're considering a purchase, be mindful of the return window to avoid any hassles. Setting reminders to track your return days is a smart move. Moreover, if you encounter issues with online orders, visiting a physical store may offer quicker solutions than waiting for online assistance.
