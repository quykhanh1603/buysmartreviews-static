Overview of Express

Founded in 1980, Express is a prominent clothing retailer with a strong presence in malls, offering a diverse range of men’s and women’s fashion.

What Customers Are Saying

  • BBB Rating: Not accredited and has received a significant number of complaints.

Highlights and Challenges

Positives: Many customers appreciate the fit and quality of the clothing relative to its price.

Negatives: Reviewers often express frustration with online customer service, describing it as unhelpful, and the 30-day return policy creates challenges for those who miss the deadline. Shipping and order issues are also common complaints.

Things to Consider Before Buying

  • Pay close attention to your return window when making a purchase and set reminders if there's a chance you'll need to return something.
  • If you encounter problems with online orders, visiting a physical store may provide quicker solutions than relying on online support.

Final Thoughts

Express is a well-established retailer, but to avoid customer frustration, it’s essential to keep track of return deadlines.
